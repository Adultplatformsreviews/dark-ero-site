What Is AISmutWriter?

AISmutWriter is a browser tool built for adult fiction that a mainstream assistant will refuse. You prompt a scene, keep a roleplay going, and, on a paid plan, make pictures that sit next to the text.

The free account does not ask for a card. The dashboard shows the credits you have left and a button to start a story, which is as close as these tools get to a honest first screen.

Paid plans add named chat models. From Casual up, the list is Spark, Ember, Velvet and Pulse. Creator adds Muse. Infinity adds Ember Elite. Image Maker and Image Remixer start at Casual. Video Maker starts at Creator and Infinity.

It is a small independent product, not a lab with a public earnings call. That is fine if you want a short scene. It is the wrong shape of company if you are storing a novel you cannot afford to lose.

What Six Credits Actually Buy

Free users get 6 credits every day. They refresh. They are enough to feel the prose and not enough to finish a serial.

The FAQ says SmutChat needs at least 2 credits before a message will send. It does not say how many messages those 6 credits become, so the free day is a sample, not a budget you can plan.

Casual, on the annual card checked 16 October 2026, moves you from 6 credits to 50 a day and unlocks the image tools. Creator is listed at 220 daily credits. Infinity is listed at 1,500, which is a different product: continuous output, not an evening of scenes.

A credit that resets is useful only if you write that day. Unused daily credits are not a savings account. If you write in bursts, a pack you can spend later is a better shape, and this site does not sell that shape.

Annual Prices, and a Discount That Does Not Match

The pricing page opens on annual billing, so the headline is a per-month figure for a year you pay up front. Monthly billing costs more. Check the monthly toggle before you treat the card as this month's bill.

On 16 October 2026 the annual figures were Casual at $90 a year, about $7.50 a month, while the card itself showed $7. Creator was $250 a year, about $20.83, with the card showing $20. Infinity was $1,500 a year, $125 a month, with Ember Elite and priority support.

The page also labels the annual option minus 29 percent, while each plan card says you save 17 percent. Those are not the same discount. Use the checkout total, not the badge.

The terms say payments are final and non-refundable, including subscriptions and credit purchases. Consumer law where you live may still give you a right the terms do not advertise. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Paid features stay until the period you already bought runs out.

Crypto checkout is offered beside ordinary cards, and the site advertises discreet billing without printing the statement name. Ask support for the descriptor before you rely on it. Credits are non-transferable and may expire under the site's own credit policy.

Privacy, Pictures and the Save Problem

A prompt is data. Read AI adult tools and your data, before you upload before you put a real name, a real address or anyone's private life into a plot. The company can see what you type. Encryption in transit is not the same thing as a draft nobody else can read.

Image tools are the place people get careless. Do not depict minors. An identifiable real person has to be an adult who consented to that depiction. The rest of the content rules still apply, and a paid plan does not delete them.

The reliability score that matters is 5 out of 10. Public notes mention lost stories, bugs and the limits of a small team. We did not reproduce a wipe on a paid account, and we will not invent a lab result. The pattern is common enough that a long project should be copied out of the browser.

Story quality is the other half of the same scorecard, and it is the reason the overall number is not a 5. Short scenes and roleplay are what the tool is good at. Continuity across a long book is what people leave to complain about.

AISmutWriter Scorecard

The prose is the reason to try it. Adult scenes come out without a mainstream refusal, the free day is real, and Casual is cheap enough to test images for a month if you insist on paying annually only after that test.

AISmutWriter Verdict

AISmutWriter is a legitimate uncensored writing tool with a usable free tier and a price table you can almost do arithmetic on. It suits a casual writer who wants a scene, a roleplay or a picture attached to a prompt.
