What Is Chai AI?

Chai AI is a character platform run by Chai Research Corp. You browse personalities other people have published, or you write one and talk to it.

The company presents creator profiles, followers and chat counts alongside the bots.

A published total of characters is a headline. It says nothing about how many of those bots are active, distinct, or any good on the third message.

The test that matters is smaller: one character you like, a scene you can steer, and a cost you can see before the week renews.

The App, Web Pro and the iPhone Gap

There are three front doors, and a purchase on one does not automatically exist on the others. The company site, the browser version of the app, and the separately sold Web Pro product keep their own accounts.

The founder has said as much in public. We did not test a transfer, so assume your chat stays where you paid.

The official Android listing was live when we rechecked it on 9 November 2026, with an update dated 6 November 2026. The store page names Chai Research, and it labels the app as carrying ads and in-app purchases.

The official US iPhone download was unavailable at that same check. A company update says the Apple app is paused while it is being reworked, and that phones which already have it are left alone.

There is no return date in that note. If you need the App Store build, look at your own storefront before you pay on the web.

Pieces and the Free Allowance

Some accounts can start without a card. The Web Pro FAQ says a Google sign-up opens a free start.

The Safety Center says some regions require a paid subscription. A recent founder reply says the company can no longer hand out as many free messages, and it does not print the replacement daily number.

Read the allowance inside the account you actually have.

Pieces are the usage credit the company has been moving toward. A heavier model spends more of them per reply.

People who already had unlimited messages were told they would keep that benefit. A new checkout gets the offer printed on that checkout.

A later explanation describes Diamond as an ad-free subscription with a weekly grant of blue Pieces for text, images and video. It does not publish one grant size or one price for every phone.

We could not open a current account-specific mobile paywall, so the figure on your screen is the only quote.

Before you compare Chai with anything else, write down four numbers from that screen: the subscription price, the Pieces included, the cost of the model you mean to use, and any separate media charge. A plan that removes adverts can still leave you buying more credit on a long evening.

What Web Pro Costs

These figures belong to Web Pro. They are the dollar amounts on the public pricing page when it was rechecked on 9 November 2026.

Pro can start as a 24-hour trial at $0 and then bill $159.99 for the year. The weekly plan is $9.99.

The "about $13.33 a month" line is the annual charge divided by twelve, and the card is billed once.

Fifty-two weekly payments of $9.99 come to $519.48 before tax or add-ons. That arithmetic is why the weekly button is the expensive door, even though the number on it looks small.

Check currency and the renewal line at the checkout you are about to confirm.

MAX sits on top of Pro. MAX Auto is listed at $0.66 per 1,000 generated tokens, and MAX Monthly is listed at $90.

Tokens are slices of generated text, so a chatty night is many of them. Ten thousand tokens at the Auto rate is $6.60 on top of the subscription, which is arithmetic from the published rate.

The Images tab lists $0.12 per image. The web terms list $0.22 per video past the included credits.

They also describe an annual video package that grants 150 generations once, with no refill when the year renews. Confirm that wording before you buy the package for the pictures.

If you wanted pictures and short clips inside one companion plan, the OurDream AI review is the nearer comparison. Chai's media prices are à la carte on top of Pro.

Renewal, Cancellation and Refunds

Web subscriptions and each add-on renew on their own. A trial becomes a paid year unless you cancel before it ends.

Cancellation on the web is in the profile, and access continues until the period you already paid for runs out. The terms describe payments as generally non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.

Cancel where the receipt says you were billed. A Google Play charge is cancelled in that Google account, and uninstalling the app leaves it running.

An Apple charge is cancelled under Subscriptions in the Apple account that paid. Save the confirmation and the end date.
