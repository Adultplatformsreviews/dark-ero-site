What Is SpicyChat AI?

SpicyChat AI is an adult character-chat site run by NextDay AI Incorporated. The site footer names a Montreal address, plus US and Cyprus companies in the same group.

You browse bots other people published, or you write one and talk to it.

Adult roleplay sits behind a mature-content toggle. The footer also carries a 2257 compliance line, which is a legal notice, not a promise that every bot is carefully written.

A homepage figure past 360,000 public characters is the company's own headline. It says nothing about how many of those bots are active, distinct, or any good on the tenth message.

The test that matters is smaller. One character you like, a model that holds the voice, and a context number you can see before the month renews.

What the Plans Cost

These dollar amounts were on SpicyChat's own monthly and annual plan pages when they were rechecked on 26 October 2026, after a rise dated 8 September 2026. Confirm the checkout in front of you, because this category changes prices without much warning.

Free is $0, with up to 4,096 tokens of context and three personas. Get A Taste is $5.95 a month or $60 a year.

True Supporter, which the company marks as the popular plan, is $19.95 a month or $199.95 a year. I'm All In is $29.95 a month or $299.95 a year.

The September rise was not even. The entry plan went from $5 to $5.95, and the two higher plans each went up $5 a month.

People who already had a recurring plan from before 8 September keep the old price until they change it.

An upgrade, a downgrade, or a switch between monthly and annual billing moves that account onto the new prices. An expired plan does not get the old rate back.

Annual billing is the cheaper door if you already know you will stay. True Supporter works out to about $16.66 a month when you pay $199.95 up front, against $19.95 if you let it renew twelve times.

The site's "save 17%" label is a bit generous. The arithmetic is closer to 16%.

Pay the year only after a month on the monthly plan has shown you the writing you actually get. Card subscriptions renew on their own.

Bank and crypto payments, on the company's own notes, are one-time and do not auto-renew. A refund page exists.

We did not re-read its exceptions, so treat a charge as final until you have read that page yourself.

UK prices are set in pounds, not as a live conversion of the dollar figures. On that same check, Get A Taste was £4.95 a month, True Supporter £16.95, and I'm All In £25.95.

Euro prices were described as matching the dollar numbers. Read the currency on your own screen.

Memory, Models and the $5.95 Trap

Context is the number of tokens the model can see at once, and SpicyChat's own notes are unusually plain about the cap. Free and Get A Taste share 4,096 tokens.

True Supporter moves to 8,192. I'm All In can reach 32,768, and only on models that support it.

Reply length is capped too, at about 180 tokens on the two cheaper tiers and 300 on the two higher ones. That is why so many people describe the cheap tiers as short.

The $5.95 plan is a weak buy if memory is the complaint. You pay to drop ads and skip the queue, and you get a Memory Manager plus more personas.

The window that makes a character forget your name stays the same size as free.

SpicyChat's own example is the useful one. After a typical character definition of about 1,000 tokens, roughly twenty recent messages fit in the free window before older lines drop out.

There is no persistent memory of you across separate chats unless a paid tool stores a summary.

Semantic Memory 2.0, on the higher plans, keeps summaries so a few facts can outlive the window. It is not the same thing as a companion that recalls last Tuesday on its own.

Lorebooks, which let you pin world facts outside the chat, are the practical patch for a long story.

The model picker matters as much as the plan. A free-tier base model and a paid model can feel like two products, and the stack changes over time.

Try the same character on two models before you decide the platform is dull. SpicyXL, the company's heavier model, sits on the top tier.

Group chats, a voice creator and a director mode for steering a scene have all shown up in the create menu through 2026. They are useful for people who like to tinker.

They do not cancel the context cap.
