What You Keep After the Plan

Basic keeps about 90 percent of buyer payments and Premium keeps about 100 percent, which is the extra 10 points you are buying. On monthly billing, $100 of sales is the crossover. Weekly Payouts, and Paxum Outside the US

Payouts run weekly, with no minimum and no withdrawal fee. That is better than a 30-day hold. US sellers are paid through Segpay. Sellers outside the US need Paxum, and that account is the complaint that shows up most often: details declined, documents rejected, payouts waiting on a verification the seller thought was finished.

Open and fully verify Paxum before the first sale if you are outside the US. Use clean, full-page documents. A payout problem of that kind is usually a rail problem, not a sign the shop stole the week. It is still your week, stuck.
