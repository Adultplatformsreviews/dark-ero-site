What Is Snifffr?

Snifffr has been a marketplace since 2018 for used panties, worn socks, lingerie, men's underwear, feet pics, private content, customs and chat. Signing up takes about a minute and does not require ID. That is good for a seller who does not want a document on file, and it is how a bad account gets in the door with you.

The company calls itself the largest board of its kind and quotes a member number that does not sit still. The FAQ has said 1.5 million. The homepage has said 2 million, across more than 100 countries. Both are the site's figures. The useful fact is the age of the shop, not the banner.

A widely copied claim says Snifffr takes 20 percent. It does not. The FAQ says it takes no percentage of direct seller-to-buyer payments and earns from membership instead. Correcting that rumor is worth a paragraph. It is not the end of the price.

Free to List, Paid to Answer

Joining, browsing and listing are free. Messaging is not, and the FAQ admits that messaging is where most sales happen. Independent notes put premium around $9 a month, with offers reported as low as $5.95. Budget the message plan from day one. The free tier is a window. You are not allowed to speak through it.

The site's own price talk puts used panties at about $20 to $50, with customs adding $10 to $30 and longer wear priced higher. It also says most new sellers make a first sale in one to two weeks. That is a modest company claim, and it lines up better with public seller notes than the million-member banners do. It is still a claim.

Zero commission does not survive contact with a physical object. A $30 sock sale still buys the socks, the packaging and the postage. Sellers who do the arithmetic often land near $11 left on that $30, which covers the message plan and not much else. A $30 digital sale on a shop that takes 15 percent can leave more, because there is nothing to post. Compare the item, not the percentage in the headline.

Nobody Is Holding the Payment

Managed shops take a cut because they hold the buyer's money and release it when the deal is done. Snifffr skipped that job. Refunds are not guaranteed. A buyer can want the parcel before they pay. A seller can take the money and delete the account. Both show up in public complaints. Time-wasters who want free chat, free photos or a fantasy allowance are part of the same inbox, and you are paying to read them.

Trustpilot showed 2.4 out of 5 from 31 reviews, a Poor label, the weakest public score among the marketplaces in this set. Thirty-one reviews across eight years is a tiny sample, and a company that does not invite reviews collects the angry ones. The limitation is real. So is the direction. Do not average it into a polite 4.

Never ship from home. A post-office box or a mail-receiving service is a cost, on the order of $50 to $150 a year, and it is not optional if you are mailing worn items. One careful sock sale can cover a $9 month. It cannot cover a home address in a stranger's hands. Payment before shipping, on a method you can live with losing once, is the whole safety model. The platform has opted out.

Snifffr FAQ

Does Snifffr take a commission?

No. The FAQ says it takes no percentage of direct seller-to-buyer payments and makes its money from membership. A 20 percent figure circulating in other write-ups does not match that FAQ. Zero still leaves you with postage, packaging and the risk.

Is Snifffr free?

Joining, browsing and listing are free. Messaging, which is where sales happen, needs premium. Outside notes put that plan around $9 a month, sometimes offered near $5.95. Budget it before the first buyer writes.

Does Snifffr hold the money?

No. You arrange payment with the buyer. Refunds are not guaranteed. That is why the commission can be zero. It is also why a ship-first deal is how people get burned.

Do I need ID to join Snifffr?

No. Registration does not require an identity document. That protects your paperwork and lets other people in without paperwork. Do not send ID to a buyer to compensate.

Is the Trustpilot score as bad as it looks?

It was 2.4 out of 5 from 31 reviews, labelled Poor. Across eight years that is a very small sample, skewed to people angry enough to look the site up. Small does not make it good. Read it as a direction, then decide if you can carry the dispute yourself.
