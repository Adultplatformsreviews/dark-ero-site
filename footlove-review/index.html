What Is FootLove?

FootLove, which people also search as Foot Love, is a feet-content marketplace with a website and an Android app. Creators post free and paid media, take private requests and get paid in coins.

The website names the operator as Badhenke AB in Härryda, Sweden, and it publishes terms, a privacy policy, a content-provider agreement, a complaints route and a cancellation policy.

A Play listing captured on 4 August 2026 showed the app under The Feets Company, with in-app purchases, a 4.1-star rating from 90 reviews, over a million downloads claimed, and a last update dated 14 November 2025. The store's 12-plus label is a store classification.

FootLove's own terms require every user to be 18 or older. The terms are the rule that applies to you.

The listing talks about coins and mentions PayPal in the payment flow. A coin balance is not a PayPal balance.

You convert coins, at a rate the terms say can change, after you clear a threshold the account describes rather than a bank transfer you can schedule yourself.

Free to Look, Paid to Talk

Figures below were inside a seller account on 4 August 2026, so treat the checkout as the live price. Basic started from £3.99 a month and Pro from £6.99.

Amounts can shift by region, currency, device or payment provider. The plans recur until you cancel them.

Paying annually lowers the subscription, which is only a saving if the contact limits are enough.

The free tier lets you open a profile, finish onboarding, upload a first free item and a first paid item, and browse parts of the shop. It does not let you message buyers.

Basic advertises access to two buyers. Pro advertises an 85 percent coin share instead of 70 percent, higher feed priority, and two extra buyer contacts a month.

Two contacts is a test, not a business. Work out whether that volume can justify six months or a year before you take the longer bill.

A cheap subscription that cannot introduce you to buyers is a cheap way to maintain a profile nobody can hire.

Coins, a Rough $40 Line, and the Licence

You earn coins from paid content and private requests. The earnings screen says you can convert once you reach 500 coins, shown as roughly $40 depending on country.

The terms say coin values and exchange rates can change. That $40 is a guide from the account, not a peg.

A 70 percent share of coin value is not the same sentence as 70 percent of what the buyer paid in cash. Processing, tax, conversion and refunds can sit on top.

On a simple illustration, $1,000 of eligible sales is about $700 at 70 percent and $850 at 85 percent, before the subscription. FeetFinder's Premium figure of 90 percent is the number people are comparing, and it is a different platform's number.
