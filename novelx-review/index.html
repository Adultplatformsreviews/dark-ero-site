What Is NovelX?

NovelX is a web tool that turns a short idea into chaptered adult fiction. The same prompt box can switch to character chat or an audiobook, and it can make cover art.

Two heat levels let you keep a scene suggestive or push it explicit.

The operator is Sub Zero Labs OÜ, a private limited company in Estonia, register code 17449187, at Sepapaja tn 6 in Tallinn. The name, the code and the street are in the footer and on the terms, the privacy policy and the help pages.

That is a clearer trail than a generator that only lists a support inbox.

Support is email, plus a community Discord linked from the footer. There is no phone line.

If you need a person, write to the published address and keep the reply.

Ten Tokens, Then $18.99

NovelX is free to start with 10 tokens. You do not need a credit card, and you do not need an account for that first sip.

On 20 October 2026 the homepage and four generator pages all showed those 10 tokens and Premium at $18.99 a month on the website, for unlimited generation. A yearly toggle was shown as 43 percent off.

Ten tokens will not carry a book. They will show you whether the voice is one you can stand.

Unlimited generation is the entire case for paying. If you only wanted a scene a week, a pack elsewhere is the more honest shape.

A day pass and other lengths are mentioned in the FAQ and were not on the pricing card we are describing.

Prices move. Confirm the figure at checkout, including anything the App Store adds if you subscribe on an iPhone.

The terms limit commercial use to paid plans. Premium users get a limited licence to publish or sell what they make.

Free users are limited to personal use. If you hoped to sell the story, the free tokens do not get you there.

Two Refund Stories

The refund rules differ between pages, and that is the sort of disagreement that costs people money. The terms say refund requests within 30 days are handled case by case.

The homepage FAQ is stricter. It limits refunds to monthly subscribers with fewer than three generations.

There are no refunds on renewals, day passes, or discounted annual and quarterly plans. iOS purchases go through Apple, so the website's FAQ does not control them.

Ask before the third generation if you are unsure. Three is a small number to discover that the friendlier page was the terms and the stricter page was the one support will quote.

What the Company Says It Keeps

The privacy policy is dated 6 May 2026 and says NovelX does not sell personal information. The steps before you type a real life into a plot are in AI adult tools and your data, before you upload.

A no-sale line is not a promise that prompts stay out of the model.

You can delete the account from the Library page. NovelX says that removes stories, characters, images and audio from active systems, with limited billing and legal records kept.

Deleting the account may not cancel an App Store plan. Cancel that in the Apple account, or the month continues.

The privacy policy says AI and media providers process your prompts and content to run the features. The homepage claims that interactions are encrypted and that the company runs regular security audits.

The policy is more modest, and it mentions encryption in transit where appropriate. We could not verify the audits or the storage setup.

Believe the policy.

The content guidelines are dated 31 March 2025, older than both the terms and the privacy policy. Public stories have been tagged with themes those guidelines forbid.

The rules and the shelf do not always match. That is a moderation gap, not an invitation to go looking for the banned shelf.

Minors are out. Do not test the line.

NovelX FAQ

Is NovelX free?

You get 10 tokens without a credit card and without an account. Unlimited generation is Premium, listed at $18.99 a month on the website when we checked on 20 October 2026.

Who runs NovelX?

Sub Zero Labs OÜ, an Estonian company, register code 17449187. The address on the terms is Sepapaja tn 6 in Tallinn.

Can I sell what NovelX writes?

Only on a paid plan. The terms give premium users a limited commercial licence.

Free use is personal. Ten tokens do not include the right to publish the result for sale.

Will NovelX refund the month?

The terms say requests within 30 days are handled case by case. The FAQ is stricter: monthly subscribers with fewer than three generations, and no refunds on renewals, day passes or discounted long plans.

Does deleting NovelX cancel the subscription?

Deleting from the Library page removes stories, characters, images and audio from active systems, with some billing records kept. An App Store plan has to be cancelled in your Apple account.

Does NovelX allow every adult theme?

The guidelines ban minors and a list of other categories, and they are older than the privacy policy. Public stories have carried tags the rules forbid.
