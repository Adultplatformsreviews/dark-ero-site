How Footly works for creators

The flow is simple on paper: register, choose a plan, verify your identity, then build a profile and upload. Buyers browse a discovery feed, subscribe, buy pay-per-view posts, tip or send custom requests.

Footly states that uploads must be made by real people, and it forbids AI-generated creator material. Its Terms also say uploads are screened before they go live, although we could not watch that process ourselves.

According to SpicyRanked's hands-on test, the plan screen appears immediately after signup and blocks the dashboard, feed, messages and uploads until you pay. Settings such as tax details, message rules and custom request options did stay reachable.

That design is the core trade-off. You get to see the fee structure up front, but you cannot preview the selling tools without spending money first.

Messaging, customs and seller controls

Footly's message settings are one of its quieter strengths. Sellers can decide who may contact them, switch read receipts on or off and ask for a tip before a conversation opens.

Tip-to-chat filters out time-wasters and turns casual messages into small earnings. Automated messages, however, appear limited to the Spotlight and Icon tiers.

Custom requests are switched on by default with a five-day turnaround in settings, based on SpicyRanked's account view. Sellers can also write house rules, such as what they will and will not shoot.

Footly's Terms reportedly allow seven business days for an accepted custom, unless buyer and seller agree on something different. Set your own turnaround clearly so the two deadlines never clash.

Footly pricing and seller plans

Footly offers three creator tiers, and its pricing page lists the same numbers that appear inside the app. The lower your plan, the bigger the slice Footly takes from each sale.

Rising plan

Rising is the entry tier, billed at $3.99 monthly or $24.99 yearly, with a 15% platform fee. Sellers keep 85% of each sale, and Footly pitches it at new creators.

Spotlight plan

Spotlight costs $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year with a 10% fee, so creators keep 90%. SpicyRanked's account view showed extras such as Discovery Boost, auto DMs, mass messaging and scheduled posts on this tier.

Icon plan

Icon costs $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year and charges just 5%, leaving creators 95%. It reportedly adds a profile badge, buyer discoverability and priority placement, which hints that lower tiers may get less exposure.

Footly confirms plan switching is self-service from creator settings with no penalty. That flexibility helps if your sales grow or shrink from month to month.

Do the Footly fees actually add up?

Footly's own example says $1,000 in monthly sales nets $846.01 on Rising, $893.01 on Spotlight and $940.01 on Icon. Those totals match a simple sum of plan cost plus percentage fee.

Run the numbers lower and the picture changes. At $60 in monthly sales, all three monthly plans leave you with exactly $47.01, so that is where the tiers cross over.

Below $60, Rising is cheapest because the fixed plan cost matters more than the cut. Above $60, each step up pays a little more, since every five-point fee drop outweighs the extra $3 in plan price.

Footly's pricing page suggests Icon is best above roughly $300 a month. Our arithmetic points much lower, so we would treat the official guidance as cautious rather than wrong.

The key risk is zero sales. If nobody buys, you still lose the plan fee every month, which is why audience size matters so much here.

Footly payouts: schedule, minimum and methods

Footly says creators are paid weekly once their available balance reaches $10. US sellers receive ACH bank deposits, while international sellers are paid through Paxum.

The pricing page lists processing at one to three business days and claims Footly absorbs payout fees. It also states PayPal is never used, which is common among adult-friendly platforms.

SpicyRanked's reading of Footly's policies notes a seven-day processing hold on fresh earnings. The Seller Agreement also mentions that fees and reserves may apply, so read it before relying on any timeline.

We could not test a real withdrawal. Until more sellers report consistent payments, consider payout speed unverified.

ID verification and account setup

Footly says creator identity and age checks are handled by Ondato, a third-party verification provider. Its pricing page bills this as included on every plan and promises verification in minutes.

SpicyRanked did not reach the Ondato step before the paywall, so the exact order of plan purchase and ID check remains unclear. Expect to need a government ID and a selfie, as is standard, but confirm on the screen itself.

Signup reportedly asks only for basic details and skipped email confirmation in our test. That is quick, though it weakens account recovery if someone mistypes an address.

Footly says verification documents stay with Ondato and are hidden from buyers. Still, keep identifying marks, mail and backgrounds out of your photos.

Privacy and content rights

Privacy is where Footly deserves the closest reading. Under its Terms, creators hand the company a global, royalty-free licence it can sublicense for promotion, which covers ads and social posts.

That licence reportedly continues for material used before you delete your account. If you want tight control over where your images appear, weigh that clause carefully.

The privacy policy says ad click IDs, purchase details and a hashed email are shared with Google for ad measurement. It does not list clear retention periods, which we would like to see added.

To ask for data deletion, email privacy@tryfootly.com, or use support@tryfootly.com as a backup. Keep a copy of any request you send.

Getting started on Footly, step by step

  • Read the pricing page and decide which plan fits your expected monthly sales.

  • Pick a plan, starting with Rising if you are unsure about demand.

Common mistakes to avoid

The biggest mistake is jumping straight to Icon before you have proven demand. The lower fee only pays off once sales are steady.

Another is assuming the marketplace will deliver buyers on its own. On a newer platform, your own promotion will likely do most of the work at first.

Finally, do not ignore the content licence. Upload only images you are comfortable seeing used in promotion, now and potentially after you leave.

Tips if you start selling on Footly

Start on Rising until your monthly sales clearly pass $60. You can upgrade later without penalty, according to Footly.

Remove GPS data before uploading, and keep your face, tattoos and room details out of shot. Small clues add up fast online.

Keep every payment inside the platform, even when a buyer promises a bigger tip elsewhere. Moving off-site removes the protection CCBill and the Terms give you.

Fill in your tax information early, since Footly says sellers handle their own taxes. Keeping simple monthly records will save stress at filing time.
