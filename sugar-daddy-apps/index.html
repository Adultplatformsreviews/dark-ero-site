What a Realistic Online-Only Arrangement Looks Like

Real online arrangements tend to start small and grow slowly as trust builds. Both people agree on clear terms, such as how often you chat and what support is offered.

Sugar Daddy Scams: Red Flags to Watch

Sugar daddy scams follow a few familiar scripts. Most aim at your money, your bank access or your private photos.

  • A "verification fee", "unlock fee" or "loyalty payment" before any money arrives.
  • A check or transfer, followed by a request to buy gift cards or send part of it on.
  • A request for your bank login so he can "deposit your allowance".
  • Pressure to move from the app to WhatsApp, Telegram or text straight away.
  • A huge allowance for a few texts, with no video call and no history.
  • Urgency and guilt trips, such as "do it now or the deal is off".
