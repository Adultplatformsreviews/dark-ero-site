Kalon AI Review 2026: One Character and a Costly Clip

At a glance

Kalon AI is a web companion and NSFW image generator from Kalon LLC in Carson City, Nevada. One character can be used for chat, pictures and short video.

On 4 October 2026 the home page listed a free plan with 50 coins at signup and 50 a day, Premium at $19.97 a month with 16,000 coins, and a year at $119.64 with 200,000 coins.

What Is Kalon AI?

Kalon AI is an adult companion and an image generator in the same browser tab. The FAQ calls it both.

You pick one of the ready-made characters, the home page lists more than 100, or you build one in a five-step wizard: profile, appearance, features, physique and details, realistic or anime.

The privacy policy names Kalon LLC. The complaints policy gives a Carson City, Nevada address.

Disputes in the terms go to individual arbitration rather than a class action or a jury. You can preview the wizard without an account.

Saving a character and generating its portrait needs a free account.

People comparing girlfriends usually start from a ranking of the best AI girlfriend apps. Kalon belongs on that list when the picture has to match the chat.

It is a weaker fit if you wanted a phone app or a novel-length memory.

Dark-Ero files it with the other generators on the AI tools category. Open that page if you are still deciding whether the job is a companion, a still image, or a clip.

Coins, Premium and the Video Math

These figures were on the public home page and the generator on 4 October 2026. Free is 50 coins when you sign up and 50 more each day, with characters, image generation and chat.

Monthly Premium is $19.97, with 16,000 coins, plus video, audio, a smarter chat model and several images in one run. The year is $119.64, shown as $9.97 a month and 200,000 coins, which the page marks as save 50%.

The generator showed the cost before sign-in: 10 coins for one image, 1,000 for a standard video, 500 for an experimental video. Sixteen thousand monthly coins are about 1,600 images or about 16 standard clips.

Heavy video users run out first. Video is listed on the paid plans, not on the free plan.

Coin packs exist, and their prices appear only after you sign in. The terms say coins have no cash value and are lost if the account is closed.

We would not buy a pack until the price and the expiry are both on the screen.

Plans renew until you cancel in settings or by email. Cancelling stops the next charge and leaves Premium in place until the period ends.

Payments are final unless the law says otherwise. EU users can withdraw within 14 days, with a full refund only if they have not used a paid feature.

The terms promise at least 30 days' notice before a subscription price change, with a chance to cancel first.

One inconsistency is worth writing down. The home page and FAQ say 50 free coins at signup.

A comparison page on the same site has said 100. When two of the company's pages disagree, believe the balance inside the account, not the older marketing line.

Characters, Katie and the Generator

The generator has Image, Video and Experimental modes. You describe a scene, attach a character, and pick a style.

Image mode can make up to six pictures in one run on a paid plan. The FAQ says video mode can turn a prompt or an existing image into a short clip of that character.

A public prompt guide covers order, weights and wording. It is a practical start if you have not written an image prompt before.

Chat is supposed to remember the story, and a changelog note says image generation from chat can see recent conversation. A 30 September 2026 entry added a model called Katie and said long chats remember more.

Subscribers get Katie for up to 1,000 messages a day, shared with a model called Natasha. Without a subscription, Katie is a one-time unlock of 1,000 coins, then 1,000 coins for each further pack of 1,000 messages.

That is a second meter, easy to miss if you only looked at the image price.

Audio and a smarter chat model are listed on both paid plans. The girlfriend page also promotes voice with a tone that follows the scene.

We did not place a call for this update, so treat voice as a listed feature, not as a tested one. There is no native app.

The company's own comparison with Candy lists that as one of Kalon's drawbacks.

Candy is the companion people price against this yearly rate. Our Candy.ai review has the $3.99 yearly chat price and the 100-token media cap, which is a different way of charging for pictures.

Rules, Moderation and Age Checks

The age rule is 18, or the local age of majority where that is higher. The age-verification policy says Kalon uses a third party where the law requires a verified age, such as in the UK, and that Kalon receives only a confirmation that the check succeeded.

That is a clearer mechanism than a banner you click through, in the countries where it actually runs.

The guidelines ban minors and youth-coded content, real people and public figures, and non-consensual scenarios. An automated system called Aether checks prompts, images, videos, chat and character bios.

The complaints policy promises to look at reports of unlawful or non-consensual content within five business days. You can appeal and ask for a person to review a moderation decision.

Explicit fictional adults are allowed. "Uncensored" in the marketing is not a blank cheque, and the banned list is specific.

If a prompt is refused, read the guideline before you rephrase your way around a safety line.

Privacy and Training

The FAQ says characters, chats and creations are private by default and visible only to you unless you share them to a community feed. The privacy policy says private chats and content are not used to train third-party or general-purpose models.

The terms still say your inputs and outputs may be used to train Kalon's own models. Both sentences can be true.

The second one is the one that matters if you wanted a sealed diary.

The privacy policy also warns that chats sent through the service may not be secure. The home page promotes encrypted chat.

Treat the homepage line as marketing and the policy as the document. We found no published name for how a card charge appears on a statement, so check the checkout if the descriptor matters.

Read AI adult tools and your data, before you upload before you put a real name, a face or a workplace into a prompt. A picture of someone you know is a worse idea than a fictional face.

The steps for leaving a companion account are in the guide to how to delete AI companion data. Cancel the plan before you wait on a deletion email, or the renewal can land while you are still "closing" the account.

Kalon Scorecard

The product is coherent. One character, one coin balance, a generator that tells you the cost, and a free daily grant you can spend on stills.

The yearly price is half the monthly rate. The rules and the complaints path are written in public, which is rarer than it should be.

Video is the tax. Sixteen standard clips a month on the monthly plan is a small habit, pack prices are behind the login, and there is no app.

Training on your own prompts is allowed even though third-party training is ruled out.

Put together, that is 7.5 out of 10. Start on the free coins and make stills until you know the look.

Move to the year only if you are actually opening it, and price a video habit before you assume 16,000 coins is a lot.

Kalon Verdict

Kalon AI is the companion we would try when the picture has to be the same person as the chat. The free plan is real, the generator shows its price, and the company is named.

It is a browser product, a clip is a thousand coins, and the terms let the company train on what you type.

Our score is 7.5 out of 10. Use the daily coins first.

Pay for the year if the stills are the thing you came for, and keep faces of real people out of the prompt. The guidelines already forbid them.
