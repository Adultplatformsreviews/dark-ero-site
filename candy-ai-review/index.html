At a glance

Candy.ai is a browser companion from EverAI Limited, a Malta company, with chat, images, voice, short video and a character gallery. There is no native app.

What Is Candy.ai?

Candy.ai is an adult companion you use in the browser. You pick a character from the gallery or build one, then chat, and you can unlock photos, voice notes and short video.

Explicit scenes are part of the product, not an accident.

Premium Prices and the Token Cap

Chat is thin without a plan, so most people end up on Premium. These are the three prices on the public subscriptions page on 4 October 2026, in US dollars.

The 12-month plan showed $3.99 a month, marked as 70% off $13.99. The 3-month plan showed $8.99 a month.

The monthly plan showed $9.99 for the first month, then $13.99.

Every one of those plans included unlimited text and 100 tokens a month. The yearly subscriber pays once and still receives 100 tokens each month, not 100 times twelve on day one.

Subscriptions renew until you cancel in settings.

The free start, per the help center, is 5 messages across every character, one custom character and one image. That is enough to see the interface.

It is not enough to judge a character you might talk to for a month.

Token pack prices appear only after you log in, and there is no full public menu of what each action costs. Public character pages have shown voice calls at 3 tokens a minute, marked as a beta price.

Check the costs inside the account before you buy a pack.

A help article also describes Premium Plus, with 330 tokens a month, 20 memory slots and early access to new characters. Its price was not on the public subscriptions page when we looked.

Images, Voice, Video and the Extra Tiers

Tokens pay for images, extra characters, voice messages, voice calls and private content packs. You can also turn a generated image into a short clip, and a scene builder lets you assemble a clip from presets.

Text on Premium does not spend the allowance, which is why light users can live inside the 100.

Some characters send photos, voice notes and video on their own, in a mode the help center says you can switch off in the chat. Turn that off if you are watching the token balance.

A proactive clip is still your token.

Live Action is interactive video with selected characters on a phone, and some actions cost tokens. Candy Shorts are scripted clips under two minutes that you watch rather than steer.

Refunds and Cancellation

Subscription refunds must be requested within 24 hours of payment, and only if you have used 20 tokens or fewer. Token packs use the same 24-hour window, and they are gone once any token is spent.

Only card payments are described as eligible. EU and UK buyers can also withdraw within 14 days of the first purchase, minus the share of the service already used.

Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps Premium until the period you paid for ends. The terms say unused tokens then expire.

A help article says they stay on the account. Those two sentences cannot both be the rule you rely on.

Spend the tokens before the period ends, and keep a copy of whichever page you were shown.

Privacy and the EverAI Charge

Charges appear as EverAI on the statement, not as the product name. That keeps the app's name off the line.

It does not hide the purchase from you, and it does not make the chat private.

The privacy notice says Candy.ai uses data to train its models, and that preparing that data can include a person reading de-identified chats. The terms also give EverAI a broad, permanent licence over what you type.

Account data can be kept for up to three years after your last activity unless you ask for deletion. Deletion sits in a Danger Zone in settings, and the company says that step also cancels a subscription.

The notice says EverAI asks for an opt-in before it shares personal data with outside companies for their marketing, and you can write to the data protection officer about access and deletion.
