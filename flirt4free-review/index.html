What Is Flirt4Free?

Flirt4Free is a credit cam site. Models stream in free rooms, and members spend credits on private, group and special shows.

One check of the home page showed 271 models online. That count moves through the day, so treat it as a snapshot of a busy grid, not a census.

You filter by category, room type and language, then join a free room to chat. When a paid show starts, credits come off one at a time, and you can leave and keep what is left.

That per-credit drip is easier to stop than a show that bills a block you cannot see.

Show Types and the Rate Table

Standard is a one-to-one private at 30 to 60 credits a minute. Premiere Chat is the same idea with top-rated models and the site's best video, at 70 to 500 credits a minute.

Voyeur Mode, watching someone else's private with no audio and no chat, is 10 to 40. Multi User runs 10 to 120, and in Combo rooms that shared show is priced at two thirds of the private rate.

Group Chat works toward a pledge the model sets. Party Chat runs on tips.

Offered Shows are a price you agree with the model. Feature Shows were listed at 30 credits a minute, and free for VIP.

Not every model offers every type, so a rate on the table can be absent in the room you opened.

Cam2Cam lets the model see you, and there is an audio-only version. Interactive toys, including Lovense, have their own FAQ section.

Videos on demand, Fan Clubs and a points programme called Flirt Rewards sit beside the live shows. The menu is wide.

The missing line is still what one credit costs.

VIP, Expiry and the Hidden Pack

VIP at $39.95 brings 150 recurring credits a month, 100 bonus credits for direct messages that do not roll over, 200 free videos a day, free hour-long Feature Shows, recorded shows and 5 percent off video purchases. That is a real bundle if you use the site every month.

It is an automatic renewal if you joined to try one show.

The terms say unused credits may expire after six months, and they are lost if you delete the account. Subscriptions renew until you cancel before the period ends.

Refunds are at the company's discretion, and billing disputes have to be sent in writing. Older reviews quoted pack prices such as 45 credits for $5.

Those figures are not on the public pages, so they are not in this one.

The support page says charges appear under VS Media billing names, or under a SegPay name for some EU payments, with a phone number beside the descriptor. The brand name stays off the statement.

That is useful, and it does not tell you the price you are about to approve.

Safety, Support and Privacy

The terms limit the site to adults who are 18 or older and who have reached the age of majority where they live. They ban content involving minors, prostitution and sex trafficking.

An 18+ warning shows before entry. Bondage and similar fetish labels appear in the terms, so read the room description before you join a show you did not mean to buy.

Support is listed as 24-hour email, a U.S. toll-free number, an international number and live support. That is more than most cam sites put on a page.

The privacy policy, updated 26 March 2025, lets you ask to see, correct or delete data by email, and it does not give fixed retention periods. EU users get the GDPR set of rights.

Nevada residents can opt out of data sales.

Flirt4Free Verdict

Flirt4Free is a long-running cam site with a real rate table, a lot of show types, named companies on two continents and support you can call. The 120 credits are enough to learn one model's pace without a pack.
