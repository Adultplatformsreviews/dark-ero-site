What Affiny AI Is

Affiny AI, also written Affiny, is a web companion for fictional adult chat. The plans guide describes a free coin wallet and two subscriptions, Companion and Intimate.

Both paid plans unlock the same tools. They differ in monthly coins and in how many companions you can create.

The help pages price chat, calls, rooms, stills, video and memory edits. The Terms, marked updated in November 2026, never name a company, a registration number or a postal address.

The entity in that document is only Affiny.

Cancel and refunds

You cancel from My Wallet or the pricing page, in a hosted checkout, not in an app store. Affiny bills on the web.

Cancel keeps the account and rolled-over coins until the period ends, then free rates apply. The plans FAQ and the Terms say refunds are not offered.

Spent coins, including extra companion slots, stay spent. A failed image or video returns its coins.

The 23 November account check reported a Stripe billing portal, and a help article says the statement typically shows Stripe. We did not pay, so the live statement line is unverified.
