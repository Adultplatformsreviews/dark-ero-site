What tokens do, and what they do not

Tokens are personal and non-transferable. They cannot be exchanged for cash, and they are not a substitute for payment.

They expire at the end of the billing period. They do not roll over.

When a paid plan ends, unused tokens expire and cannot be restored. A refund, if one is granted, cancels the paid service immediately and revokes the tokens that remain.

That is a hard shape. A yearly bill of $46.79 does not build a token balance you can sit on.

Cancel and refunds

You cancel in account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, and the account then returns to the free version.

Premium features stop at that point. The terms do not describe an app-store subscription, so we are not describing an App Store or Google Play cancel path.

Refund requests for a subscription must be submitted within 24 hours of payment. No refund is granted if more than 20 tokens have been used.

Only credit-card payments are eligible under that clause. Token-pack refunds, if you buy a pack, also have to be requested within 24 hours, and they are refused once any of those tokens are used.

The terms say extra statutory rights may apply in the EU or the UK. They also say repeated refunds, extra accounts used to get refunds, or a chargeback can end the account.

You are told to contact the company before a chargeback. A chargeback can suspend access and wipe remaining tokens.

Legal mail is legal@coquette.ai. General contact on the pages we opened is contact@coquette.ai.

Those addresses are text, not a promise that a reply arrives inside the 24-hour refund window. Write before you spend the tokens if you might want the money back.

Privacy Rules

The privacy policy names Euranka Sarl as the controller for personal data on the service. It cites the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and the GDPR for users in the EEA.

The terms, updated later, define "Company" as Coquette.ai. They put disputes under Swiss law and the courts of Lausanne, and they do not print a street address or a company number.

A controller name in one document and a product name in the other is a real gap. We did not match a registration number on the Coquette pages we opened, so this review does not quote one.

The privacy policy says the service does not sell or rent personal data. It may share data with providers for hosting, analytics, and payments, with authorities when the law requires it, and with a buyer if the business is sold.

Account data is kept while the account is active. Payment and transaction records can be kept for up to 7 years.

Conversations and AI interactions are stored for a limited period to improve the service, and then anonymized. The policy does not define that period in days.

Data collected includes name, email, username, and an encrypted password, plus chats, preferences, billing status, IP address, browser, device, and messages you send to support. Card numbers are described as handled by payment providers, not as a number the policy says Coquette stores itself.
