What Is Fanvue?

Fanvue is a London-registered subscription platform where creators post photos, video and updates for paying fans. It earns from monthly subscriptions, paywalled posts, pay-per-view messages, tips, bundles and checkout links.

It is not adult-only. Fitness coaches, travel and lifestyle creators and AI personas sit next to explicit creators.

That mix gives it a slightly more mainstream feel than Fansly.

The model will feel familiar if you have used OnlyFans. The 2026 difference is how hard Fanvue leans into AI, both as a creator tool and as a category of creator.

How Fanvue Works

Creators sign up, pass an ID check and build a profile. Fans can follow for free if the creator allows it, or subscribe for locked content.

Single posts, messages and bundles can be sold on top.

Checkout links let you sell a specific item without forcing a full subscription, which is handy for one-off custom sets or a feet photo pack promoted on social media.

The AI Toolkit

Fanvue ships AI Messaging for automated replies and mass messages, AI Voice Notes and AI Voice Calls, plus AI-driven analytics and earnings insights.

Automations can fire on events such as a new follower, a renewal or a fan's first message. You choose where AI helps and where you reply personally.

For a creator with hundreds of fans, that can save hours each week.

The tools do not create an audience. They help you work the fans you already bring, so judge them on the time they save, not on promised earnings.

AI and Virtual Creators

Fanvue openly accepts AI influencers and virtual models as long as they are disclosed as AI. That is a real difference from Fansly, which bans photorealistic AI content.

Fanvue Fees, Prices and Payouts

Joining is free and there is no monthly creator plan. Fanvue takes its share from what fans pay you.

  • Standard fee: 20%, so you keep 80%.
  • Intro promo: 15% for the first 30 days after your ID check, then back to 20%.
  • Subscription price range: $3.99 to $100 a month.
  • Paywalled posts: $3 to $500 each.
  • Minimum payout: $20, higher in some regions.
  • Crypto via TripleA: 1% fee, the only payout route Fanvue charges for.

The important point is that the 15% rate is temporary. After a month, the fee matches OnlyFans and Fansly, so switching platforms for that number alone makes little sense.

How Money Reaches You

New earnings sit in a pending state, normally for 7 days and sometimes up to 28. Once available and above $20, you request a payout yourself; it usually arrives within a few business days.

Supported routes include worldwide bank transfer, crypto through TripleA, MassPay eWallet and Cosmo. PayPal is not offered.

Earnings are paid gross, so tax is your responsibility.

Leave eligible money untouched for 12 months and it can be marked dormant, so withdraw regularly. For realistic expectations on income, see this independent look at how much subscription creators really make.

Fanvue NSFW and Content Rules

Explicit content is allowed when it is labelled, and every creator and fan must be 18 or older. Anything that is, or appears to be, underage is banned outright.

Real creators complete ID verification. AI accounts must disclose that they are AI and still follow every other rule.

Is Fanvue Safe and Legit?

Fanvue is a real, registered UK company with a published earnings policy, a working help centre and named payout partners. It is legit, not a scam.

Creators pass an ID check, two-factor login is available and UK data protection law applies. Fans verify a payment method rather than a photo ID, which is lighter than a buyer-verified marketplace.

Treat Fanvue's homepage figures on total payouts and creator numbers as company claims. They are not independently audited.

Cancelling as a Fan

Fans cancel from account settings under subscriptions by turning off auto-renew. Access continues until the paid period ends, and free trials should be cancelled before they convert.

How to Get Fans on Fanvue

Fanvue converts traffic; it does not create it. Treat it as the checkout at the end of your own marketing.

  • Post on a schedule. An active feed signals that a subscription is worth the money.
  • Share one link everywhere. Put your Fanvue link on X, Reddit, TikTok and Instagram bios where your audience already spends time.
  • Use free previews. Let followers sample your style, then upsell pay-per-view posts and bundles.
  • Answer quickly. Fast replies keep fans subscribed, and the AI tools can cover the routine messages.
  • Run short offers. Limited discounts bring lapsed subscribers back without permanently lowering your price.

Feet creators should build a small portfolio of consistent, well-lit shots before launching, because buyers judge quality within seconds. Price custom requests higher than standard sets and never share your face, location or other identifying details unless you have decided to.

Who Should Use Fanvue

Fanvue suits creators who already have followers and want AI help to handle messages at scale, as well as AI or virtual creators who are not welcome on Fansly. It also suits anyone who wants a second checkout in case a main platform has an outage or a policy change.

Skip it as your only platform if you have no outside audience. In that case, start with a beginner-friendly option or a marketplace where buyers search for content, then add Fanvue later.

Fanvue Verdict

Fanvue is a credible UK platform with a fair split, quick-to-learn tools and the strongest AI feature set among mainstream fan sites. AI creators in particular have few better options.

Its weaknesses are audience size and a headline fee that does not last. Add it if you have fans to send and want AI help; do not uproot an existing OnlyFans base to chase a 30-day discount.
