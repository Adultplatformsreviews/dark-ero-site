How OurDream AI Works

After an age gate, the interface shows Create, Explore, Chat, Generate, My AI, Feed and Community. Upgrade and Buy Coins buttons sit beside account controls.

Subscriber chat costs no DreamCoins, while free users pay one coin per message. Creating a new chatbot and some premium reply modes cost a few coins extra.

Images, Video and Voice

The Generate tab handles images and short clips for subscribers, paid from the monthly coin pool. Voice messages and calls also draw coins.

OurDream AI Pricing

  • Free: $0, chat at 1 DreamCoin per message, no images or video, coins mainly via referrals.
  • Monthly Premium: $19.99 a month with 1,000 DreamCoins each month.
  • Annual Premium: $119.88 a year, about $9.99 a month, with a signup coin bonus and 1,000 coins monthly.

Some promotional checkout tiles did not match the documented standard plans. Always confirm the final price on the checkout screen.

Heavy image and video users can burn through 1,000 coins quickly, and top-up packs cost extra. Light chat users get the most value from the annual plan.

Cancellation and Refunds

You can cancel and keep access until the paid period ends. Refunds are refused once more than 25% of a product has been used.

After you unsubscribe, past media is blurred and old coins are not restored. Download anything you want to keep before cancelling.
