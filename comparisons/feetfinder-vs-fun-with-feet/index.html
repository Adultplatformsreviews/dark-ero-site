FeetFinder and Fun With Feet are both feet-only marketplaces. FeetFinder sells a plan and keeps a share of each sale.

Fun With Feet sells a flat membership, and its pages disagree on whether it takes a cut.

If you want published plan prices and a $30 payout minimum, FeetFinder is the pick. If Fun With Feet confirms in writing that your account pays no commission, and you bring your own buyers, its membership can leave you more.

More than two sites are compared in FeetFinder vs alternatives, which also covers OnlyFeet, Snifffr, OnlyFans and Fansly.

Key takeaways

  • FeetFinder says sellers keep 85% on Basic and 90% on Premium, and a payout can be requested from $30.

Plans and the commission question

FeetFinder Basic and Premium

FeetFinder sells Basic and Premium monthly, yearly or for a lifetime, at the prices in the table. Yearly plans are paid up front.

Lifetime plans are paid once.

Sellers keep 85% on Basic and 90% on Premium. Plans are charged to a card on file in advance of each term, not taken out of sales.

The Fun With Feet membership

Fun With Feet lists a listing fee of $9.99 for 1 month or $14.99 for 3 months. Buyers join free and pay small transaction fees on top of your price.

A recurring membership renews by itself. One that does not recur has to be bought again.

You can cancel from the Billing page.

Which commission is real

On 10 October 2026 the English Seller FAQ said Fun With Feet takes no commission and that you keep 100% of what you earn. The English sell page and the Spanish Seller FAQ say the same.

The German, French and Italian versions of that Seller FAQ say it takes 15%. The Terms say the company earns from seller memberships and a small commission when buyers top up a wallet, and that it may change compensation rates.

That is inconsistent published information. This page shows both outcomes.

Ask support in writing which rate applies to your account, and keep the reply.

$30 on FeetFinder, $50 on Fun With Feet

FeetFinder sellers can request a payout from $30. There is no daily or weekly cap, and limits depend on the account.

Each request needs approval, and methods depend on the country.

Fun With Feet sets a $50 minimum. Its Seller FAQ says payouts are normally processed within 24 to 48 hours.

A transaction flagged as suspicious may be held for up to 30 days by the payment company. It lists no payout methods on public pages and says withdrawals arrive in local currency.

Say the first month brings $40 of gross sales. On Basic that leaves $34 after the 15% fee, enough to request a payout once it is available.

The request still needs approval. On Fun With Feet the same sales leave $40 at 0% or $34 at 15%, both under $50, so you could not withdraw yet.

Language, buyers and who runs the sites

Read FeetFinder taxes if you are in the US.

Can you run both

Yes, through permitted channels. FeetFinder's Seller Agreement bans phone numbers, email addresses and URLs in your content, and sellers may not take buyer payments outside its billing.

Fun With Feet's General FAQ bans promoting other websites, and its Buyer FAQ treats dealing away from the site as a breach. Promote each profile from your own social accounts.

If you are also weighing a plan-plus-cut site, see FeetFinder vs OnlyFeet. For a site with no plan and a cut of each sale, read the OnlyFans review.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fun With Feet cheaper than FeetFinder?

It depends on the commission and on your sales. With the same sales and no commission, the 3 month membership has a lower modelled cost than yearly Basic above about $25 of average monthly sales.

At 15%, it never beats yearly Basic.

Does Fun With Feet take a commission?

Its published information disagrees. On 10 October 2026 the English and Spanish Seller FAQ said it takes none and you keep 100%.

The German, French and Italian versions said 15%. Ask support in writing before you buy a membership.

Which suits someone who only sells now and then?

If you stay listed all year, yearly Basic at $14.99 up front is the lowest fixed cost on either site. One Fun With Feet 3 month membership can leave more if sales fall in one short stretch, but only at 0% and only if no renewal is needed to withdraw.

Can you get the membership back if you make no sales?

Fun With Feet's Refund Policy, effective 5 March 2026, rules out refunds when earnings are lower than expected or after a billing cycle has been charged. FeetFinder charges each plan in advance whether or not you sell.

Treat either fee as a cost you may not earn back.
