Where FeetFinder stands

FeetFinder is a feet-only marketplace run by FLRT Inc. of Nevada. Buyers join free and need a card on file to buy.

Sellers pay for a plan and pass an ID check before they can sell. The full write-up is the FeetFinder review.

Sellers can request a payout once the available balance reaches $30. There is no daily or weekly payout limit, and limits depend on the individual account.

When the monthly plan beats a 20% cut

FeetFinder charges a plan plus a smaller cut. OnlyFans and Fansly charge no plan and a bigger cut.

Every threshold below assumes the same sales on both sites.

If you already bought an annual or lifetime plan, that money is spent either way. Compare only the fees you would still pay from here.

Basic against a 20% platform

On Basic you keep 85 cents of each dollar. On a 20% platform with no subscription you keep 80 cents.

The 5 cent gap has to cover the plan.

Monthly Basic is $4.99. Divided by 0.05, that is $99.80, so FeetFinder leaves more from about $100 of sales a month.

Annual Basic is about $1.25 a month, which breaks even near $25 of sales a month.

Premium against Basic and against 20%

Premium keeps 90 cents, a 10 cent edge over a 20% platform. Monthly, $14.99 divided by 0.10 is $149.90.

Annual, $49.99 divided by 12 and then by 0.10 is about $42.

Premium keeps 5 cents more per dollar than Basic and costs $10 more a month, so the monthly upgrade pays off above $200 of sales. On annual plans the extra $35, spread over a year, pays off near $58 of sales a month.

Fun With Feet at 0% and at 15%

On the one-month plan at 0%, you keep 15 cents more per dollar than Basic and pay $5 more. That gap closes near $33 of sales a month.

At 15%, the one-month plan leaves exactly $5 a month less than monthly Basic.

The three-month plan is about $5 a month. At 0% it beats monthly Basic at almost any sales level, and it beats annual Basic above about $25.

At 15% it is a near tie with monthly Basic.

OnlyFeet Pro

OnlyFeet Basic takes the same 20% as OnlyFans plus a plan fee, so it leaves less than a 20% site with no subscription.

Who should stay, and who should add a site

These are fit calls, not guarantees. Nobody can promise sales will match after a move.

Stay on FeetFinder if

  • You have no audience of your own. Built-in buyer traffic is the main thing the plan buys.
  • You sell digital photos and videos, which is what its buyers come for.
  • The alternative is a 20% site with no subscription, and monthly sales would pass about $100 on monthly Basic, or about $25 on annual Basic, if sales stay the same.
  • You want a checked marketplace. Sellers pass an ID check, and buyers need a card on file.

Add a second platform if

  • You have a social following. Add OnlyFans or Fansly, with no plan fee and recurring subscriptions.
  • You sell worn items. Add Snifffr, and price in postage, packaging and the item.
  • You drive your own traffic and want a flat fee. Fun With Feet can be a second shop. Run the numbers at both 0% and 15%.
  • You want OnlyFeet Pro for a 0% fee. Check the checkout price against the break-even figures, then read the seller reports.

Most people who switch really add a platform. They keep FeetFinder as the shop window and a subscription page for regulars.

How to leave without breaking FeetFinder rules

A published reading of the Seller Agreement found the trap most switching notes miss. Profile and chat content must not contain URLs, email addresses or other contact details, and payments must go through FeetFinder's billing.

Messaging buyers a link to a new page breaks those rules and puts pending earnings at risk. A safer order is below.

  1. Keep copies of your own originals before you change anything.
  2. Turn off renewal if you are leaving. Plans are charged in advance. You do not need to wait for a payout to cancel renewal.
  3. Do not delete the account yet. Cancelling renewal and deleting the account are separate steps. Keep it until earnings have been reviewed and paid.
  4. Request what you can. A payout needs a $30 balance. Below that there is nothing to withdraw.
  5. Promote the new page from your own social accounts, not from FeetFinder chats.

Frequently asked questions

Should I stay on FeetFinder for a few more months?

Often yes, if the plan you would still pay is small. Annual Basic costs $14.99 a year.

Compare the fees from now on with the sales you can realistically expect elsewhere.

Which FeetFinder alternative has no monthly seller fee?

OnlyFans and Fansly charge no seller plan and take 20% of each payment. Snifffr is free to join and list, but messaging and cashing out tokens need paid premium.

Fun With Feet and OnlyFeet both charge a seller plan.

Is Fun With Feet really commission free?

Its English seller help says it takes no commission. Its German seller help says 15%.

Both pages were live on 9 October 2026. Work out costs at both rates, and check the rate in your dashboard before you sell.

Can I sell on FeetFinder and another site at the same time?

Yes. Keep each sale on the platform where the buyer found you, and use each site's own billing.

FeetFinder does not allow payments through outside apps.

Can I message FeetFinder buyers to follow me somewhere else?

Not through FeetFinder. Its Seller Agreement bans URLs, email addresses and other contact details in profile and chat content.

Promote the new page on your own social accounts.

Is OnlyFans or FeetFinder better for feet content?

FeetFinder suits sellers who need buyers to find them, because the audience is there for feet content. OnlyFans suits sellers who already have followers, because it charges no plan and pays recurring income.
