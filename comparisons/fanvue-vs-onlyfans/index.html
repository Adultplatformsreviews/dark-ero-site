Creator cut

Both platforms keep 20 percent of qualifying fan payments and send 80 percent to the creator. On Fanvue that split covers subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view, bundles and custom content.

On OnlyFans it covers subscriptions, paid messages and tips. Refunds and chargebacks are clawed back from the creator's share.

Taxes and card fees added at checkout sit outside Fanvue's 80 percent. A creator charging $9.99 with 100 subscribers has about $999 of subscription sales before those extras, and keeps about $799 before their own tax.

Fanvue's advertised 15 percent is not the standard rate. Our review says it applies for the first 30 days after ID approval.

The explainer adds that an older 85 percent rate for people who joined in 2022 has ended. Read the live earnings page if you are still inside a promo window.

A dispute rate above 1.5 percent in 30 days can cost $50 for each extra dispute on Fanvue. That fee is separate from the 20 percent.

Fansly's terms also list an 80 percent creator share, so a third shop sits on the same headline cut. The Dark-Ero file is the Fansly review, and the outside file is the Fansly review.

Subscription price in 2026

There is no single Fanvue OnlyFans subscription price in 2026. Each creator sets a number inside a cap the platform publishes.

Fanvue's cap is $3.99 to $100 a month, billed every 30 days. OnlyFans is free, or $4.99 to $50 a month, and bundles can run up to $250.

The subscription is often only the entry fee. Our OnlyFans file uses the example of a $9.99 page plus two $15 paid messages, which is $39.99 before tax.

Fanvue paywalled posts run $3 to $500, and paid messages and tips sit on top of the subscription. OnlyFans paid messages and tips run $3 to $100, with tips up to $200 later.

VAT, local tax and card fees can be added on Fanvue, and the breakdown is supposed to show before you pay. A free trial becomes a paid subscription unless the fan cancels.

Fan payment methods on Fanvue, from the explainer, are cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, crypto through the Fanvue wallet, and PIX in Brazil. PayPal, Discover, Cash App and bank transfer are not the fan payment methods there.

SpicyRanked's OnlyFans review is the outside pass on that side of the price. Confirm the creator's own button, because the cap is not the price.

AI and virtual creators

This is the split that is not a fee. Fanvue treats AI creators as a real category, and every one of them goes through the same identity checks as a human creator.

Fully AI accounts, AI personas based on a real person, and humans who sometimes use AI all have to show the AI label. A persona based on a real person also needs that person's verification and consent.

If a fan asks an AI account whether it is real, the creator has to say it is AI. Characters have to look clearly adult.

One verified identity can hold up to 15 AI creator accounts and 2 human ones, after at least one successful withdrawal and with no active compliance warning. The platform is not adult-only, so fitness and lifestyle accounts sit next to explicit ones and next to AI personas.

The company says most creators use at least one in-house tool, from message rewrites to a voice you set up. The product list in our review is AI messaging, AI voice notes, AI voice calls, and automations on events such as a new follower or a first message.

OnlyFans allows AI content only if it clearly features the verified creator and is labeled, for example with #ai. There is no public API, and plugging AI chatbots into the platform is not allowed.

A virtual creator who is not the verified person does not fit that OnlyFans rule. That is the practical reason someone running a disclosed AI account looks at Fanvue.

Fansly, for a third data point in our Fanvue review, bans photorealistic AI content. Three shops on an 80 percent split are still three different AI policies.

Payouts

Both floors sit at about $20. Fanvue's can vary by region, and OnlyFans states $20 on most methods.

Fanvue earnings usually move from pending to available after 7 days, and the policy allows holds up to 28 days. A help-center note mentions 30 days when a fan has not verified an email.

OnlyFans holds earnings 7 days, or 21 days in some high-risk countries. The creator still has to pass ID checks, including repeat selfie checks about every 45 days.

Fanvue asks for at least five pieces of content before a withdrawal, and the payout method has to match the name on the ID. A new payout method can take up to 24 hours to clear a security check.

The Fanvue help center estimates 3 to 5 business days to send a payout, and the policy aims to start one within 10 business days. You request it yourself once the money is available.

Withdrawals through TripleA carry a 1 percent fee, which Fanvue describes as the only payout provider it charges for. PayPal is not a Fanvue payout method.

Leave eligible Fanvue money untouched for 12 months and it can be marked dormant. Withdraw on a schedule.

What a typical creator clears on the bigger brand is a different essay, our how much OnlyFans creators make note. Fanvue has not handed the public a matching average, and homepage totals should be read as company claims.

Discovery

Neither shop is a traffic source you can budget like an ad. OnlyFans gives new creators very little internal discovery, which is why the review says to plan traffic before you plan a price.

Fanvue's own discovery is mostly a shuffle, with newer and faster-growing accounts nearer the top. Bring an audience if you want sales in week one.

The explainer quotes Fanvue's own creator guide on the mix of early money. In the first two months, the subscription was about 10 percent of new creators' earnings, and paid messages were about 60 percent.

That mix is a reason not to judge either platform by the subscription cap alone. A low monthly price with no messages can lose to a higher cap that fans never reach.

Content rules and safety

Both require creators to be 18, or older where local law says so, and both require identity checks that are not shown on the public profile. Anyone else in the content needs their own check and signed consent before the upload.

A stage name is allowed on Fanvue, and a face is optional. OnlyFans still wants the ID, and it still wants the repeat selfie.

Fanvue charges are described as the brand domain with no creator name, or as FANVUE or SHIFT HOLDINGS LTD, depending on the method and the country. OnlyFans statements show OnlyFans or OF, so billing is not discreet.

Anyone who can see the statement can recognise either charge. A famous name does not hide the line.

Fanvue payments are generally non-refundable once the content is delivered. The terms allow 90 days for a billing query, a help page still says 30 days, and a chargeback raised before you write to support can end the account.

OnlyFans gives fans almost no refund rights. Turn off auto-renew on a trial before it converts, on either shop.

The safety habits that survive a change of logo are in is OnlyFans safe. A registered company is not the same thing as an invisible statement.

Company scale claims need the same caution. Fanvue says it has more than 325,000 creators and over 17 million monthly active users, and our review says to treat homepage figures as unaudited company claims.

Who should pick which

Choose Fanvue if you:

  • Want AI messaging, voice notes or voice calls inside the same shop
  • Run a disclosed AI or virtual creator, with the real person's consent when the persona is based on one
  • Can live with a $3.99 to $100 subscription cap and a 20 percent standard fee
  • Will bring your own fans, or you are using the first-30-day promo with your eyes open
  • Want a London company name on the paperwork and can accept SHIFT HOLDINGS LTD on a statement

Choose OnlyFans if you:

  • Already have an audience that knows the bigger brand
  • Want the wider free-or-$4.99-to-$50 subscription range, plus bundles up to $250
  • Are the verified person in the content, including any AI edit that is labeled as you
  • Do not need a chatbot plugged into the platform
  • Can accept OnlyFans or OF on the card, and a 7-day or 21-day hold

Choose neither as a first audience. If the product is one narrow fetish, a niche shop can beat a general feed, which is the point of sites like OnlyFans.
