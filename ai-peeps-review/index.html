What Is AI Peeps?

AI Peeps is an adult character studio where the bots are called Peeps. You chat, you build your own, and you spend PeepCoins on images, video and voice.

It says it launched in August 2024 and that one person still builds it.

The home page claims more than 100,000 Peeps and a fresh list every Monday. We did not count the library, so the figure stays a claim.

Ordinary companionship and adult roleplay are both on the menu, with an 18+ gate in front of the adult pages.

The terms name the operator as AI Peeps Lab di Biscione Valerio, a sole trader in Brescia, Italy, with a street address and an Italian VAT number repeated in the privacy policy and the footer. We did not check that VAT number against a register.

The wiki credits support to a solo creator called Val.

The partners page calls it a character studio, with four image engines, three video models and voice cloning. The wiki agrees on the four engines, named Forge, Photoreal, Prism and Anime, and on video models called Base, Plus and Omni.

How Chat and Memory Work

Older chat is compressed into short memories, which the wiki calls recursive summarisation. Each memory holds up to 700 characters.

Free accounts get 1 memory slot per Peep, Peep Pal gets 5, Peep Pro gets 20, and only Pro can edit them. We found no token size for the context window, so a token comparison with another app would be a guess.

Group chats scale with the plan. Chat and Pal list 3 groups of 2 Peeps.

Pro lists 10 groups of 3. New chats also start in 2 temporary slots, and a Peep that nobody has saved can disappear with the chat that held it.

Save the one you care about.

Permanent slots are 3 on the free plan, 25 on Peep Pal and 45 on Peep Pro, according to the wiki. That is the practical limit, ahead of any slogan about an unlimited library.

Images, Video and Voice

Forge edits and combines images. Photoreal is the lifelike engine.

The wiki says Prism has the widest adult range, and Anime has 14 sub-styles. Public Peep profiles have to stay suitable for a public page.

A private adult image set as the cover becomes public, which is an easy way to publish something you meant to keep in the chat.

That engine list is why it also sits beside a ranking of the best NSFW AI image generators. The ranking is about pictures.

Peeps charges coins for them on top of the chat plan.

Video starts from an image you already have. A Quick Video of 5 seconds at 480p is a flat 50 coins.

The wiki's rough Base guide runs from 40 coins for 5 seconds at 480p up to 550 coins for 15 seconds at 1080p. Plus costs about 1.5 times Base, and Omni, which adds sound, starts at 100 coins for 5 seconds at 480p.

The exact cost is shown before you confirm.

Voice messages are 3 coins each, unless a subscriber's monthly audio allowance covers them. That allowance is listed as 600 on Peep Pal and 2,800 on Peep Pro.

Tavern Card Import

This is the feature that matters if you are leaving another app. The wiki says you can upload a Tavern card in V1, V2 or V3, as a PNG or a JSON file, and the creator fills itself in.

SillyTavern, Chub and Janitor cards are named, along with most apps that export the same format.

People comparing those libraries usually pass through a ranking of the best Janitor AI alternatives. Peeps is the odd one in that set, because the import itself is the product.

A conversation can come with the card. The latest 150 messages stay word for word, and anything older is folded into a memory summary.

Importing is free, up to 10 cards and 5 chats a day. Example dialogues, lorebooks and system prompts are left behind, and a hidden definition is guessed.

Creating the Peep still costs 30 coins, the same as any other new character.

Real-person and copyrighted characters can be imported and are kept private. Minor characters are refused.

Character.AI has no download button, so the wiki points at a browser extension, and RisuAI's .charx files are unsupported.

What AI Peeps Costs

You can open a character and chat with no account and no email. That taste uses a lighter model and includes no images, no voice and no saving.

A free account unlocks the full chat model, 20 PeepCoins at signup, the first few chat pictures at no cost, and a daily check-in of 10 coins. There is no monthly coin allowance on the free plan.

The daily message cap is the unpublished number. The wiki says heavy use over several days can drop the allowance to 10 messages a day, and that each message past the free ones costs 3 coins.

A separate note says the cap is 80 messages a day and is shown inside the chat. The public pages do not print one figure you can budget against.

Thirty days of check-ins at 10 coins would be about 300 coins. That is our arithmetic from the published bonus, and it assumes you show up.

Paid plans are listed at $8.99, $14.99 and $29.99 a month, with 300, 1,700 and 3,900 coins. On 3 November 2026 the live plans page showed Peep Pal at $14.99, a first month at $7.49, and a yearly price of $149.88, presented as $12.49 a month.

Peep Chat and Peep Pro monthly prices come from the site's own list file, because the plans page shows one tier at a time. Yearly billing is described as ten months' price.

Ten months of Pro at $29.99 would be $299.90. That is arithmetic we did, and a figure we did not see printed.

Paid plans include unlimited text chat. Monthly allowance coins do not roll over.

Purchased coins do not expire. Prices, credits and payment methods can change, so the plans page on the day you pay is the contract.

The coin table lists 3 coins for a text message past the free ones, 3 for voice, 10 for an image, 10 for an edit and 30 to create a Peep. On 3 November 2026 the shop showed 900 coins for $4.99, 2,200 for $9.99, 5,800 for $24.99 and 12,500 for $49.99.

A 10-coin image lands near 6 cents in the small pack and near 4 cents in the large one. The wiki's example pack prices run higher, and the shop at checkout wins.

Cards run through CCBill on the payments wiki, which matches the card button on the plans page. The privacy policy names PayPal, NOWPayments, G2A, Stripe and Finby as examples, and it does not name CCBill.

Telegram Stars, G2A and crypto are offered too. A gift card or a code is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Card plans renew until you cancel in the account panel, and the terms say cardholders are emailed at least 7 days before a recurring charge. Refunds are described as generally final in the terms and as case by case in the wiki.

Is AI Peeps Safe?

Public media has to stay suitable for a public page, and an image is sorted by what was actually produced, which can differ from the prompt.

The real-person rule is written three ways. The home FAQ says content involving real people is never allowed.

The terms ban sexualised depictions of real people without explicit consent. The wiki says real-person characters can be imported if they stay private.

We cannot say which sentence the product enforces.
