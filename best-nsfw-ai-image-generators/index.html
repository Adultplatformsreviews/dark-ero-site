What earned a place

A tool had to produce a still, with a free start or an honest paid price, and a written adult rule. Marketing that only says uncensored did not move the rank.

1. Kalon: one face across many stills

Kalon attaches a generator to a companion so the same character can appear in more than one still. On 13 November 2026 the home page said image generation was down, along with chat and video, while a version 2 is built.

Premium was still listed at $19.97 a month or $119.64 a year, shown as $9.97 a month. Coin costs for a still and a clip are in our Kalon AI review.

SpicyRanked's Kalon AI review walks through the same coin table. Pay only after the home page says generation is running again.

2. Candy.ai: photoreal companion stills

Candy.ai is a companion that also generates stills, and photoreal output is the reason it sits this high. On 13 November 2026 the subscriptions page showed a 12-month plan at $3.99 a month, marked 70% off, against a $13.99 line.

Our Candy.ai review covers how media spends the token allowance. An outside Candy AI review asks the same checkout questions.

SpicyRanked's Candy AI review is a second writeup of that plan. A heavy month of stills will outrun the included media budget.

3. Promptchan: a prompt box with daily gems

Promptchan is a prompt-first adult image tool, with chat added on, and a free start that uses daily gems. On 13 November 2026 the upgrade page listed Plus at $14.99, Premium at $23.99 and Pro at $33.99, each billed every 28 days.

That cycle is shorter than a calendar month, so a year of renewals costs more than twelve times a monthly headline. The page markets uncensored generation, and it still says adults only, with no minors and no real-person sexual deepfakes.

4. SoulGen: portraits, with a terms clash

SoulGen is an image-first portrait tool, with anime and realistic modes and a video add-on. Its pricing page did not show a dollar figure in the HTML on 13 November 2026.

Older terms have banned uploading pornographic material while the product sells adult character images, so read both pages. Use it for a portrait, and use Kalon or Candy when the character also has to chat.

5. NovelAI: tagged anime art

NovelAI is an anime image generator paired with a storyteller, and the art side is tag-driven. On 13 November 2026 the home page said subscriptions start at $10 a month.

The public gallery is kept non-explicit, which is a display rule rather than a full description of private output. Learn the tags if anime is the goal, and skip it if you wanted a companion selfie.

6. OurDream: companion stills on a coin budget

OurDream generates stills of a character you build, in realistic or anime styles, and the same account can chat. On 13 November 2026 the help center listed $19.99 a month or $119.88 a year, both with 1,000 DreamCoins a month.

Our OurDream AI review explains that free accounts do not generate images. The published rules ban minors, deepfakes of real people and lookalikes.

7. Mage: a model workshop

Mage, from Ollano, is a workshop of models and inpainting rather than a girlfriend with a face you keep. We did not read a live dollar plan on 13 November 2026, so none is quoted.

Its own material has allowed creative freedom and still banned minors and deepfakes. Choose it when you want to hop models, and choose Kalon when you want one character.

8. Perchance: free community generators

Perchance is a pile of community generators, many of which run in the browser with no account. There is no official paid tier on perchance.org, and quality varies by which page you open.

Some community pages have a weak age gate, which is a reason to be more careful, not less. It will not hold one face the way a companion generator does.

9. Kindroid: selfies of one Kin

Kindroid generates selfies of the single character you build, and the website has been the looser filter compared with the phone apps. The homepage did not print a price on 13 November 2026.

Our Kindroid review has the web price from the company help pages. SpicyRanked's Kindroid review covers the selfie credits.

10. Seduced: stills without a chatbot

Seduced presents itself as a dedicated stills generator, with style extensions and no companion chat to set up. We did not confirm a current plan price on 13 November 2026.

Celebrity deepfakes have been prohibited in its terms, and prompting around that ban can close the account. It is a specialist tool, so compare the entry price with Promptchan's 28-day plans before you commit.

11. Unstability: hosted models

Unstability is a hosted art generator, formerly Unstable Diffusion, aimed at people who want several models in one login. We are not quoting a plan price, because the figure was not one we re-read as a clean public rate on 13 November 2026.

Illegal content, including anything involving minors, is still illegal on a hosted model. Read the current allowed-content answer, which has been thin on the public FAQ.

12. Civitai: a model library with a split front door

Civitai is the checkpoint library, with on-site generation, and in 2026 it split a general front door from an unrestricted sibling site. We did not quote Bronze or higher prices, because the amounts were not in a static page we are willing to treat as current.

Use the rules on the domain you actually generate on, because the two doors are not the same product. It is a workshop for people who already know checkpoints, not a one-click selfie.

13. SmutFinder: stories that now mention pictures

SmutFinder is a story generator first, and it is on an image list because the homepage now talks about pictures inside the story. On 13 November 2026 the pricing page listed credit packs from $4.99 for 200 credits up to $149.99 for 11,250.

Our SmutFinder review treats it as a text product, so confirm the pictures in the app before you pay for them. SpicyRanked's SmutFinder review covers the same packs.

14. SpicyChat: in-chat stills on a higher plan

SpicyChat is a character chat, and it makes this list only because paid tiers can generate a still inside the thread. The subscribe page did not show prices in the HTML on 13 November 2026.

Our SpicyChat AI review explains which tier has been the one that unlocks images. Free text on SpicyChat is a chat product, and the still is the paid extra.
