What Is SecretUndies?

SecretUndies is a person-to-person marketplace for worn intimate items. The platform hosts the shops and payments but is not a party to the sale itself.

It offers around ten language versions, yet its Terms say the service is aimed at Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Every shop seen in the review was based in Germany.

Dark Ero keeps a dedicated buy used panties category if you want to compare this niche at a glance. Its wider fetish directory covers related marketplaces too.

How SecretUndies Works

Each seller gets a small shop with listings, a chat inbox and order tools. Buyers browse, message the seller, pay through checkout and confirm once the parcel arrives.

What Buyers See

The marketplace lists dozens of categories, but SpicyRanked found only a handful of live listings, all from one seller. Prices ranged from about €15 to €80.

Buyers can also post a request describing what they want. With so few active shops, we cannot say how often those requests are answered.

How Selling Works

A free account lets you build a profile and a few private draft listings. To publish, you must buy a monthly plan, and listings are still reviewed.

Product photos must not show faces, recognisable tattoos or other identifying details. Explicit images and body fluids are banned outright.

SecretUndies Fees and Seller Plans

There is no separate buyer fee that we could find. Buyers pay the item price plus shipping shown at checkout.

  • Starter: €4.97 a month, 20% platform fee, up to 5 active products.
  • Premium: €14.97 a month, 15% fee, up to 30 products, verification and one weekly boost.
  • VIP: €29.97 a month, 5% fee, unlimited products and two weekly boosts.

On a €50 sale, a Starter seller keeps €40 and a VIP seller keeps €47.50, before the monthly plan. Extra boosts cost €5 for 24 hours or €10 for a week.

Payouts

Payouts start at €50 and go by bank transfer. The Terms say money is released no earlier than 21 days after valid tracking or proof of shipping.

Disputes, refunds and chargebacks can delay that further, and sellers carry chargeback costs. Stripe may also ask for ID before paying out.

Is SecretUndies Safe?

It is reasonably safe for buyers who keep every step on the platform. Its checks on who is actually selling are thinner than its marketing implies.

Age and Seller Verification

All users must be 18 or older, and SecretUndies may request proof at any time. We found no mandatory age check when an account is created.

The verified badge is bundled with the Premium plan and its check steps are not explained. Even the help pages say a badge is no guarantee.

Payment Protection

Buyer money is described as held until the buyer confirms the order, with Stripe processing card, SEPA and Klarna payments. Sharing phone numbers, emails or outside payment links is banned and filtered in chat.

Any request to pay off-platform is a classic scam sign. The Dark Ero guide on avoiding seller scams and this independent list of six common selling scams apply equally to worn items.

SecretUndies Privacy

Privacy is where SecretUndies shines. Its homepage made no outside requests before consent and set only two first-party cookies.

Hosting is with IONOS in Germany under a GDPR agreement. Transaction records are kept for up to 10 years for tax reasons, which is normal.

Sellers should use a parcel locker or pickup point rather than a home address. Dark Ero's safety guides include more tips on staying anonymous.

SecretUndies Pros and Cons

What Works

  • No third-party tracking before consent.
  • Payments and chat stay on the platform.
  • Published fees from 20% down to 5%.
  • Named Cyprus company with complaint channels.
  • Data hosted in Germany.

What Does Not

  • Almost empty catalogue when checked.
  • No mandatory age check at signup.
  • Vague seller verification.
  • Paid plan required before your shop goes live.
  • Slow payouts with a €50 minimum.

SecretUndies Verdict

SecretUndies gets privacy, payments and fee clarity right. What it lacks is buyers, sellers and firm age checks.

We give it 5.5 out of 10. It could suit privacy-minded sellers in the DACH region, but most people will earn more on a larger marketplace today.

SecretUndies FAQ

How much does it cost to sell on SecretUndies?

Plans cost €4.97, €14.97 or €29.97 a month, with platform fees of 20%, 15% or 5% per sale.

When do SecretUndies sellers get paid?

No earlier than 21 days after shipping proof, by bank transfer, once your balance reaches €50.

Is SecretUndies safe for buyers?

Yes, if you keep payment and chat on the platform. Never pay a seller through outside links.

Does SecretUndies verify sellers?

Verification exists but its steps are not explained, and it comes with the Premium plan rather than being mandatory.

Who runs SecretUndies?

SecretUndies Ltd, a company with an address in Larnaca, Cyprus.

Is SecretUndies only for Germany?

Its Terms target German-speaking countries, although the site offers several languages.
