What Is Chaturbate?

Chaturbate is an adult live stream where independent broadcasters run public shows and viewers tip with tokens. You do not need an account to watch.

You need a free account to tip, follow someone, or use most of the paid features. The help center says many broadcasters set token amounts for specific actions, and the terms treat every tip as a gratuity.

A private show is one to one, charged per minute at the broadcaster's rate, with an optional minimum length. Only a viewer can request one.

Other viewers can pay per minute to spy unless the broadcaster turns that off. A premium private has its own rate and does not allow spying.

Password shows hide the room and take any agreed tokens up front. Ticket shows run through room apps, and the ticket is a tip of a set amount.

What Tokens Cost, and What Broadcasters Earn

Token package prices appear only after you log in, and the figure may include VAT. The help center values 200 tokens at $20.99, which is the one public anchor worth remembering.

Crypto purchases start at 250 tokens and are described as the best rate. A wire transfer has a $250 minimum and credits at $0.08 a token.

A money order or certified check starts at $160, in US dollars, payable to Multi Media LLC. The help center also lists more than 37 regional methods, including PayGarden gift cards.

Independent broadcasters convert tokens at $0.05 each, so 1,000 tokens pay $50 before fees. Studio broadcasters do not convert tokens themselves.

The balance goes to the studio each night. Pay periods run from the 1st to the 15th and from the 16th to the end of the month, with payment inside 7 business days, and most methods need at least $50.

Daily payouts cost $3.95 a day and open after a few normal payouts. Every payee has to be confirmed as over 18.

Supporter membership is $19.95 a month for no ads, anonymous tipping, room previews, the ability to message a broadcaster first, and a one-time bonus of 200 tokens. Since 30 June 2025, members in Sweden cannot buy, send or receive tokens, after a change in Swedish law.

That is a hard stop, not a setting you can toggle.

Auto Refill, Recordings and the Statement

Auto Refill is optional and works with cards only. When the balance drops below 10 percent of the last purchase, it buys that package again, so someone who bought 100 tokens is charged again at 10 tokens left.

You can switch it off in settings. Leave it off until you have decided a refill is something you want while a show is running.

Tips are final when they are sent. A broadcaster cannot be forced to refund one.

If the show was not what was promised, the route is a report, not a chargeback you invent in chat. Billing errors have to be raised within 30 days of first appearing on a statement.

Unused money on deposit can be withdrawn through support, minus the processor's costs. Billing support is listed at 1-877-338-7068.

Broadcasters can let a private-show buyer keep a recording, and the terms say that option is on by default. Look at the prompt before you confirm.

Cam to Cam shares your camera, video only, and it needs an age check. Fan clubs are monthly memberships priced by each broadcaster, paid by card or tokens, and they renew until you cancel.

Members get that broadcaster's fan-club content and a green username in chat.

There is no official app. The mobile browser is the product, and you can pin a shortcut on an iPhone or an Android home screen.

When Epoch handles a card payment, Epoch's name is what shows on the statement. The billing page does not publish the descriptor for direct card billing, so the first charge is the one to read while you still remember making it.

Is Chaturbate Safe?

This is a long-running platform with a named operator, not a nameless popup. It is only for adults of 18, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher.

Broadcasters upload a government ID and a selfie, and anyone who appears on stream has to be verified too. The privacy policy names Incode for identity checks, which can include a faceprint.

Accounts, uploads and broadcasts are subject to real-time automated and manual review. Reports are reviewed within 4 business days, and broadcasters get a free content-takedown notice service.

For a viewer the risks are spending and privacy, not a missing company. Chat, tip notes, profiles and private messages are treated as public information under a privacy policy dated 8 November 2023.

Fraud-prevention data is kept indefinitely. If you ask for deletion, the username is kept to stop impersonation, and an account deactivated for more than 120 days cannot be reactivated.

Do not pay anyone off the site. Two-step verification works with an app, a text, a phone call or a hardware key, and the help center says the codes do not mention Chaturbate.
