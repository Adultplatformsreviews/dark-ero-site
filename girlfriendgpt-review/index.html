Prices You Cannot See Yet

The subscription page sends visitors to a sign-in screen. Public pages do not list the plan prices.

Earlier write-ups quoted monthly numbers we could not confirm without an account, so those numbers are not repeated here. If a blog still prints a GirlfriendGPT price, treat it as stale until you see the checkout yourself.

What the terms do say is enough to judge the shape. A free example is up to 2 or 3 characters and about 10 to 20 messages in total.

After that you earn credits with tasks or you upgrade. Paid tiers add more characters and somewhere between 5,000 and 20,000 message credits a month, depending on the tier.

Credits reset every month and do not carry over.

The terms say paid subscriptions are not refunded, except for a company mistake or a technical problem. Requests are reviewed within 14 days.

If you paid through CCBill, you ask CCBill for any refund and you cancel on CCBill's site. Other subscriptions cancel on the account's manage page.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not return the rest of the current period.

A stricter refund law in your country still applies.

Payments are described as handled by CCBill and TrustPay. There is no App Store app.

The download page offers an installable web app and an Android file from the website. Support is a contact form, an FAQ and a Discord.

We would not install an Android file from a product we already do not recommend.
