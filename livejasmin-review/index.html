What Is LiveJasmin?

LiveJasmin is a webcam platform with female, male, trans and couple performers. Models stream in free chat, and members pay credits to take them private, join a VIP show or start a video call.

Basic members can chat in free rooms and save favourites. Premium, which is what you become by buying at least 27.99 credits, can go private, send offline messages and send Surprises.

The filters are the part that feels finished. Category, show type, price, language, age range and a long list of tags are on the public grid.

On 3 October 2026 the price filter grouped rooms from 0.01 credits a minute up through 9.99 and beyond. That band is public.

The pack that turns it into money is not.

Private Shows, VIP Shows and Cam2Cam

A private show is one to one and billed per minute at the model's rate. A VIP show is a group show with a pre-show and a credit goal, and it starts if the goal is met or if the model starts it.

Video calls are listed as their own show type. Inside a private you can switch on Cam2Cam or two-way audio, and the FAQ notes that not every model offers the audio.

Club Elite is a free loyalty scheme that gives points for spending, with ranks from Crush to Eros. Pro Membership is a separate paid tier, with perks such as free messages and going invisible in chat.

Fan Clubs are monthly subscriptions to one model, in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond, and they renew until you end them under My Subscriptions.

The terms say there is no refund once you start using a credit package. A credit refund is described for narrow cases such as a system fault.

Cash back applies only to a package that has not been touched at all. A new-member offer of 50 percent off the first purchase was marked as limited, and the terms mention 9.99 promotional credits for validating a card.

Confirm both on the account you actually open, because a promo line is not a permanent rate.

SmartBuy and the Statement

SmartBuy buys more credits when the balance falls to 7, so a private is not cut off mid-sentence. The FAQ says the default purchase is a 27.99 credit package.

That is a second buy you did not click, arriving at the moment the show is working. Leave it off unless you have already decided you want that pack, and check the switch before a long session.

The price you pay follows the country of the first purchase, which means the account's first checkout sets a track for later ones. The Get Credits link sends logged-out visitors to sign up, so there is no honest way to quote a universal dollar price in this review.

Older write-ups that listed fixed pack prices were guessing. This one does not.

The FAQ says charges appear as JWSbill or CCBill, depending on the processor. JWSbill belongs to the Luxembourg operators, who also handle billing support.

The terms were last modified on 20 April 2026. There is no iOS app.

An Android app is offered from the mobile site, outside Google Play, which is a download to treat with the same suspicion as any cam-site APK you did not go looking for.

Safety and What the Site Keeps

The terms say performers are 18 or older, or 21 in some regions, and the site publishes a 2257 statement. The footer links to content removal, non-consensual imagery removal, a DMCA form and an EU Digital Services Act page.

The FAQ says the site uses 256-bit SSL and is audited each year against the PCI DSS card standard. Some places require an age check at signup, which the site says it hands to a third party.

The privacy policy names joint controllers and a data protection officer, and it says personal data is not sold as California law defines a sale. Chat logs, device data and usage data are kept for up to 120 days after your last purchase.

Financial records are kept for ten years under Luxembourg law, so closing the account does not wipe the billing file. On desktop, Forget My Data sits in Privacy Settings.

On mobile, the FAQ says you email support to ask.

LiveJasmin Pros and Cons

What Works

  • Price-band filters are usable before you join.
  • Cam2Cam, two-way audio and video calls are part of the private.
  • VIP group shows can split a goal across members.
  • The Luxembourg operators, the billing name and the terms date are public.
  • Removal routes for content and non-consensual imagery are in the footer.

What Does Not

  • Credit pack prices stay hidden until signup.
  • Later prices follow the country of the first purchase.
  • SmartBuy can buy a pack when the balance hits 7 credits.
  • There is little to do for free beyond chat.
  • Refunds are limited once a pack has been used, and there is no iOS app.
