What Is Fapello?

Fapello is an adult discovery site where users submit images and clips that are grouped into pages for specific people. Navigation includes search, trending posts, rankings, a forum and user feeds.

Its own marketing ties the service to OnlyFans models, influencers and adult creators. That wording is a positioning claim, not proof of any partnership or licence.

If you came here looking for a specific creator, Dark Ero's guide on supporting creators directly explains why leak-style sites are a poor shortcut.

How Fapello Works

Public pages can be browsed without an account or payment. The interface also advertises sign-up, uploads and account features, which we did not test.

Profile pages sometimes include links presented as a person's social accounts. Those links can help you find an official account, but they do not show who uploaded the content.

Where the Content Comes From

Fapello describes a user-submission model and asks uploaders to grant it a licence. A licence clause cannot confirm that the uploader had the right to share a given photo in the first place.

Google's public copyright transparency records also list removal requests for fapello.com URLs. A request is an allegation, not a court ruling, but the volume is a useful signal.

Is Fapello Free?

Browsing is presented as free and paid for by advertising. We found no required subscription.

Free does not mean risk-free. Clicking an ad can take you to a separate service with its own charges, and that is where many people get caught.

Is Fapello Safe?

We cannot call Fapello safe. Device security, advertising, privacy and consent are separate issues, and none is fully settled by public evidence.

Ads and Redirects

Fapello's own login notice says unregistered visitors will see pop ads. Ads vary by device and country, so no single check can clear every destination.

Never install an extension, player or app offered by a pop-up. Dark Ero's guides on telling a real adult site from a malware trap and browser hygiene for private browsing are worth a few minutes.

The Ofcom Decision

In July 2026 Ofcom imposed £600,000 plus £30,000 in fixed penalties, with a further conditional daily penalty. The findings concern age assurance and responses to information requests in the UK.

That is a formal regulatory finding, not a ruling that every item on the site is unlawful everywhere. It still weighs heavily on trust.

Fapello Privacy

The privacy policy describes collecting IP addresses, browser and device data, plus third-party ad and analytics cookies. We could not find a clear update date or a named company with a postal address.

A photo-search feature has been described, but how it matches faces and stores uploads is not explained. Do not upload anyone's face to it.
