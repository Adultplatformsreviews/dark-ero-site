What NoShame Actually Is

NoShame is an adult AI character chat. The marketing name on the home page is No Shame AI. Legal pages say NoShame.

You pick or design a girlfriend, boyfriend or anime character, then chat and ask for pictures.

The homepage title we saw claimed "200+ Characters". The public sitemap on 17 November 2026 listed 271 URLs under /chat, up from the 270 character URLs counted on 16 November.

We did not open every profile, so treat 271 as a sitemap count. It is consistent with the "200+" line, and it is not a hand audit.

The nav also includes a creator, a generate studio, character profiles and a collection. A trans collection URL is in the sitemap.

Character import was still a waitlist when that page was read on 16 November 2026, with Character.AI, Chai and Janitor AI named as sources you might leave.

We could not try an import because the tool was not live.

On 10 November the creator copy still talked about turning a photo into a character.

By 16 November that upload line was gone from the public creator, which opened on settings, including an age slider that started at 18.

We did not sign in on 17 November, so we cannot swear the upload is gone inside every account. The public creator no longer advertised it.

The Terms name the agreement as being with NoShame, the operator of noshame.ai. The privacy notice we opened does the same.

Neither page printed a legal company, a postal address or a governing law. Support email on the site is hello@noshame.ai.

Signup screens have carried lines such as "3.2k reviews" and "thousands online". We did not find a public review log behind those figures, so both stay slogans.

What the Public Prices Say

You can see the plan table without paying. On 17 November 2026 the Premium page, after the 18+ notice, showed three Premium periods.

  • 1 month, billed monthly: $19.99 a month.
  • 3 months, billed quarterly: $15.99 a month, marked 20 percent off.
  • 12 months, billed annually: $7.99 a month, marked 60 percent off and Best Value.

Premium lists unlimited messages, 200 gems a month, more photos and videos, priority response, and a line that reads "No restrictions".

The compare table on the same page lists a Premium+ column: unlimited messages, 500 gems a month, a choice of AI model and a choice of reply length.

The dollar buttons for that column, read on the public page on 16 November 2026, were $39.99 a month, $31.99 billed quarterly and $23.99 billed annually.

The 17 November render we saved showed the Premium prices above, and it still described Premium+ with the 500-gem row.

Confirm the Premium+ checkout total before you tap it. We did not complete one.

The free row on that compare table is 15 messages, one-time, and a 15 gem welcome gift, with no image generation, video generation or priority support.

The home FAQ says the welcome messages do not reset. When they are gone, the page points you at Premium.

Treat the free plan as a 15-message sample.

Paid plans renew until you cancel. The Premium FAQ says you can cancel and keep access until the end of the current period.

Card payments run through Epoch, described as the authorised sales agent. Visa, Mastercard, American Express and cryptocurrency are the methods named on that FAQ.

The page says card details are not stored on NoShame's servers. A change made at Epoch can take up to an hour to show in the account.

We did not open a live crypto invoice, so that flow stays at the published wording.

The refund policy, last updated 7 July 2026, says purchases unlock digital features immediately.

It lists refunds for duplicate or erroneous charges, a confirmed fault that could not be fixed, an unauthorised charge, a refund required by consumer law, and any other case of dissatisfaction, which it says will be reviewed on its merits.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The contact address is hello@noshame.ai.

Gems, Photos and Voice Costs

Gems are the extra currency. The terms call them virtual items with no cash value.

They cannot be sold, transferred or redeemed for money. Unlocked items are described as staying in your collection.

On 17 November 2026 the Premium page priced what gems buy:

  • A photo costs 4 gems.
  • A 5 second video costs 15 gems, and a 10 second video costs 30.
  • Cinematic video was listed at 30 gems for 5 seconds, 60 for 10 seconds and 120 for 20 seconds.
  • A voice call costs 12 gems a minute.

The same page said gem packs start at $9.99 for 300 gems.

On 16 November 2026 the public store listed the rest of the ladder: 600 gems for $19.99, 1,650 for $49.99, 3,450 for $99.99, 7,200 for $199.99 and 11,250 for $299.99, with the four largest packs marked as a 10 to 25 percent bonus.

The crypto tab showed the same amounts that day.

We did not re-open every pack on 17 November, so treat the upper rungs as the 16 November store and check the live store before you buy a large one.

On the smallest pack a gem is about 3.3 cents, so a 4-gem photo is about 13 cents before any bonus gems.

That is our arithmetic from the published pack, not a price the site prints per photo.

There is also an earn-gems path: post a watermarked character image to listed subreddits, leave it up for a day, and submit the link.

The first approved post ties the Reddit account to the NoShame account.

User-made character images cannot be shared for gems, and the site's own subreddit does not count. We did not run that flow.

Safety Policies and the Age Gate

On 17 November 2026 every public page we opened put an 18+ notice up first.

It says the site contains explicit AI-generated adult content, offers an "I am under 18" exit, and treats any other choice as confirming you are 18 or older and that adult content is legal where you live.

That is a self-declaration. We saw no ID check and no age-estimation step.

The written rulebook is broader than many new companion apps publish.

The underage policy, dated 7 July 2026, bans sexualised depictions of minors, including age-play framed as underage, and says automated systems block prohibited requests before a reply.

It also says records may be kept and material reported to NCMEC or other authorities.

The blocked-content policy bans minors, non-consent, incest framed as a blood relative, bestiality, necrophilia, extreme sexualised violence, and non-consensual intimate imagery of real people, and it says content must stay lawful and inside card-network rules.

Those are written rules. We did not chat, so we cannot tell you how the filter behaves on a real message.

The 2257 exemption says the depictions are fictional and AI-generated, with no real performers. The anti-trafficking policy bans using the service to arrange real-world sexual services.

We did not find evidence that would let us call NoShame a scam. Dated policies, a named billing agent and a refund path are real.

The missing legal entity, and an age gate that is only a click, are why the trust score stays in the middle.

Privacy Notice Versus the Home FAQ

The homepage says "100% Privacy Protection." The home FAQ, still live on 17 November 2026, says chats are encrypted and that no personal data is shared with third parties.

The privacy notice, last updated 7 July 2026, is narrower. It says NoShame does not sell personal data.

It also says data is shared with service providers that help run the service, with hosting, analytics, payment and AI infrastructure named as examples, under safeguards and where the law requires it.

Those two statements do not match. Read the notice, not the FAQ tile, before you put a real detail in a chat.

The notice says the service collects account data such as an email and a display name, usage data such as messages and prompts, payment status from the processor rather than full card numbers, and optional persona details.

Retention lasts while the account is active and as needed for legal, safety and accounting reasons, then data is deleted or anonymised.

No fixed number of days after deletion is published.

You can delete the account in settings, and you can write to hello@noshame.ai for access, correction, deletion or export.

The notice says the service is not directed at anyone under 18 and that data from minors will be deleted if discovered.

We did not find a public line that keeps chats out of model training.

The terms say you keep ownership of your prompts and grant a licence to process them to operate, secure and improve the service.

"Improve" is not defined, so training use stays unverified.

If you leave, the practical steps are in how to delete AI companion data. Cancel the charge first, then delete the account.
