What Is AI Angels?

AI Angels is an adult AI companion website where you chat with ready-made virtual girlfriends. Its homepage tagline invites you to create your dream AI girlfriend.

The service is operated by AiAppDevelopment OÜ, a company based in Tallinn, Estonia. Card payments go through the billing company Verotel, which shows a discreet descriptor on statements.

It sits among the sites in the SpicyRanked list of best AI girlfriend apps. Dark Ero's AI sex chat sites section covers the same type of product.

How AI Angels Works

After an 18+ screen, you land on a grid of companions with filters for ethnicity, body type and age bands. Pick one and the chat opens straight away.

Some text chat is free, but the limit is described differently across the site. Paid features then consume a monthly credit allowance.

Main Features

  • Text chat with a large roster of preset companions.
  • A custom companion builder on paid plans.
  • Group chats with two companions at once.
  • Photos, voice notes, calls and short video, paid with credits.
  • Long-term memory that the site claims lasts beyond 90 days, which we could not test.

One oddity stood out. The homepage grid included an advert for a rival companion service, ourdream.ai, sitting among the AI Angels characters.

AI Angels Pricing

Pricing is the hardest part of this review. SpicyRanked saw two different plan tables on repeat visits, which suggests visitors are split into test groups.

One version lists a $12.99 monthly plan with about 1,200 credits and a $39.99 plan with about 3,600 credits. The other lists $3.99 a month billed every 6 months, $5.99 a month billed quarterly, and $12.99 monthly.

The second version also sells one-time credit packs from $9.99. A homepage banner separately advertised a discounted Premium price, and we could not map it to either table.

Our advice is simple. Screenshot the exact offer at checkout before paying, so you have proof of what you agreed to.

For a wider look at how plan tiers hide costs, see the Dark Ero guide on what free versus premium really buys you.

Refunds and Cancellation

You can cancel at any time and keep access until the period ends. Refund requests must arrive within 24 hours of payment, and only if you used no credits.

EU and UK users have a 14 day withdrawal right on paper. The Terms say it can be lost once you start using the digital service.

Is AI Angels Safe?

Its written rules are strict, banning anyone who appears under 18. The problem is that some of the site's own marketing pulls in the opposite direction.

A footer link promotes a teen (18+) companion category with youthful framing. Even with an 18+ label, that is a poor choice for any adult AI service.

Age checking at entry is a click-through button rather than real verification. We would like to see stronger checks for a site this explicit.

Privacy and Data

AI Angels promises not to sell or rent personal data, and it publishes a 24 hour removal target for illegal content. Those are welcome commitments.

Its privacy policy is short, though. It says little about how long chats are kept or whether they help train AI models.

Before sharing anything personal, read the Dark Ero piece on AI adult tools and your data and its privacy guides. SpicyRanked also explains how to delete AI companion data.
