How this ranking was put together

The order follows five plain questions: do the written rules allow the adult chat you want, does the app explain memory, are photos or voice actually in the product, is the price on a public page, and does a company name sit on the legal pages. Looks and ad slogans did not move a rank.

  • Rules. Adult chat has to be allowed in writing, with a clear ban on minors.
  • Memory. A companion that forgets you by the next day is a chatbot with a pet name.
  • Media. Photos, voice and video only count when the product actually offers them.
  • Price. The number on the public page beats a banner that hides the renewal.
  • A name. A company, a policy date and an age rule should be findable before you pay.

Kalon still leads on product shape, because one character is meant to cover chat, stills and video. Its home page also said the features were down, which is the first thing to read before a card charge.
