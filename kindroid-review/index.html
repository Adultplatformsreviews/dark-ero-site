What Is Kindroid?

Kindroid is a subscription AI companion where you write a character profile with a backstory, key memories, a voice and an avatar. The app's pitch is an AI friend that listens, remembers and grows with you.

It runs on the web, iOS and Android. The app stores list the seller as Beautifully Incorporated, with a Los Angeles mailing address in the legal pages.

How Kindroid Works

Every Kin combines the profile you write with several layers of memory. The goal is a companion that recalls earlier details instead of restarting each session.

Kindroid Memory

Persistent memory holds the backstory, key memories, example messages, directives and recent chat. Cascaded memory, for subscribers, stretches back across hundreds to thousands of messages.

Retrievable memory covers long-term memories and journals that the Kin pulls in when relevant. It reads well on paper, though recall over many weeks is the thing to judge for yourself.

Calls and Selfies

Subscribers get voice and video calls and a monthly pool of audio credits. Calls use those credits per minute, and unused credits do not roll over.

Selfies also use credits, which subscribers earn over time. The Help Center says the apps apply an NSFW keyword filter to media, while the web version does not.

That selfie engine is why Kindroid also shows up in the SpicyRanked list of best NSFW AI image generators.

Group Chats and Rooms

Subscribers can run group chats with several Kins. Rooms go further by mixing invited real people and Kins in one private space.

Kindroid Pricing

The web is the cheapest place to subscribe. App store purchases add a markup on every plan and pack.

  • Standard, monthly: $13.99 on the web, $15.99 in the apps.
  • Standard, 3 months: $37.99 on the web.
  • Standard, yearly: $139.99 on the web.
  • Ultra add-on: $24.99 a month on the web, on top of Standard.
  • MAX add-on: $59.99 a month on the web, on top of Standard and Ultra.

Stacked together on the web, Standard plus Ultra is about $39 a month, and the full MAX stack is about $99. Kindroid says the add-ons unlock only after meaningful usage history.

Extra selfie credits, character slots and voice characters are sold separately. Check the Billing menu carefully if you downgrade, because each add-on cancels on its own.

What Happened to the Free Plan

On 1 October 2026 Kindroid retired its free Lite tier. New accounts now get a single preview of about 50 flagship messages, a few selfies and a small audio allowance.

When that runs out, the account turns read-only. Older reviews describing a lasting free Kindroid plan are now out of date.

Trial and Refunds

A 3 day premium trial is available, but it needs a payment method and converts automatically unless you cancel. Starting it also ends the free preview for good.

Kindroid describes refunds as case by case and generally not offered. It also warns that disputing charges can get you banned, so start with a monthly web plan.

Is Kindroid Safe?

Kindroid is unfiltered by design for adults, so safety here means how it handles real harm. It names three red lines: imminent self-harm, imminent harm to others and child sexual abuse material.

It treats anyone under 18 as a minor whatever age a character is given. Enforcement starts with warnings and escalates to an account lock, which we feel is too gentle for the most serious category.

Age Verification

A successful payment counts as age verification for paying users. Free users are screened with behavioral age estimation, and anyone flagged must subscribe or verify through Yoti.

Kindroid says it stores no ID documents and that Yoti deletes verification data. One mismatch is worth knowing: a store listing rated the app below the 18+ rule in its own Terms.

Kindroid Privacy

Kindroid publishes detailed legal pages, which is more than many rivals manage. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted, though, so treat them as readable by the service.
