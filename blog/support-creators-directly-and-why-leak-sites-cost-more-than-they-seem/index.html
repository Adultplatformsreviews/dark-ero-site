The free version of paid content has a price. It is just paid by someone else.

The creator economy changed adult content more than any technology did. Performers can now run their own businesses and keep most of the revenue.

Leak sites exist to break that, and understanding the trade clarifies why direct support matters.

How the direct model works

On subscription platforms, a viewer pays a creator, the platform takes a cut, and the creator keeps the rest. It is the first model in the industry's history where the person on camera controls the business.

That is worth protecting.

What a leak site actually does

A leak site takes that paid content and gives it away to draw traffic it can monetise with advertising. The creator earns nothing, the leak site earns everything, and the viewer funds a stranger's ad revenue while believing they got something for free.

Many creators offer free tiers, previews and public accounts precisely so people can sample their work honestly. That is the free access worth using — given, not taken.

The practical case, not just the moral one

Leak sites are also among the most malware-dense pages on the web, precisely because they attract people looking for something for nothing. The free content often comes bundled with a cost you didn't agree to.

Direct support is safer as well as fairer.
