Is Selling Feet Pics Legal?

A normal photo of an adult's feet does not become a crime because a buyer has a fetish. The harder questions are age, consent, the shop's contract, and whether you report the money.

US adults can generally sell lawful photos of their own feet.

A shop can still ban a payout, a chat, or an off-platform payment that no statute mentions.

Anyone under 18 stays out of this market, including with a parent's permission.

  • Create only what you have the right to sell, and get consent if another adult is in the frame.

Three different questions

People mix up whether the content may be sold at all, whether a named shop will allow it and pay out, and whether the income has to be reported. All three can be true at once, or only the first.

A registered platform, a card processor and an ID check can all be in place while a statement, a leak or a fake login still causes damage. Legal permission does not make the work invisible.

Age and consent

You must be an adult. Do not create, request, sell or distribute sexualized content involving anyone under 18, or under a higher age of majority where that applies.

A parent's permission does not change that. Minors do not belong in this market, as sellers or as people in the frame.

Sell work you made and have the right to distribute. Another creator's feet pics are not yours to upload, and another adult in your photo needs their consent plus whatever ID process the shop requires.

Verdict

For an adult selling lawful photos of their own feet, the US position is generally that the sale is allowed. Age, consent, the shop's contract and tax still sit on top of that.

You can often stay pseudonymous to buyers while the platform and the tax authority still know who you are. This is general information, not legal advice, and your state can add rules a national page will miss.

FAQ

Is it legal to sell feet pics in the US?

Adults can generally sell lawful photos of their own feet, and the buyer's fetish does not by itself make that illegal. Age, consent, shop terms and tax still apply, and this is not legal advice.

Can someone under 18 sell if a parent agrees?

No. Do not involve minors in this market as sellers or as people in the photos, even with a parent's permission.

Do I have to show my face for the sale to be legal?

Face-out selling does not decide legality on its own. You can often hide your face from buyers while the shop still requires a private identity check.

Is moving a sale off the app illegal?

An off-platform payment can be lawful in the abstract and still break the shop's terms, drop the dispute record, and raise the scam risk. Prefer the checkout the site requires.

Do I owe tax on feet pic sales?

US income from this work is generally taxable, including small and part-time amounts, even when no form arrives. Keep records and ask a tax professional if you are unsure how to file.

Do these rules apply in the UK or Australia?

No, those countries have their own criminal, platform and tax rules. Read the UK and Australia guides rather than assuming a US page covers them.
