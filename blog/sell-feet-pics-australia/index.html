How the money comes home

Sellers outside the US are commonly paid through Paxum, or through Masspay where the shop offers it. You then send the money to an Australian bank, and the wallet or the bank may charge to turn US dollars into Australian dollars.

A mid-October 2026 Reserve Bank rate used in the source check put US$30 near A$43. Your provider can use a different rate, so budget the conversion instead of spending the US dollar figure as if it were already Australian.

PayID is handy between Australian banks, and it shows the payer the name linked to it. Do not give a buyer your PayID, your bank details, or a personal payment app when the shop's terms require its own checkout.

Keep the selling money in a separate account so tax time is a ledger rather than a guess. That separation does not hide your identity from someone who is allowed to pay you.
