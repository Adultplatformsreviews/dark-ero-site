How to Cancel an OnlyFans Subscription

Cancelling an OnlyFans subscription means turning auto-renew off for that creator. You keep the days you already paid for, and the next charge is the one that stops.

Dark-Ero editorialUpdated 2026-11-1018+

At a glance

There is no OnlyFans app. Cancel in a browser, on a computer or a phone.

Auto-renew is switched off from the Following list or the creator's profile.

Access runs to the end of the period, and the charge you already made is not refunded.

Key takeaways

  • Cancel, block and delete are three different buttons.
  • Free trials do not renew on their own. A discounted price renews at the full price unless you turn renewal off.
  • OnlyFans describes cancellation one subscription at a time, with no bulk switch.
  • A wallet balance can still pay a renewal if you left that option on.
  • Reload the Following page and check the renewal label before you trust it.

Most people who say they cancelled still get billed, because they blocked a creator or deleted a bookmark. The switch that stops the money is auto-renew, on that subscription.

Screens and the help-center wording are in this how to cancel an OnlyFans subscription guide. The labels can move, so match what you see to the idea, which is renewal off.

The clicks

Open a browser, go to the real site, and sign in. There is no official app for this, and OFTV is a separate free video app that does not manage paid subscriptions.

On a computer, open the account menu and the subscriptions or Following list, then turn auto-renew off for the creator you mean. On a phone, use the mobile browser and the same list, not an icon someone texted you.

You can also turn renewal off from that creator's profile, if the subscription controls are sitting there. Either path is fine, and you should end on a screen that no longer says the subscription will renew.

OnlyFans describes this one creator at a time, and the help center does not offer a cancel-all button. If you pay several people, repeat the step, and do not assume the first one covered the list.

What changes, and what does not

If you cancel on day 15 of a 30-day subscription, you keep the other 15 days. The same is true of a 3, 6 or 12 month bundle: you get the time you paid for, and the next rebill is what stops.

Cancelling is not a refund. Help says subscriptions are not refundable unless the terms were broken, and a charge you simply regret is not that case.

The help pages do not confirm whether the creator is told when you turn renewal off. You remain a subscriber until the paid period ends, so do not assume the change is invisible.

You can turn auto-renew back on before the period ends, and the subscription continues at the next date. After the period ends, subscribing again is a new paid subscription.

A free trial does not auto-renew, though you can choose to renew at the subscription price. A discount is different: when the promotional period ends, it renews at the full price unless renewal is already off.

A bundle you bought at a discount is still a bundle. Cancelling after the first month of a year does not convert the unused months into cash.

Block and delete are different

Blocking a creator is not the documented way to stop a renewal. OnlyFans says subscriptions renew unless auto-renew is off, the subscription was a free trial, the payment fails, or the price goes up.

Deleting the whole account is the third action, and our how to delete your OnlyFans account page is that job. Do not delete the login when you only wanted one creator to stop billing.

The ranking site's how to delete your OnlyFans account guide separates the same two buttons. Creators who delete have to wait out paid time their fans already bought.

A wallet balance can pay renewals if you turned that option on. Removing a saved card is extra caution, and it is not proof the renewal flags are off, so check each subscription anyway.

Confirm it worked

Reload the Following page, or the creator's profile, and read the renewal line. If it still says the subscription will renew, the click did not stick.

Look at the next date on the calendar you actually use, and look at the card statement when that date passes. A charge after a successful cancel is a support email, with the screenshot, not a second guess in a forum.

Verdict

Turn auto-renew off, confirm the label, and use the days you already bought. Do not expect a refund, and do not delete the whole account unless you are done with the site.

Check the Following list once more on the day before the old renewal date. That single look catches the subscription the first pass missed.

FAQ

Do I cancel in an app?

No. OnlyFans has no official app for managing subscriptions.

Use the website in a browser, including the browser on your phone.

Do I lose access the moment I cancel?

No. You keep access until the period you already paid for ends.

The cancellation stops the next charge.

Can I get a refund?

Help treats subscriptions as non-refundable unless the terms were broken. A change of mind is not that situation, and a duplicate charge is a message to support.

Does blocking the creator cancel the bill?

Do not rely on a block. The documented stop is auto-renew, switched off for that subscription.

Check the Following page after you do it.

Will a free trial charge me?

OnlyFans says a free trial does not auto-renew. A discounted subscription renews at the full price unless you turn renewal off.
