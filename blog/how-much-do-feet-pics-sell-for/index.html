What listings ask

A $5 single is the floor many sellers list at, and a lone image is a less common product than a set. Volume discounts mean the per-image price falls as the set gets bigger, which is normal retail, not a mistake.

A custom ten-image set, with poses and props the buyer names, often lists at $60 to $150 because you are shooting to order. Custom video is cleaner as a per-minute rate, and sellers often quote $50 to $200 a minute, with sound adding more.

Worn socks and similar items sit in a different band, often higher per parcel and lower in volume. Tips can match a set for someone with regulars, and they are rare for a seller who has not built those relationships yet.

Subscription pages, where a shop allows them, turn one monthly price into a year of access. The same files can still be sold again as separate items, which is why a $10 subscription and a $30 set are not rivals so much as two products.

FAQ

How much does a single feet pic sell for?

Sellers commonly list singles at $5 to $15. A set is the more usual product, and the per-image price drops as the set gets larger.

How much is a feet pic set?

A five-image set often lists at $20 to $40, and a ten-image set at $40 to $75. Custom sets of about ten images often run $60 to $150.

What do beginners actually make?

Self-reported first months on FeetFinder often fall between $50 and $200, and some sellers make nothing while they learn the shop. Those figures are not an official average.

How much does FeetFinder take?

Basic keeps 85 percent of the sale and Premium keeps 90 percent, after you have paid the plan. The plan is charged even in a month with no sales.

Does Snifffr take a percentage?

Snifffr takes no cut of the sale and charges a Premium membership to message and cash out. A November 2026 check put that membership at $12.95 every 30 days, so confirm it at checkout.

Can I trust a $10,000 month I saw online?

Treat it as one person's claim unless the shop published it. The upper band exists in self-reports, and it is a poor budget for a first month.
