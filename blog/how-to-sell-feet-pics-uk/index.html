The £1,000 allowance

The trading allowance can cover up to £1,000 of gross trading income a year, which is the total you receive before fees and costs. If every side hustle together stays at or under that line, you generally may not have to tell HMRC, and you still keep records.

Over £1,000 of gross trading income you generally register for Self Assessment. You can then deduct up to £1,000 instead of actual expenses, and you cannot claim both, so the choice is whichever your records support.
