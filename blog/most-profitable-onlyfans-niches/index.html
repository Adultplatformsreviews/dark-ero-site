The split is the same everywhere

OnlyFans keeps 20 percent of fan payments and creators keep 80 percent, on subscriptions, paid messages and tips. A niche does not negotiate that number.

Fenix International reported 5.063 million creator accounts for the year to 30 November 2025, up from 4.634 million. It paid creators about 6.2 billion dollars that year, which divides out to roughly 100 dollars a month per account.

Averages like that hide a steep drop. A small group earns a living, and a large group earns less than a night out, which is why a niche is not a business plan on its own.

Fees, the 20 dollar withdrawal minimum and the discovery problem are in our OnlyFans review. The platform will not find your fans for you.

How to pick one you can sustain

Write down what you will not make, and cross off every niche that needs it. A profitable idea you will abandon in week three is a hobby with a fee.

Then ask who already listens to you, on a social account you can actually point at a paywall. A clear angle beats a generic page, because the feed will not explain you.

Fans pay extra for custom messages, a specific theme, and attention, and each of those costs you time. Price the time, or the 80 percent is a percentage of a number you resent.

Subscriptions run from free, or 4.99 dollars, up to 50 dollars a month. New pages often start near the bottom so the first subscribers have an easy yes, and raise the price once the promise is obvious.

Rules that delete a niche

If anyone else appears in explicit content, OnlyFans says you must show they are 18 or older, that they consent, and that they agree to the terms. They can be a tagged creator, or they can verify through a release the company sends.

Most of an account's content has to feature the creator who opened it. A page of other people's clips is not a niche, and it is not allowed.

Roleplay is allowed, and roleplay that would break the rules if it were real is not. Pretending to be under 18, and pretending there is no consent, are the examples the help center names.

The acceptable-use rules also restrict extreme harm, including extreme bondage and content built around bodily waste or injury. If the theme needs that, it is the wrong platform, not a branding opportunity.

AI edits are allowed when the result clearly features you, the verified creator, and fans can see that AI was used. Wholly generated people, or a face the company cannot match to your ID, can be removed, and OnlyFans does not hand creators a chatbot to run the DMs.

Faceless, couples, and feet

Faceless niches lower the chance a neighbour recognises you, and the ID check still happens in private. You can also hide a profile from chosen countries, or from states and provinces in the US, Australia and Canada.

Feet, voice, ASMR and careful framing are the usual faceless routes, because the buyer came for the theme. A weaker personal connection often means a smaller cheque, which is the trade you are making.

Foot content can also live on a marketplace that already has buyers, which is the point of our FeetFinder review. That site charges sellers, and it takes a share, so the 80 percent comparison is not one to one.

Couples need every person in the explicit frame verified before the post. A partner who will not do the ID check is a niche you do not have.

Verdict

The profitable niche is the one you can film for months, that a specific audience already looks for, inside the rules. The 80 percent is identical, and the audience is not.

Ignore any list that quotes a salary for a theme. Price, verification and a place you can actually send people are the parts that pay.

FAQ

What is the most profitable OnlyFans niche?

There is no published ranking that survives contact with the payout data. The accounts that earn are the ones with an audience and a niche they can keep making.

Does the niche change the 80 percent?

No. OnlyFans keeps 20 percent of fan payments in every niche.

Tips, messages and subscriptions are all inside that split.

Can I post without showing my face?

Yes, and the company still verifies your identity in private. Feet, voice and careful framing are the usual routes, and they often earn less than a face at the same audience size.

Are AI pictures allowed?

Only when they clearly show you, the verified creator, and fans can tell AI was used. A fully generated person can be removed.

Can my partner appear?

Only if they are verified as an adult and they have agreed to the terms. Unverified people in explicit posts are how accounts get into trouble.
