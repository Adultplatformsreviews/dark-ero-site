"Best dating app" is a sloppy phrase. Hinge, Bumble and Tinder are built for different evenings, and an average score is how people download the wrong one.

The ranking behind the numbers here is this best dating apps list, checked against public pages in October 2026. The scores are an editorial judgment, not a lab result, and the prices move.

Before you treat any of those numbers as a personality test, read our SpicyRanked review. A score is a sort order, not a date.

Disclosure: Dark-Ero is independent, with no affiliate deal with these apps and no link to their sign-up pages. Adults 18+ only.

How this list was scored

Each app was judged from its own public feature, pricing and help pages. The six marks were match fit, what you actually get, whether the paid plan is worth opening, safety tools, how widely it is used, and whether the product matches a normal goal.

Where an official page will not print a price, this guide will not invent one. Figures below were last lined up on 17 October 2026, and your own store can disagree the day you tap upgrade.

Hinge, if you want a date

Hinge sits at the top of that ranking with an 8.2, because it pushes you toward a date rather than an endless stack. The slogan is "designed to be deleted," and the profile is a set of prompts instead of one photo you judge in a second.

You like or comment on a specific answer, which gives the first message something to hold. Free accounts get a limited number of likes each day and can undo one skip a week.

Hinge+ adds unlimited likes, every incoming like at once, unlimited undos and stronger filters. HingeX puts priority likes, a sharper recommendation pass and an always-on boost on top of that.

Plans come weekly, monthly, three months or six months, and the price depends on location. How many profiles you see also depends on your city and your filters, so a friend's screen is a poor quote.

Bumble, if the tools matter

Bumble scores 7.5 and is the pick when the safety tools and a free inbox matter more than prompt-writing. You can swipe, match and message without paying, and the listed tools include photo and ID checks, blurring of explicit photos, in-app calls, and block and report.

The old women-first rule ended in August 2026. Either person can send the opening message now.

The clock stayed. Both people have about 72 hours to send that first line, and once someone sends it the other has about 72 hours to reply.

A free account can extend one match a day. Leave the match quiet and it still disappears.

Photo verification for US members, and the way a Boost is sold, are in our Bumble review. Open that before you pay for a lift you have not seen a price for.

Boost is unlimited swipes, five SuperSwipes and one Spotlight a week, plus a way to undo a swipe and to reopen an expired match. Premium adds the people who already liked you, finer filters, an incognito mode and travel mode.

Premium+ adds photo insights, priority visibility and two Notes a week, and it raises the weekly extras to ten SuperSwipes and two Spotlights. Bumble does not publish one price, so the number in your app is the only one that applies.

The ranking site's Bumble review grades the same trade from the other desk. Read it next to ours if you are weighing the safety tools against the paywall.

Tinder, if you want the crowd

Tinder is the other 7.5, scored for reach rather than manners. The ranking site's Tinder review is where that reach gets graded, including the claim of more than 185 countries and over 100 billion matches since 2012.

The free app lets you swipe, match and chat, which is why it is the easy first download in a new city. It is also where a photo-first stack turns into fatigue.

Tinder Plus adds unlimited likes, unlimited rewinds, Passport and Incognito, and it is meant to hide ads, though some subscribers still see them. Gold adds See Who Likes You, weekly Super Likes, and usually one Boost a month, with weekly plans and some offers skipping that Boost.

Platinum adds prioritized likes on top of Gold. The price changes with the region and the account, so a screenshot from last spring is already stale.

Our Tinder review stays on that paywall and on what the free tier already does. A friend's Gold price is a rumor until your own store shows a figure.

Whether the middle tier earns its fee is the point of this is Tinder Gold worth it note. It pays off when you would rather see the people who already liked you, and it is a weak buy if the free app has not produced a conversation.

A separate set of Tinder reviews is the mood check from outside either write-up. The quote on your phone is still the quote.

Apps that are not a swipe stack

Feeld scores 7.8 for people who are already talking about non-monogamy. Couples can link profiles, and Desires are where you list kinks and the relationship style you actually mean.

The free plan lets you connect and message. Majestic shows who liked you, removes the like cap, adds filters, and includes an Incognito mode where only people you like, or Ping, can find you in Discover.

Majestic is sold monthly, for 90 days or yearly, and the price follows your location. A linked profile makes the setup visible earlier, and it still does not replace a conversation about boundaries.

OkCupid scores 7.3 and takes the long way around. Optional questions build a match percentage, and when you have both answered in public you can see a clash on kids or pets before you say hello.

Free accounts can send likes and intros, see matches and chat with them. Premium shows everyone who liked you, opens the public answers and includes three SuperLikes a week.

Premium Plus adds priority likes, read receipts, fifteen SuperLikes a week in total, and a monthly Rush Hour boost. Incognito is a separate subscription, not a switch inside the other plans.

The gender and orientation options are why it keeps coming up for queer daters. The questions are why it suits people over 40 and over 50 who are tired of guessing from a photo.

Coffee Meets Bagel scores 7.0 by refusing the infinite stack. Around noon you get a small batch of suggestions, called bagels, which suits someone who would rather read five profiles than swipe for an hour.

The free plan is that batch, likes, chat once you match, and basic age and distance. Premium shows who liked you and adds extra bagels, eight flowers a month, tighter preferences, a monthly boost, activity reports and read receipts.

Platinum adds priority likes, an always-on boost and incognito. The cost of a strict filter is an empty day, because the batch can run out.

Facebook Dating scores 6.8 and is the only major option here with no paid plan at all. It lives inside the Facebook app on Android and iPhone, and you need to be 18, in a supported country, with an account at least 30 days old.

Suggestions lean on your profile, your groups and your events. A crush sent to a friend, or to a friend of a friend, is visible to that person, so the quiet like is not quiet.

The price of zero is more of your life inside Meta. Some people will not pay that even when the dating itself costs nothing.

Seeking and the adult boards

Seeking scores 7.0, and it is not the sugar site the old name suggests. The rebrand away from SeekingArrangement finished in 2022, and the current rules ban sugar dating and every transactional relationship.

The ban covers allowance talk, paid dates and the sugar-daddy phrasing, and a profile that breaks it can be refused or suspended. You can join and browse free, and you pay when you want messaging and real visibility.

There is no iPhone app at the moment, so iPhone users get the website. Platinum is 150 US dollars for 30 days, or 380 for 90 days billed up front, those prices in place since January 2026.

Diamond is 375 dollars for 30 days and is sold as up to twelve times the visibility. Platinum includes unlimited messaging, three times the visibility, more than one location and stronger filters, and a Gold tier exists with no published price.

Read the Seeking review before you treat it as a yellow swipe app in a nicer suit. The fee and the rules belong to a luxury dating product, and the old name is how people get this wrong.

If an allowance was the actual goal, you have left this list. Our sugar daddy apps guide is the page for that lane, and it is a different risk from a coffee date.

Private Sugar Club reviews are the narrower version, member fees instead of a city-sized stack. Compare the terms before the photos if a fee is the question you came in with.

Ashley Madison scores 6.0. Since February 2026 it has described itself as a discreet dating site for singles and for people in any relationship, not only the married-affair brand people remember from the 2015 breach.

You can join free, and members get daily winks and private-key requests. Messages need credits, which do not expire, but you lose them if you delete the account or stay inactive for 12 months.

New members can start with an introductory pack of 100 credits. The card line is meant to be discreet, though some banks print more than the company hoped, and Preferred, Prime and Priority Man are optional add-ons sold beside the credits.

If a partner has not agreed, that is an ethical problem you walked in with, not a feature. Credits also add up faster than a monthly subscription looks on a comparison chart.

Adult Friend Finder scores 5.5 and has been online since 1996, which makes it one of the older dating boards still charging people. Beside profiles it runs live video and adult chat rooms.

Free members mostly browse, and they can message Gold members who have allowed it. Gold is what unlocks ordinary messaging, it renews on its own, and the current price is whatever checkout shows today.

The Adult Friend Finder review is the longer look at that Gold gate. A dated design can still bill you cleanly, and a clean bill is not a reason to skip the renewal setting.

Habits that beat a badge

No verification badge is a background check. Do not send cash, gift cards or crypto to a match, meet in a public place, and tell a friend where you are going.

A reverse image search catches some stolen photos and misses a real person with bad intentions. Report inside the app when someone crosses a line, and stop swiping when it starts to feel like a chore.

If you are on the web versions, keep the login in its own browser profile. Browser hygiene for private adult browsing is the setup, and it applies to Seeking and Adult Friend Finder as much as to any other adult site.

What to open first

For a relationship, start with Hinge and add Bumble if you want the safety tools. For casual volume, start with Tinder, and for non-monogamy start with Feeld.

For a slower batch, Coffee Meets Bagel or OkCupid will waste less of an evening than a giant stack. Facebook Dating is the one that costs nothing, Seeking is the luxury filter that bans allowances, and Ashley Madison and Adult Friend Finder are the discreet and adults-only boards.

Use the free plan for a week or two before you pay anyone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, so cancel in the store or on the site that actually billed you.

Verdict

For most adults who want an actual date, Hinge is the first download, Bumble is the safety-minded second, and Tinder is what you add when the city feels empty. Pay only after that free week, and only after the price on your own account is a number you accept.

The niche apps are better when you already know you want non-monogamy, a curated batch, a luxury room or an adults-only board. They are worse when you install them because a score told you to.

Prices and rules were taken from public pages and can change without this article noticing. This is not a reason to send money to a stranger.

FAQ

What is the best dating app in 2026?

Hinge is the best starting point if you want a relationship, because the prompts give you a real thing to answer. Add Bumble for the safety tools, or Tinder if you mainly want a large nearby pool.

Which dating apps are actually free?

Bumble, Hinge, Feeld, OkCupid and Tinder let you match and chat on the free tier. Facebook Dating has no paid plan, while Seeking, Adult Friend Finder and Ashley Madison charge before most messages.

Do women still have to message first on Bumble?

No. Since August 2026 either person can open the chat, with about 72 hours to send that first line and about 72 hours for the other person to answer.

Is Tinder Gold worth it?

It is worth paying for if you want to see who already liked you instead of swiping through everyone. It is a weak buy if the free app has not produced a conversation you liked.

Does Seeking allow sugar dating?

No. Seeking bans sugar dating, allowance talk and paid dates, and it can suspend a profile that breaks the rule.

Are dating apps safe?

No app can promise that. Use the verification tools, meet in public, and do not send money to someone you have only matched.
