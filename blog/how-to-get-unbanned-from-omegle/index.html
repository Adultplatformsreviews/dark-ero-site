The original ban expired with the site

Omegle permanently shut down on 8 November 2023. An old ban from that service no longer has a server to enforce it, and it also has nobody who can send you an unban email.

As of early October 2026, omegle.com was showing a launching-soon page. The terms on that project name a company called RC Tech America Inc. and describe an adults-only service with accounts, which is not the old Omegle team coming back.

OmeTV is a separate product, and its own FAQ says it is not the new Omegle. A ban there belongs to OmeTV, and writing to a dead Omegle address will not touch it.

Work out who banned you

The ban screen is from the site you were actually on, which might be OmeTV, Chatroulette, a newer clone, or the new project at the old domain. A ban on one does not normally follow you to another, and no shared Omegle authority sits over all of them.

Read that site's rules before you appeal. OmeTV, for example, says moderators look at a report together with a screenshot, and that repeated complaints can trigger an automatic ban.

If a support address exists, use it, with the date and a calm account of what happened. Many of these sites do not promise a formal appeal, and some say they do not have to tell you why you were banned.

Wait, or move on

People commonly report short bans, the kind that follow a single report or a lot of skipping, clearing in about 7 to 14 days. Nobody publishes an official timer, so the only check is to try again every couple of days.

Trying every hour does not speed that up. A serious violation can be permanent, and no blog can see the flag on your account.

When a short ban ends, the same behaviour usually earns another one. Keep your face in frame where the rules require it, stay within the clothed areas if that is the rule, and report a rude stranger instead of answering in kind.

If the ban is long, use a different adult site and follow its rules from the first minute. That is the legitimate fix, and it is also the boring one.

Unban tools are the scam

Software on your phone cannot edit a ban that lives on the chat site's servers. An APK, a cracked app, or a paid unban service is asking for your device or your money, and it cannot do the thing in the advert.

FAQ

Can I get my old Omegle account back?

There is nothing to restore. The service shut down on 8 November 2023 and has not reopened.

A launching-soon page at the old domain is a different project.

How long do clone bans last?

Users often describe anything from about a week to a few months, and the sites rarely publish an official length. Serious violations can be permanent, and retrying all day does not shorten a timer.

Will a VPN unban me?

No. A VPN does not edit the site's ban record, and using one to dodge a ban can break the terms.

Use a support form, or use a different site.

Are unban APKs safe?

No. They cannot change a ban stored by the chat site, and they are a common way to pick up malware.

Delete the offer, and do not grant it permissions.

Does an OmeTV ban mean I am banned everywhere?

No. OmeTV is a separate company, and a ban there stays there.

Each clone keeps its own list.
