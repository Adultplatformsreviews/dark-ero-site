What counts as a figure

A number earns a place here when it traces to the creator's own post, a named interview, a statement from OnlyFans, or a filing. Lists that copy one another, and monthly totals with no original source, stay off the page.

Many large accounts never post their numbers, so the true top of the platform in 2026 may be invisible. Agreement between blogs is not verification.

The statements people can actually find

Sophie Rain opened her account in May 2023. In January 2026 a screen recording of her statistics page was read as about $101.2 million all-time gross, and the May 2026 screenshot moved that self-shown lifetime gross to about $110 million.

She has also described a 2024 total around $43 million as counted before the 20 percent commission. Those are dashboard figures she showed, not an audited net, and she has said the income is not a reality for most creators.

Bhad Bhabie posted the most complete statement in the set. A July 2024 story showed $71,332,786.34 gross and $57,060,758.48 net from April 2021 to 11 July 2024, and the net is 80 percent of the gross.

Paid messages were the largest slice of that net, ahead of subscriptions, with tips much smaller. OnlyFans confirmed the launch itself, telling a reporter she had passed $1 million in her first six hours.

Amouranth posted a statement in January 2024 showing $57,058,995.18 gross and $45,651,323.47 net from the start of 2020 to 3 January 2024. Messages, subscriptions and tips all showed up, and the net is again 80 percent of the gross.

Bella Thorne's early record was a launch, not a salary. OnlyFans told Variety she earned more than $1 million in her first 24 hours in August 2020, and she told the Los Angeles Times she had made $2 million in under a week.

Launch weeks show how many fans arrived at once. They do not tell you what the account earned the month after.

Gross, net and a normal month

Gross is what fans paid, and OnlyFans keeps 20 percent under its terms. Net on the Bhad Bhabie and Amouranth statements matches that 80 percent, and net is still not personal profit once assistants, editors, agencies and tax are paid.

When a source does not say whether a figure is gross or net, the honest label is that it was not stated. Guessing a cost and subtracting it would invent a third number.

Fenix International's fiscal 2025 accounts, the year ended 30 November 2025, show about $6.2 billion paid to 5.063 million creator accounts. Divided through, that is a mean of about $102 a month after the fee, with inactive accounts sitting in the denominator.

In a 2026 statement reported by Variety, OnlyFans said 5,076 creators had earned more than $1 million since the 2016 launch. Set against 5.063 million accounts, that is about 1 in 1,000, and it is a lifetime count rather than a monthly success rate.

Verdict

The public ceiling is a self-shown lifetime gross near $110 million, and the documented nets on two famous statements are 80 percent of gross. The company itself points at a few thousand people who have ever cleared $1 million, against millions of accounts.

Use those figures as the ceiling, not as a plan. Adults only, and an unsourced monthly salary is the number to drop.

FAQ

Who has the highest reported OnlyFans earnings?

On figures that can be traced, Sophie Rain has shown the largest total, about $110 million all-time gross in May 2026. It is self-shown, unaudited, and not a monthly rate.

Does OnlyFans publish a top earners list?

No, it does not publish a ranking or a full earnings file. Any list with dollar figures is a compilation of public claims.

What is the difference between gross and net?

Gross is what fans paid, and OnlyFans keeps 20 percent, so the creator payout is 80 percent. The Bhad Bhabie and Amouranth statements show that split, and payout is still before staff and tax.

How many creators have made $1 million?

OnlyFans has said 5,076 creators earned more than $1 million since 2016. That is a lifetime count, set against 5.063 million creator accounts in fiscal 2025.

Are celebrity monthly figures reliable?

Most of the famous monthly totals do not trace to a statement, the creator, or OnlyFans. A launch week and a lifetime gross are real kinds of numbers, and they are not a salary.

Do top earnings predict a new account?

No, the same filings that show billions in payouts also show a per-account mean near $102 a month once every account is counted. The ceiling and the middle are different facts.
