What pairs sell for

Most used panties sell for about $20 to $50 a pair. Sofia Gray's own ranges sit around $10 to $25 at the low end, $25 to $50 in the middle, and $50 and up for premium listings.

Snifffr's FAQ says most of its sellers price between $20 and $50 a pair. A day of wear is the ordinary listing, and two to seven days is where they say the premium starts.

Specific requests typically add $10 to $30 on Snifffr's figures. Lace and less common cuts tend to list higher, and a new seller is wiser at the bottom of the band until reviews exist.

On a flat membership, the useful sum is how many sales cover the fee. A quiet month still bills the plan, which is the trade for keeping the whole sale.

How a first week looks

Pick one verified shop, open it under a stage name, and list three to five items you can describe honestly. A new email, and a payment account that does not flash a legal name, belong in that setup.

Upload the ID the shop asks for, through the shop, not through a link in a message. Fuller profiles are what Sofia Gray credits when sellers charge more, so say what you offer and what you will not do.

Photos need soft light, a plain background, and nothing that identifies you. Leave faces, mail, tattoos you want kept private, mirrors and windows out of the frame.

Each listing should name the style, size, fabric, wear time, add-ons and shipping. Reply while the buyer is still on the page, and ask for a review after a clean sale so the next price can move.

Packing and postage

Seal the item in an airtight bag, then a plain padded mailer with no branding on the outside. The return line is a PO box or a mailbox service, under a stage name or initials.

Your home address does not go on the parcel. Pantydeal's own FAQ points sellers at a separate email and a PO box for that reason.

Tracking is what you hold when someone says the parcel never arrived. Keep the receipt and the screenshot, and describe the customs form as clothing, which is what it is.

International buyers can owe import duty, and Snifffr's terms put that cost on the buyer. If a destination is unclear, sell inside your own country until it is not.

Scams and boundaries

The ordinary cheat is pay-after-delivery, or a screenshot of a transfer that never hits your balance. The same pattern is spelled out in how to sell feet pics without getting scammed, and it applies to a parcel just as hard.

An overpayment that asks you to refund the difference is a reversal waiting to happen. Buyers who drag the chat onto a private app on the first message are leaving the verification behind.

Nobody legitimate needs you to pay them before you get paid. A request for a legal name, a workplace, or a face photo with no payment attached is a block and a report.

Meetups are not part of the job. Keep the chat on the shop, take the money first, and stop when a thread feels wrong.

Verdict

A worn-items sale works when the shop checks ages, you keep the payment, and the parcel cannot be walked back to your door. Membership fees are the real cost, because the commission line is often zero.

Which sites take a commission?

Should I ship before I am paid?

No, take the full payment before the parcel goes out. A screenshot of a transfer is not the same thing as money in an account you control.

Can I use my home address?

Does eBay allow used underwear?

eBay's used-clothing policy does not allow used underwear or socks. A listing there can be removed and can put the account at risk.
