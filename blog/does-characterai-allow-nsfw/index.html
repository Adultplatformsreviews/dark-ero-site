Does Character.AI Allow NSFW?

Character.AI does not have an adult mode, a paid switch, or a verified-18 exception. The ban is in the terms, and the bankruptcy sale has not published a change to it.

Dark-Ero editorialUpdated 2026-11-1018+

An age check restores ordinary chat. It does not turn the filter off.

The parent company filed Chapter 11 on 12 October 2026, and the content rules were unchanged.

What the rules actually say

The terms, last updated 27 August 2025, say you may not post content that is obscene or pornographic. The community guidelines, still up when checked in October 2026, prohibit illegal sexual content, pornographic content and nudity.

A help article dated 2 May 2023 says pornographic content will not be supported at any point in the future. A second note from the same day calls that stance final, and says asking for the filters to come off can get the account banned.

There is no toggle, no c.ai+ perk, and no verified-adult mode that lifts those lines. The models are built with the limits in them, which is why a clever prompt is a bad plan and a terms problem.

Romance that stays non-explicit is still welcome. Flirting, a slow story and a dramatic plot are inside the rules, and a scene that turns sexually explicit is outside them.

The same pages ban sexual content involving minors, sexual harassment, and real people used by name or likeness without permission. They also ban promoting self-harm, which is a safety rule and not a style note.

Age checks and the under-18 chat ban

Since late 2025, accounts verified as under 18 sit in a separate experience. Character chat is off, and reading mode is what remains, along with things like Feed and comics.

The company said on 29 October 2025 that open chat for under-18 users would be removed no later than 25 November that year. Teen chat was capped by the hour first, then the removal started with a US group on 24 November 2025.

Reading mode does not wipe history. A help page updated in July 2026 says past chats stay saved and viewable.

Every account goes through age assurance. The first pass is an estimate from login and activity signals, and a person who is flagged under 18 can contest it.

The contest is a selfie through Persona, and an ID upload is only the last step if the selfie is not enough. Character.AI's data help page, updated 14 July 2026, says Persona deletes the ID and the selfie data within seven days, and that Character.AI never receives those files.

Passing the check puts you back in ordinary chat. It does not change the pornography ban, so a verified adult is still inside it.

Apple's US listing and Google Play both show an 18+ rating, checked in October 2026. A store rating says who the app is aimed at, and it does not override the company's own content rules.

The terms themselves set a lower floor for joining: 13, or 16 in the UK and the European Economic Area. That floor is about who may open an account, not about what the account is allowed to say.

The Chapter 11 sale does not edit the filter

Character Technologies filed for Chapter 11 in Delaware on 12 October 2026, with Disney as the opening bidder on a technology license. The app was still running after the 30 October hearing, and the sale announcements do not mention content rules.

What that filing does to the product, the chats and the dates is in our Character.AI bankruptcy page. Until Character.AI's own pages change, the ban stands.

The court dates and the Disney exclusions are also in this Character.AI bankruptcy write-up. A new owner could rewrite a policy later, and nobody has done that yet.

Verdict

Character.AI does not allow NSFW content, and it has said it will not. Stay if you want non-explicit stories, and leave if you want pornographic chat.

Do not hunt for a bypass, and do not treat the bankruptcy as a secret policy update. Pick an adult product on purpose, then read what it keeps.

FAQ

Is there an NSFW toggle if I verify my age?

No. The age check restores ordinary chat for adults who were flagged as teens.

The pornography and nudity rules still apply.

Does c.ai+ unlock adult content?

No. A paid plan does not add a content setting the free plan lacks.

The help pages describe the ban as applying across the product.

Can under-18 users chat with characters?

Accounts verified as under 18 are placed in a reading experience, and character chat is not available. Past chats remain viewable, according to the July 2026 help page.

Will a new owner allow NSFW?

The sale papers published through 1 November 2026 do not mention the content rules. Until Character.AI edits its own terms, the ban is the policy.

What happens if I try to dodge the filter?

The guidelines treat attempts to bypass safety systems as a violation. The published consequence can be a warning, a suspension, or a permanent ban.
