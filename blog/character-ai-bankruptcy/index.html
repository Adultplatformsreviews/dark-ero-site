Is the app still open?

On 12 October 2026, Character Technologies, Inc. filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition in the US Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware. The case number is 26-11603.

Chapter 11 lets a company keep operating while a court watches a sale or a plan to deal with debts. The same day, Character.AI said the service was still up and that paid plans stayed active.

The company also said the quieter part out loud. If a bid that keeps the app running does not show up, the platform will be wound down.

It pointed to legal, regulatory and operational pressure, and to cash running low. The sale was the company's estimate for late November or early December 2026, which is a hope, not a date the court has locked forever.

What the Disney bid actually buys

Disney Worldwide Services is the opening bidder, the offer everyone else is invited to beat. The number on that offer is 20 million dollars in cash, and part of it was paid before the filing.

The deal on the table is a permanent, worldwide license to some of the technology, and the license is not exclusive. Character.AI could still license the same technology to someone else.

Read the exclusions before you treat this as a rescue of the website. The draft leaves out the consumer app, the accounts, the brand, the chats people typed, and the outputs the model sent back.

A term sheet in the same papers says Disney will not acquire or operate the consumer platform. A license for engineers is a different object from the chat window you open at night.

On 30 October 2026 the court approved the bidding rules and confirmed Disney as that opening bidder. The judge did not appoint a privacy ombudsman, and did encourage the company to hire a privacy expert.

Dates worth putting on a calendar

Under the rules approved on 30 October, the bid deadline is 20 November 2026 at 5pm Eastern. An auction on 23 November happens only if another qualifying bid arrives, and a sale hearing was pencilled for around 24 November.

A group of states objected to the sale rules on 23 October and asked for more time. The court approved the procedures anyway, so those dates stand until a later filing moves them.

Attorneys general have also asked that any buyer keep safety and privacy protections, and that users get a chance to delete data before any sale of that data. That request is not the same thing as an order.

Chats, characters and the subscription

For now the company says its privacy policy still covers the account, and that its security practices are unchanged. Nothing in the Disney license hands your transcripts to Disney.

Other assets can still be offered to other bidders, and a later buyer could ask for user data. The filings reviewed for the 1 November update did not name a buyer for the chats.

Characters you built live on their servers. If the site closes and nobody takes the product, those characters can simply stop loading, and an export may not be enough to rebuild one somewhere else.

Copy the name, the definition and a few example lines into a file you control. That takes minutes and does not depend on a download link that expires.

Subscriptions were described as still active, with billing unchanged for the moment, and with a promise of notice if paid features change. The 12 October update did not set out refunds if the service is wound down, so a yearly renewal is worth a look before you let it charge again.

Verdict

Character.AI is open, and it is also for sale. Keep using it only if you can stand the chance that a buyer, or a shutdown, changes the terms.

Export what you care about, copy character notes by hand, and do not renew a long plan on autopilot. The docket can still move a date, so the case site is the last word.

FAQ

Is Character.AI shut down?

No. The company said on 12 October 2026 that the app was still running, and that status still held after the 30 October hearing.

A shutdown is what they warned would happen if no bid keeps the product going.

Did Disney buy Character.AI?

Disney is the opening bidder for a technology license, not the new owner of the website. The draft license leaves out the app, the accounts and the chats.

Will I get a refund if it closes?

The 12 October update did not publish a refund plan for a wind-down. Check the renewal date in the store that bills you before a yearly plan charges again.

Can I download my chats?

Yes, through the account export, and the help center says that file can take three to four weeks. The link in the email lasts seven days, so download it when it arrives.

Does the bankruptcy unlock adult content?

No. The sale papers do not change the content rules.

Verified adults are still inside the same ban on pornographic content and nudity.
