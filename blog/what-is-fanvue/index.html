How the shop works

Shift Holdings Ltd runs it from London, company number 12729677. Fanvue says it has more than 325,000 creators and over 17 million monthly active users, which is the company's own count, and the roster includes ordinary creators and fully AI personas.

You have to be at least 18, or older where local law says so. Creators verify a legal identity that is not shown on the profile, and anyone else in the content needs their own check and signed consent before the upload.

A stage name is allowed, and a face is optional. Fans pay a price the creator sets between $3.99 and $100, billed every 30 days, or they pay once for a post or a message, and a free trial becomes a paid subscription unless they cancel.

VAT, local tax and card fees can be added, and the breakdown is supposed to show before you pay. PayPal, Discover, Cash App and bank transfer are not the fan payment methods, while cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, crypto through the Fanvue wallet, and PIX in Brazil are.

The 80 percent and the waits

On the core platform the standard split is 80 percent to the creator and 20 percent to Fanvue, on subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view, bundles and custom content. Taxes and transaction fees added at checkout sit outside that split, and an older 85 percent rate for people who joined in 2022 has ended.

A creator charging $9.99 with 100 subscribers has about $999 of subscription sales before those extras, and at the standard rate keeps about $799 before their own tax. Fanvue's creator guide says that in the first two months the subscription was only about 10 percent of new creators' earnings, while paid messages were about 60 percent.

Earnings are paid before tax, so the return is yours. Withdrawals through TripleA carry a 1 percent fee, which Fanvue describes as the only payout provider it charges for, and an account with a dispute rate above 1.5 percent in 30 days can be charged $50 for each extra dispute.

Money usually moves from pending to available after 7 days, and the policy allows holds up to 28 days, with a help-center mention of 30 days when a fan has not verified an email. You withdraw from about $20, sometimes varying by region, after at least five pieces of content are up and a payout method in the same name as the ID has cleared.

The help center estimates 3 to 5 business days to send a payout, and the policy aims to start one within 10 business days. PayPal is not a payout method, and a new payout method can take up to 24 hours to clear a security check.

AI accounts and tools

Fanvue treats AI creators as a real category, and every one of them goes through the same identity checks as a human creator. Fully AI accounts, AI personas based on a real person, and humans who sometimes use AI all have to show the AI label, and a persona based on a real person also needs that person's verification and consent.

If a fan asks an AI account whether it is real, the creator has to say it is AI. Characters have to look clearly adult, and one verified identity can hold up to 15 AI creator accounts and 2 human ones, after at least one successful withdrawal and with no active compliance warning.

The company says most creators use at least one in-house tool, from message rewrites to a voice you set up. That is the product difference people are actually buying, once the fee is the same as the bigger brand.

FAQ

What is Fanvue?

Fanvue is a UK subscription platform where fans pay creators for posts, paid messages, tips and voice notes. Human creators and clearly labeled AI creators are both allowed.

How much does Fanvue take?

The standard fee is 20 percent, so creators keep 80 percent of qualifying sales. Checkout taxes and transaction fees sit outside that split.

Is Fanvue legitimate?

It is owned by Shift Holdings Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales and based in London. That identifies the operator, and it does not remove billing or privacy risk.

Is there a Fanvue app?

There is no Fanvue app in the App Store or Google Play. Use the website, and add it to a phone home screen from the browser if you want an icon.

How much is a Fanvue subscription?

Each creator sets a price between $3.99 and $100 a month. Pay-per-view posts and messages cost extra, and tax or card fees may be added at checkout.

When can creators withdraw?

Earnings usually become available after 7 days, sometimes longer, and the floor is about $20. Fanvue estimates 3 to 5 business days to send the payout once it starts.

What shows on a bank statement?

Help says fanvue.com with no creator name, and the terms also allow FANVUE or SHIFT HOLDINGS LTD depending on the method and the country. Anyone who can see the statement can recognise the charge.
