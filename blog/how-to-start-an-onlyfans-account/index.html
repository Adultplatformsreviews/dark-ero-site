Who can open one

You have to be at least 18, and some countries set the age of adulthood higher, including 20 in places such as Japan. If you are under that age, this is not a workaround and there is nothing to set up.

There is no official app, so the signup is a browser tab you typed yourself. Any download that asks for the new password is the fake, and OFTV does not log into this account.

Email and stage name first

Create an inbox you will use only for this account, and put a second login step on that inbox. A work address ties the account to a job, and a shared family address ties it to a person who did not agree.

Choose the stage name before the form, not during it. It shows on posts, messages and search, and changing it after people know it is how you lose the audience you just built.

Search the name on the networks you plan to post on, and on OnlyFans itself. Easy to spell beats clever, and a name that is already a big account is a support headache.

What that name reveals to buyers is our can OnlyFans creators see your email page, read from the other side. They will see the username you pick, so do not pick your legal name.

Become a creator

A new account is a fan profile until you add payout details and pass creator verification. The path is in settings, under the option to become a creator, and the site walks the rest.

You will need a government photo ID, a selfie, and a selfie that shows the ID. The check is there to match the face to the document, and a creator account is not an anonymous account.

Expect the review to take a day or two, and longer when they are busy. Do not announce a launch date you cannot move.

Repeat selfie checks happen about every 45 days after you are approved. Fail one and the account can lock while a person looks at it, which is a reason to keep the original ID somewhere you can find it.

Bank, price, and the first posts

Payouts are a bank deposit or a wire, added under banking in settings. PayPal, Venmo and Cash App are not the payout rail, and some banks later refuse adult-industry credits, so have a backup bank in mind.

Creators keep 80 percent of what fans pay, and OnlyFans keeps 20 percent. The usual minimum withdrawal is 20 dollars, and new earnings wait about seven days before you can send them out.

A subscription can be free or from 4.99 to 50 dollars a month. A new page is easier to join near the low end, with paid messages and tips doing the extra work, and you can raise the price once people know the promise.

Do not post the entire catalogue on the day verification clears. Put up a clear profile, a few posts that show the niche, and a note about what a subscriber actually gets.

Starting with nobody watching

Top earners almost always arrive with an audience from somewhere else. You can start from zero, and it is slower, which is a fact and not a moral judgment.

Open the social accounts under the stage name before you charge, and spend a few weeks posting where your niche already gathers. The first subscribers are usually people who already liked the free version of you.

Talk to people. A page that never answers messages behaves like a folder, and folders do not get renewed.

Verdict

Use a fresh email, a stage name you searched, and the website you typed yourself. Pass the ID check, attach a bank, price the page so a stranger can say yes, and bring your own audience.

Skip any helper who wants the password in exchange for growth. The account is the asset, and handing it over is the fastest way to lose it.

FAQ

Is there an OnlyFans app for creators?

No. You sign up and manage the account in a browser.

OFTV is a separate free video app and does not log into your creator account.

What ID do I need?

A government photo ID, plus selfies the checker can match to that document. A creator account cannot skip this, including faceless pages.

How much should I charge at the start?

The allowed band is free, or 4.99 to 50 dollars a month. New pages usually start near the bottom and raise the price after the offer is clear.

When do I get paid?

Creators keep 80 percent, and earnings typically wait about seven days before a withdrawal. The usual minimum is 20 dollars, and the payout goes to a bank, not to PayPal.

Can I start with no followers?

Yes, and it is slower than starting with an audience somewhere else. Post in public under the stage name before you expect the subscription to move.
