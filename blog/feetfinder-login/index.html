When the page says no

Check the email spelling first, and remember that passwords are case sensitive. If the email fails, try the username, then refresh, then a private window.

Clear the cookies for the real site, or switch browser, before you assume the account is gone. The reset from the sign-in page is the fastest fix, and it only works for the email that is actually on the account.

Look in spam if the reset mail is missing, and pick a password you do not use for banking or for that inbox. A password manager will also refuse to fill the password on a lookalike host.

If none of that works, email hello@feetfinder.com with the username and a short note on what the page does. A screenshot of the message on screen helps, and the password does not.

Keeping the login

Ignore any message that asks you to confirm the password through a link. If you think someone else is in, reset immediately and then tell support.

FAQ

Where do buyers and sellers sign in?

On the same official sign-in page, with the email or username on the account and the password. There is no separate seller portal.

Is there a FeetFinder app?

No official phone app exists, so use the website in your mobile browser. Avoid any store listing that asks for the FeetFinder password.

How do I reset a FeetFinder password?

Use the forgot-password step on the official sign-in page and open the link sent to the account email. Check spam, and choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

What if the email will not work?

Try the username, a private window, and a different browser before you contact support. Then email hello@feetfinder.com with the username and what you see on screen.

What does the seller ID check ask for?

A government photo ID and a selfie in which you hold that ID, with the face and the text both readable. A buyer who asks you to send that ID to them is not the check.

Does support need my password?

No, the published support address is hello@feetfinder.com and a real reply will not ask for the password. Anyone who wants it is not support.
