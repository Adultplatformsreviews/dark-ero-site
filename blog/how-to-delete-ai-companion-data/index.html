Companion apps keep more than a username. They keep the chats, the characters you published, and often a subscription that bills from a different company than the one that hosts the bot.

App-by-app menus are listed in this how to delete AI companion data guide. What follows is the order, and the parts that still surprise people in 2026.

Disclosure: Dark-Ero is independent and has no deal with the companion apps named here. Adults 18+ only.

Do it in this order

Cancel the renewal where the money actually leaves, which is the App Store, Google Play, or the website that shows the charge. Deleting the phone app, or emailing support, is not that step.

Then export anything you still want, take your name off public characters, and only then delete the account. Doing it backwards is how people lose the archive and keep the bill.

Some apps give you a short window to change your mind after you press delete. Treat that window as a courtesy, not as a backup plan, and stay logged out once you mean it.

Character.AI, export and delete

You delete a Character.AI account from profile settings, and the help center says the deletion is permanent. The labels differ slightly on the website and in the mobile app, so follow the words you see.

On the website, the export sits under the profile, then profile settings, then account, then the data controls. The help center says the file can take three to four weeks, and the link in the email works for seven days.

We could not confirm a current official article for deleting one chat and leaving the account. If the button is not in front of you, do not trust a screenshot from last year, and use the full export instead.

The privacy policy says Character.AI keeps information for the time necessary for the purpose it was collected. It can keep material longer for legal duties, disputes and enforcing its agreements, which means delete is not a promise that every log vanishes on the hour.

Section 7 says that if a character you made was available to other users, Character.AI may keep it active after you delete the account. Unpublish it, and strip anything that identifies you, before you close the login.

The policy also says the company collects chat communications and uses them to run the service and train models. People in the UK or the EEA can turn off Improve the Model for Everyone, and that opt-out covers new content, not the history already used.

The company is in a court sale, which our Character.AI bankruptcy page tracks. Export while the account still works, because a wind-down is a bad day to request a file.

The hearing dates and what the opening bid leaves out are in this Character.AI bankruptcy note. User data is excluded from the Disney license, and a later bidder could still ask for it.

Verdict

Stop the charge, export the file, unpublish the public characters, then delete. Anything you skip in that list is the part that is still yours on someone else's server.

Character.AI's sale is a reason to do this in November 2026, while the download still works. Keep the export somewhere that is not the same inbox as the account.

FAQ

Does deleting the app cancel the subscription?

No. Cancel the renewal in the App Store, Google Play, or the website that shows the charge.

Then delete the account, or the bill can outlive the icon.

How long does a Character.AI export take?

The help center says three to four weeks, and the link works for seven days after the email arrives. Download it the day it lands.

Are my characters deleted with the account?

Not always. Character.AI says a character that was available to other users may stay active.

Unpublish it, and remove identifying details, before you close the account.

Can I delete one chat and keep the account?

There was no Character.AI help article we could rely on for that control in October 2026. Use the full export if you need a copy, and do not follow outdated button names.

Does the bankruptcy delete my data for me?

No. The app was still running after the 30 October hearing, and the company said its privacy practices were unchanged.

If you want the data gone, you still have to ask.
