What a creator sees

They see your username and the profile name you chose, plus the messages, comments and tips you send. The earnings screen can show that a username tipped or bought a paid message, and the username is the identifier.

They do not see your email through normal account tools. OnlyFans lists the email as data it holds, and the on-site mail system attaches messages to usernames.

They do not see your legal name unless you put it in the username, the profile, or the chat. Most fans are not asked for a legal name at all, though the company can ask for ID in some cases.

They do not see your full card number or the security code. OnlyFans says a payment company charges the card, and that OnlyFans receives a token, the card type, the expiry, and the first six and last four digits.

They do not see the list of other creators you subscribe to. Each creator sees the subscription to their own page, and the rest stays account data.

OnlyFans does not document a feature that shows a creator which fan opened a post, or how many times. The privacy policy does list engagement statistics as data the company holds, which is not proof those numbers are handed to the creator with your name attached.

Who else might read the chat

The contract between fans and creators says third parties may help a creator run the account and talk to fans. An assistant or an agency can read what you wrote, so write as if a small team might see it.

OnlyFans moderation can review messages too. That helps when someone is threatening you, and it means a diary you wanted sealed is not sealed.

What the bank still prints

Help says the statement line shows the brand name, the letters OF, or the brand name followed by .com. Anyone who can open that statement can guess the charge, and a VPN does not rewrite it.

The descriptor does not include the creator's name. Hiding from the creator and hiding from a partner who shares the bank login are two different jobs.

You cannot edit the words OnlyFans and its processor chose. What you can do is keep that card, or that account, where the wrong person cannot read the line.

Verdict

Creators see what you show them, under the name you picked. They do not get the email or the full card, and the bank line can still announce the charge to anyone who shares the account.

Keep the profile dull, assume a team might read the chat, and decide about the statement before you subscribe. That is the whole privacy model.

FAQ

Can a creator see my email?

No. The email stays with OnlyFans, and messages are attached to your username.

Do not type the address into the chat if you want that to remain true.

Can they see my real name?

Only if you put it in the username, the profile, or a message. The company does not hand creators a legal name as part of a normal subscription.

Can they see my card?

No. OnlyFans says it receives a token and partial digits, not the full number or the security code.

The creator is not in that payment file.

Can they see my other subscriptions?

No. Each creator sees your subscription to them.

The rest of the list is account data.

Does OnlyFans show on a background check?

A standard employment check looks at criminal records, courts, and, with consent, credit and job history. An OnlyFans subscription is not one of those databases, while the card statement is still visible to anyone with the bank login.
