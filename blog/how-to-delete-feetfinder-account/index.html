FAQ

How do I delete my FeetFinder account?

Sign in, open the Profile tab and press Delete. Subscriptions run until they expire, unpaid earnings are paid out, and then the account is removed.

Can I cancel the plan and keep the account?

FeetFinder has not published a separate cancel-only button. Email hello@feetfinder.com, name the plan, and ask them to confirm when it stops.

Do I lose unpaid earnings if I delete?

Unpaid earnings are paid out before the account is removed. You can also request a payout yourself once the balance reaches $30.

What is the payout minimum?

The published minimum is $30, and approved payouts are released within 3 to 5 business days. There is no daily or weekly cap on how many requests you make.

What does FeetFinder look like on a statement?

The line reads SEGPAY.COM*FeetFinder. A charge after you meant to leave can be a subscription still running to its end date.

Will support ask for my password to delete the account?

No, email hello@feetfinder.com and keep the password. Anyone who needs the password to close the account is not support.
