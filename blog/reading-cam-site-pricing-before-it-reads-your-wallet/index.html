Tokens, tips and private shows are designed to obscure the real cost. Here is how to see through it.

Cam sites are the most opaque pricing model in adult entertainment, and that opacity is deliberate. Converting money into tokens breaks the link between spending and cost, which is exactly why the model exists.

A few habits restore that link.

Do the token maths once

Before anything else, work out what one token costs in real money on the site you are on, and write it down. Every price on the site is now translatable.

A '200 token' private show is not an abstract number — it is a specific amount of your currency, and you should know which.

Know the three ways money leaves

Cam spending happens through tips in public rooms, per-minute private shows, and spy or group modes. Each has a different rate, and the per-minute ones are where costs run away, because the meter runs whether or not anything is happening.

Set a token budget before you load the site, buy that amount once, and don't refill in the moment. The single-purchase habit is the entire defence against a model built to make you refill without noticing.

Free rooms are genuinely free

Public rooms cost nothing to watch, and many sites run entirely watchable free tiers. If you are new to a platform, spend time there first — it tells you whether the site is worth spending on before you spend anything.

What we record

Where we can, cam entries note the real cost of a typical private session, not the token price, so you can compare platforms in money rather than in each site's invented currency.
