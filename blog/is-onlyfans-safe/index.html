What safe can mean

There is no single safety score that covers a fan with a shared bank login and a creator posting their face. A registered company, a card processor and an ID check can all be true while a statement, a leak or a fake login page still ruins your week.

The useful split is what OnlyFans says it does, and what it cannot do even when the settings are perfect. Screen recordings, a partner who reads the mail, and a text with a lookalike link all sit in the second pile.

Who runs OnlyFans

OnlyFans is operated by Fenix International Limited, UK company number 10354575, with a London registered office. Companies House lists it as active, incorporated on 1 September 2016.

Ofcom, the UK online-safety regulator, sits over that company. In March 2025 Ofcom fined Fenix 1.05 million pounds because the age-check information it had been given was wrong.

Fenix had said the facial-age challenge threshold was 23, when it had been set at 20 since November 2021. You still have to be 18 to use the site, so that number is an extra margin above the minimum, not the minimum itself.

The company reported the error itself, and settling cut the fine by 30 percent. Ofcom still criticised the delay of more than two weeks, and the weak checks on information sent to the regulator.

Cards, renewals and refunds

OnlyFans says a payment company processes the card, and that OnlyFans receives a token, the card type, the expiry, and the first six and last four digits. It says it does not receive the full card number or the security code.

Prices are shown and charged in US dollars, and your bank can add a conversion fee the site does not quote. Daily payment limits are set for you, and a wallet lets you load money once instead of tapping the card for every tip.

Subscriptions renew on their own. They stop when you turn auto-renewal off for that creator, when you were on a free trial, when the payment fails, or when the price goes up.

You cancel from the Following page. Access runs to the end of the period you already paid, and that period is still billed.

Closing the whole account is a separate job from unfollowing one creator. The clicks are in this how to delete your OnlyFans account guide, and the next statement is still worth reading after you think you are done.

Subscriptions, tips, streams, paid posts and paid messages are treated as non-refundable unless the terms were broken. The contract does not cancel consumer rights that local law makes mandatory, so a double charge or a purchase that never arrived is still a conversation with your bank.

A chargeback filed because you changed your mind is the move that gets accounts limited. If the charge was one you did not make, tell the card issuer quickly and keep the emails, especially on a debit card, where waiting costs you more of the protection.

Statements and what creators see

The statement is not discreet. Help says the line shows the brand name, the letters OF, or the brand name followed by .com, which is short and still obvious to anyone who sees the account.

If a partner, a parent or a bookkeeper reads your statements, decide that before you subscribe. A VPN does not rewrite a card descriptor.

What a creator sees is smaller than a conspiracy and larger than a blank username. This note on whether OnlyFans creators can see your email is the detailed pass, and the practical rule is that they see what you choose to send.

They see the profile name, the messages, the comments and the tips. Leave a legal name, an address and a phone number out of the profile and the chat, and do not use the site to set up a meeting.

Direct messages are not hidden from the platform either. The moderation rules say chats can be reviewed, which helps when someone is threatening you and does nothing for a diary you wanted sealed.

ID checks

Everyone has to be at least 18, and how hard that is proved depends on whether you post and on where you live. Fans may be asked for an email check, a short facial-age clip, or a full ID plus a selfie.

The privacy policy says facial age estimation is not used to recognise you later, and that this step is not face recognition. In the UK, porn services were given until July 2025 to put highly effective age checks in place under the Online Safety Act.

Creators hand over a much thicker file before they can post. The list includes a legal name, a verified email, a postal address, a date of birth, a government photo ID, a plain selfie, a selfie holding that ID, social handles, and bank or tax details where the law requires them.

There is no anonymous creator account hiding in the same product. If you cannot stand a passport scan sitting with a company, this is the wrong shop, however careful the username is.

Creator accounts are selfie-checked again on a rolling 45-day cycle. Fail one and the account locks while a person reviews it, and the content comes down from public view in the meantime.

OnlyFans says it keeps a copy of the creator's ID and the selfie with the ID. Face-matching data stays with the verification company, which OnlyFans says it cannot see, and OnlyFans gets the pass or fail plus the check details.

Some of that identity and tax material can be kept for the life of the account, and up to seven years where the tax rules say so. The privacy policy says the data is not sold, and it also lists record-keeping and repeat checks as reasons it is held, which is worth reading before you upload.

You can withdraw consent for the checker to keep face data by emailing the privacy contact named on the policy. That is a real request, not a toggle, and it does not delete records the law tells them to keep.

Start every check from an account you opened yourself, by typing the address or using a bookmark. A message that says the account dies unless you upload ID through a link is the ordinary fake.

A genuine handoff can open on a subdomain of the real site, including the ID host the company uses to reach its checker. A domain that merely contains the brand name, with some other ending, is the one to close.

Leaks, blocks and agencies

Fans can screen-record. The terms forbid copying, and a phone does not read the terms.

Privacy and Safety settings can stamp a text watermark, turn on video DRM, and hide a profile from chosen IP addresses, countries, or states and provinces in the US, Australia and Canada. DRM only covers video uploaded after you enable it, and a watermark can still be cropped.

A block stops that fan, not the next account they open, and a country hide does not stop a VPN. Treat both as friction, which is still worth switching on before the first post.

When a copy shows up on another site, the free path is the company's stolen-content report, which asks for your name and a way to reach you, plus a notice you or an agent send to the host. In the US, the Take It Down Act tells covered platforms to remove intimate images shared without consent, and known identical copies, within 48 hours of a valid request. The FTC's enforcement date for that duty is 19 May 2026, and it still does not reach every forum.

If someone threatens to post your images, do not pay and do not send another file to buy silence. Save the screenshots, block them, and report it to the platform and, in the US, to local police or the FBI.

A leak is also lost income for the person who made the work. Support creators directly, and why leak sites cost more than they seem is the buyer-side version of that fact.

OnlyFans says it does not work with or endorse agencies or managers. If someone wants your login in exchange for growth, that is your account leaving your hands, and the help pages suggest independent advice before you sign anything.

The platform also cannot stop a fan from filing a chargeback. A successful one is taken out of the creator's income, and the terms allow the creator's share of that payment to be deducted.

Logins and fake apps

Turn on two-step login in account settings, with an authenticator app or an SMS code, and use a password you do not use for email or banking. Give the account its own inbox, and put two-step login on that inbox too, because whoever can reset the mail can reset OnlyFans.

The login-session list is where a device you do not recognise shows up. If you think someone else is in, change the password, turn the second step on, sign the other sessions out, and check the recovery email and phone for forwarding you did not set.

A separate browser profile beats a clever username. Browser hygiene for private adult browsing is the setup, mostly so autofill does not drop a legal name into a chat box.

There is no OnlyFans app, so use the website and pin it to the phone if you want an icon. OFTV is a separate free video app with no nudity and no login to your account, which means any other download asking for the password is the fake.

Verdict

OnlyFans is safe in the narrow sense that decides whether this is a real company. Fenix is on the UK register, Ofcom has already fined it, cards run through a processor, and creators are checked hard.

It is not safe in the sense of invisible. The statement prints the brand, the ID file is kept, chats can be read by moderation, and a screen recording does not care about DRM.

Use it if you can live with your name on a bill and, if you post, a passport in a compliance folder. Turn on the second login step, ignore every app and every verification link, and do not pay a stranger to delete a leak.

This is general information from public help pages and regulator notes, not legal advice. Rules move, and the law where you live still applies.

FAQ

Is it safe to put a card on OnlyFans?

OnlyFans says a payment company charges the card, and that OnlyFans keeps a token plus the card type, expiry, and first six and last four digits, not the full number. If a charge was not yours, tell the bank quickly, because a debit card's protection shrinks when you wait.

Does OnlyFans show on a bank statement?

Yes. Help says the line shows the brand name, the letters OF, or the brand name followed by .com, and anyone who can see that statement can guess the charge.

Can a creator see my real name or email?

OnlyFans does not publish a full list of fan data a creator receives. They do see what you send, including the profile name, messages, comments and tips, so keep a legal name, address and phone number out of the chat.

What happens to a creator's ID?

OnlyFans says it keeps a copy of the ID and the selfie holding it. Face-matching data stays with the checker, and some identity and tax records can be kept for the life of the account and up to seven years.

Can I get a refund on OnlyFans?

Subscriptions, tips, streams, paid posts and paid messages are treated as non-refundable unless the terms were broken. A chargeback filed because you changed your mind can get the account limited.

Is there an OnlyFans app?

No. The site is the product, and OFTV is a separate free video app with no login to your account, so any other download that asks for the password is the fake.

Can OnlyFans content still leak?

Yes. The terms ban downloading, and a screen recording ignores the terms, while watermarks, video DRM and free takedown notices still leave copies behind.
