What Is CamSoda?

CamSoda is a live webcam site. Performers stream, viewers tip with tokens, and a public room can be watched without an account and without a payment.

Chat, tips, follows and private shows need a free registration and an email check.

The site does not publish a standing performer count. One evening snapshot of the public listing, 24 September 2026, showed about 1,674 rooms live and 110 in the voyeur category.

That is a moment, not an average, and it is already smaller than the largest free grids.

The Voyeur House

The house cams are the feature worth naming. They are 24-hour cameras in shared living spaces, so the picture is people cooking, sitting and talking, with intimate moments when they happen rather than when a tip menu says so.

Taste decides whether that is appealing. The product fact is that the format exists and many of those rooms are free to view.

Under a voyeur broadcast, the FAQ describes a playback timeline for the previous 24 hours. That is a recording of the room's day, not a private you bought.

Private-show recordings are separate, and only if the model turns them on. The prompt before you start is supposed to say so, in bold if you will not get one.

Token Packs and Private Rates

Everything paid runs on tokens, and the bigger bundle costs less per token. On the payments guide checked in October 2026, 50 tokens were $6.99, 100 were $10.99, and the far end was 3,100 tokens for $249.99, with bonus tokens on the larger packs.

Final totals move with country, VAT and exchange rates. You see the total at checkout, which is the number to trust.

Private shows are 6 to 120 tokens a minute, exclusive privates that block spy mode are 12 to 150, and spy itself is 6 to 90. Each performer picks inside those ranges, and some set a minimum length.

In a sample of 21 private rooms that same September evening, the median sat around 60 tokens a minute. At that median, fifteen minutes is about 900 tokens, which is a large-pack purchase, not a $6.99 experiment.

Premium is listed at $19.95 a month for an ad-free view, private messaging, anonymous tipping and 200 bonus tokens once. It only pays for itself if you are on the site often enough to use those extras.

A header offer of 100 tokens for referring a friend was up. A card-verification bonus was not on the current pages.

Billing Settings Worth Switching

Easy Re-ups buy tokens when the balance runs low. That is the setting behind a lot of the billing complaints, and the help pages say it can be switched off under Settings, then Settings and Privacy.

Do that before the first private, not after the statement arrives.

Some accounts also have Token Credit, which lets you spend up to a daily limit and be charged later. The limit lives under Settings, then Daily Spending Limit.

Know whether yours is on. Token purchases are generally final, with listed exceptions for duplicate billing, technical errors, unauthorised transactions and fraud disputes.

The statement will not necessarily say CamSoda. The safety guide says you will see securelive or the biller's name, and PayPal payments through Epoch have shown as EPOCH.COM *SECURELIVE.

Tokens are not transferred between accounts, so anyone selling cheap CamSoda tokens off the site is not selling a balance the site will honour.

CamSoda Pros and Cons

What Works

  • Public rooms, and many voyeur rooms, are free to watch.
  • The smallest pack is $6.99, so the first buy can stay small.
  • Private, exclusive and spy ranges are published, even though each model picks a rate.
  • Model checks ask for a government ID and a selfie with that ID.
  • A named U.S. company is on the 2257 statement.

What Does Not

  • Easy Re-ups can buy tokens without a fresh decision.
  • Token purchases are final apart from a short list of billing exceptions.
  • The roster is smaller than Chaturbate or Stripchat.
  • There is no store app, and viewer support has no phone line.
  • A median private gets expensive across a quarter of an hour.

CamSoda Verdict

CamSoda earns a look because the voyeur house is a real format, the door is free, and $6.99 is enough to learn the token without funding a long private. The operator is named, model ID checks are described, and the rate ranges are public even though each room picks its own number.

The score is 7.0 out of 10. Easy Re-ups, final-sale tokens and a private that can run to a couple of dollars a minute are the reasons it does not sit higher.

Recommended if you watch the house cams first, buy the small pack, and turn automatic re-ups off before you go private.

CamSoda FAQ

Is CamSoda free?

Public shows are free, and the voyeur page says many house rooms are free to view. Tipping, private shows, spy and recorded clips need tokens.

Who operates CamSoda?

The 2257 statement names Secure Live Media, LLC in Miami Beach, Florida, as custodian of records. The help center says CamSoda operates under U.S. law.

How much is a CamSoda token?

The payments guide lists 50 tokens for $6.99 at the small end. Larger packs cost less per token, and the checkout adds tax and currency conversion.

What is Easy Re-ups?

It buys tokens automatically when the balance runs low. The site's own guide says you can switch it off under Settings, then Settings and Privacy.

Are CamSoda tokens refundable?

The terms treat credit purchases as final sale, including if an account is closed. The payments guide lists exceptions for duplicate billing, technical errors, unauthorised charges and fraud disputes.

Is there a CamSoda app?

Not in the App Store or Google Play. The product is a browser site you can pin, which is a web app rather than a store download.
