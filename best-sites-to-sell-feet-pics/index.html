Marketplace or subscription app: which model fits you

The ten platforms above fall into two camps. Feet marketplaces such as FeetFinder, Fun With Feet, FootLove and Footly sell you access to buyers who are already browsing, and they charge for that access through a plan fee.

Subscription apps such as Fansly, OnlyFans and Fanvue flip the deal. Joining is free and they only earn when you do, but they expect you to bring the audience from social media, forums or an existing fan base.

Neither model is better in every case. A marketplace is a shortcut to your first buyers, while a subscription app rewards consistency once people already know your work.

Hybrid sites like OnlyFeet and FetishFinder sit in between, mixing marketplace browsing with recurring subscriptions. They suit sellers who want monthly income without giving up discovery entirely.

  • Pick a marketplace if: you have no followers and want sales within weeks.
  • Pick a subscription app if: you already post publicly and can drive your own traffic.
  • Pick a hybrid if: you want both repeat subscribers and new-buyer discovery.

Red flags in a platform itself

Scams do not only come from buyers. Some sites that promise easy feet pic money are thin shells built to collect uploads, card details or sign-up fees.

Be wary of any platform that hides its operator, publishes no seller terms or refuses to say how payouts work. A real business names a company, a payment processor and a withdrawal method.

Another warning sign is a site that asks for payment through crypto only, or pushes you to move chats to an outside app. Every platform in our top ten keeps billing in-house.

Finally, check how long the site has existed and whether sellers report real withdrawals. A brand-new platform is not automatically bad, but it has to earn trust through clear terms rather than big promises.

Staying safe and avoiding scams

The classic scam is a buyer who wants content now and promises payment later. Another is a request to pay by gift card or through an app outside the platform.

Keep every sale inside the site's own checkout, where protections and records live. Walk away from anyone who pushes you off it.

Protect your identity with a separate email and creator name. Strip location data from files and keep tattoos, jewellery and room details out of frame.
