Red Flags That Should Pause Any Deal

Fraud scripts recycle the same tells. Spot one and you can halt without a long debate.

Agencies describe overpayment ploys where a stranger pays more than agreed and begs you to return the gap. The inbound funds later fail, and your outbound refund is the real loss.
