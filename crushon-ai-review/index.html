What Is CrushOn.ai?

CrushOn.ai is a character chat site where adults talk to bots other people made, or write their own. The company is TECHIEPIE LTD, with an address in Nicosia and a second address in Dover, Delaware.

The terms we read were last modified on 22 September 2026.

You sign in with Google, Discord, SubscribeStar or email, and you confirm you are 18. Adult tags stay hidden until you turn on an Unfiltered switch.

Some hidden material, the FAQ says, waits on reward tasks first.

The library is the product. Anime-styled companions and roleplay scenes dominate the popular end, and the quality runs from a carefully written persona to a two-line prompt.

Sorting by ratings helps. It does not remove the thin bots.

Credits, Plans and the Top of the Table

Message credits are the meter. On 2 October 2026 the public pricing page showed six rows.

Free was 100 credits, 8K memory and 200 saved messages per character. Standard was $5.99 a month or $58.88 a year, with 2,000 credits and 16K memory.

Premium was $14.99, with a regular $19.99 struck through, 6,000 credits and 16K. Luxe was $39.99 or $299.99 a year, with 20,000 credits.

Elite was $89.99 or $799.99 a year, with 55,000 credits and 24K memory. Imperial was $199.99 or $1,799.99 a year, with 125,000 credits and 24K.

Treat the struck-through Premium price as a promotion, not as a permanent discount. The older Deluxe name was marked retired, and Luxe carried the popular badge.

Prices were in US dollars as shown that day. The terms say the company has no duty to update pricing information, so the screen you pay on is the quote.

Free models are unlimited on every plan. Pro and Ultra models spend credits.

A separate Pro Models package at $39.99 a month removes the credit cost of Pro models, which is a second subscription sitting beside the membership. Do that math before you stack them.

You can buy extra credits, and paid plans are described as getting 5% to 25% more on those purchases. Coins from logins, character creation and invites convert one-for-one into bonus messages.

Diamonds are a different currency, used to tip creators or unlock paid albums. The pricing page offers a card and an "other payments" option.

The FAQ also names SubscribeStar and Paymentwall, plus the stores for app subscriptions. We could not confirm PayPal or crypto on the public pages that day.

Free chats are deleted after 7 days of inactivity. Paid plans keep history for as long as the membership lasts.

That split matters if you are testing the free tier and you expect the thread to be there next week.

Library Quality and the Unfiltered Switch

Creation is a text form: a description, sample lines and notes on how the character should behave. Your bots stay private unless you publish them, according to the FAQ.

Creators can receive Diamonds and, under the terms, sell image and video. The tools are less visual than a slider-based girlfriend builder.

People who like writing a persona will prefer that. Beginners often will not.

Public comments, including Trustpilot, split in a predictable way. People who left a filtered app praise the freedom.

People who expected polished, in-character replies describe repetition, lost context and the bot answering as if it were you. We are treating those as reports, not as a lab score.

The pattern is consistent enough to plan around.

A settings panel, described in the company's wiki, covers model choice, language, temperature, diversity and reply length. Custom instructions can reduce repetition.

Age Checks and the House Rules

Signup asks you to confirm you are at least 18 and that you accept the terms. We found no ID check or selfie check on the public pages, and no parental controls.

That is a self-declared gate. It is weaker than a product that sends you to a third-party age check.

The community guidelines ban characters who are minors or not clearly over 18, including characters who were written as younger and then aged up. They also ban realistic images of real people, plus hate, harassment, self-harm and non-consensual material.

A loose filter is not a licence. Those lines are still the rules.

Support runs through Discord tickets and support email, with a FAQ for billing. Some public reviews call the support slow.

We did not open a ticket for this update, so treat that as a pattern in other people's reports, not as a timed test of our own.

Privacy and Training

The privacy policy, last modified 6 March 2025, says CrushOn may use content from character chats to train models. That is the line that should stop a diary.

The FAQ says chats and characters stay private unless you publish them, which means other users cannot see them. It does not mean the company will keep them out of training.

The company says it does not store your financial data. It does use Google Analytics and Tag Manager.

In the mobile app, third-party ad software may collect device IDs, IP addresses and data about ads you see. Personal data is kept for as long as the company considers reasonably necessary, with no fixed period printed.

A deleted account can be restored by logging in within 3 days, and after that the deletion is described as final.

FAQ

Is CrushOn.ai free?

Yes. The free plan includes 100 message credits a month, unlimited use of the free models and 8K memory.

Paid plans start at $5.99 a month for Standard.

What do CrushOn plans cost?

On 2 October 2026, Standard was $5.99 a month, Premium $14.99 with a higher price struck through, Luxe $39.99, Elite $89.99 and Imperial $199.99.

Yearly billing lowered the monthly equivalent. Read the live pricing page, because the terms say the company does not have to keep that page current.

Is CrushOn unfiltered?

It is looser than the mainstream companions, once you turn the Unfiltered switch on.

The guidelines still ban minors, characters who are not clearly adults, and realistic images of real people.

Does CrushOn train on chats?

It can. The privacy policy says content from character chats may be used to train models.

The FAQ says chats stay private unless you publish them, which answers a different question.

Is there a CrushOn app?

The FAQ says there are iPhone and Android apps, but on 2 October 2026 the store links led to not-found pages.

The official wiki offers an Android file, and the site works in a mobile browser.

Can I cancel CrushOn and delete the account?

Cancel under the subscription screen and you keep access until the period ends.

Fees are not refunded for unused time, except where the law requires it, and you cannot delete an account that still has a live subscription.
