An AI smut writer is any tool that will actually produce erotic prose, whether that is a one-prompt generator, a chat you steer, or a model on your own computer. This page ranks the same fourteen, from public pages we could open on 13 November 2026.

Three shelves, one order

The top of the list is a finished story from a prompt, the middle is a chat you take part in, and the bottom is free software you run yourself. A mainstream assistant that refuses the prompt stays off the list, because it does not do this job.

  • Story. A chapter you can read without playing a character.
  • Chat. A scene you write by replying.
  • Local. A model on your machine, with no monthly plan and no shared log.

1. SmutFinder: complete stories

SmutFinder writes chaptered erotic stories from a prompt, and the pricing page on 13 November 2026 showed packs of 200 credits for $4.99, 1,000 for $19.99, 3,250 for $49.99 and 11,250 for $149.99. Our SmutFinder review explains the six-month credit expiry and the training terms.

SpicyRanked's SmutFinder review covers the same packs. An outside page on Smutfinder is a shorter walkthrough.

2. NovelX: prose, chat and audio

NovelX covers a story, a chat and an audiobook in one subscription. On 13 November 2026 Premium was $18.99 a month, or $8.25 a month billed as $99 a year, with $129.99 struck through.

Our NovelX review flags the refund wording that does not match across pages. The NovelX review notes the same clash.

3. NovelAI: long fiction with a lorebook

NovelAI is the lorebook storyteller, with anime art attached, and it is a real setup rather than a single form. On 13 November 2026 the home page said subscriptions start at $10 a month.

It belongs near the top if you are writing a continuing world. It belongs lower if you wanted one scene and no tags to learn.

4. Sudowrite: a partner for a novel

Sudowrite is a general author's studio that some erotica writers use for structure, rewrite and a story bible. On 13 November 2026 the pricing page described three plans from $10 a month billed yearly or $19 a month billed monthly.

Confirm the current adult-content terms on the site before you paste a chapter. It is overkill for a single explicit scene.

5. AI Dungeon: turn-based fiction

AI Dungeon is interactive fiction you play a turn at a time, which some people use for erotic scenes and many people do not. Its pricing page did not show dollar plans in the HTML on 13 November 2026.

The safety settings have moved over the years, so check the mature toggle in the account you have now. A one-prompt studio will feel simpler if you only want a finished scene.

6. SpicyWriter: stories with a model choice

SpicyWriter is the story tool that took the slot left when Dreamily shut down, with a free basic tier and a pro unlock for stronger models. We did not find a public pro price on 13 November 2026, so none is quoted.

Point limits on the free tier are the thing to read on its about page before you plan a long chapter. Treat any third-party dollar figure as unverified until the company's own page shows it.

7. SpicyChat AI: community roleplay

SpicyChat AI is a chat library, not a story form, and it earns a place because a lot of erotic writing now happens as roleplay. The subscribe page did not show prices in the HTML on 13 November 2026.

Our SpicyChat AI review keeps the memory caps by plan. The SpicyChat AI review is the outside version.

8. Janitor AI: free narrative chat

Janitor AI is a free community of characters for narrative roleplay, and its site blocked this check on 13 November 2026. The best Janitor AI alternatives page is where to go when you want pictures or a cleaner editor.

Use it for a scene you play, and move up the list when you want a chapter you only read. Adult bots need the right tag, and some regions add an age check.

9. CrushOn AI: polished adult chat

CrushOn AI is a character chat with an explicit mode and voice, and it is a writer only in the sense that you build the scene by replying. The pricing page returned 403 on 13 November 2026.

Our CrushOn.ai review has the credit model and the training clause. SpicyRanked's CrushOn AI review covers the same app.

10. KoboldAI: local and self-hosted

KoboldAI is free open-source software for running a story model on your own machine, or in a light browser build. There is no subscription to quote, and the cost is the hardware and the model you choose.

It suits someone who does not want a company's log of the story. It does not suit someone who wanted a heat slider and a finished chapter in a minute.

11. Pygmalion: hosted chat and a local model

Pygmalion is both a hosted roleplay chat and a model people run locally. We did not find a clear public price list on 13 November 2026, so none is quoted.

The hosted version is the easy start, and the local version is the private one. Read whichever terms match the version you actually open.

12. SillyTavern: a front end for many models

SillyTavern is free software that sits in front of other models, local or rented by the API. The app itself has no plan, and the bill is whatever backend you connect.

It is the right tool if you already know which model you want. It is a project, not a website you can finish a scene on in one sitting.

13. Yodayo: anime chat, images and video

Yodayo, with the parallel Moescape brand, is an anime chat that also sells images and video through its own currencies. The buy page we opened on 13 November 2026 did not show pack amounts as plain figures, so no price is quoted.

Use it when the picture and the character are one product. Use a story tool above when the prose is the whole point.

14. PepHop AI: a smaller character chat

PepHop AI is a niche character chat with message bundles on paid tiers. Those dollar figures were not in the HTML we fetched on 13 November 2026.

Confirm the live bundle before you compare it with a free library like Janitor. A message cap is the whole product, so the number matters more than the artwork.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI smut writer in 2026?

For a finished story, start with SmutFinder, whose packs ran from $4.99 to $149.99 on 13 November 2026. For chat, use SpicyChat or Janitor, and for a private setup use KoboldAI or SillyTavern.

What can you use without paying?

SmutFinder has a free chapter, Janitor is a free chat library, and KoboldAI and SillyTavern are free software. The hosted chats still keep their own bans, and the local tools still have to follow the law.

Is a local model uncensored?

A local model can be less filtered than a website, and it is still bound by the law on minors and real-person sexual images. You also take on the hardware, the model file and the lack of a refund desk.

Which smut writer is which?

SmutFinder is the prompt-to-story tool, and SmutWriter is the workspace. AISmutWriter is a third product with its own review on this site.

What did the public pages charge?

On 13 November 2026 we could read SmutFinder's four packs, NovelX at $18.99 a month or $99 a year, NovelAI from $10 a month, and Sudowrite from $10 a month yearly or $19 monthly. The chat apps and PepHop did not show a dollar figure in the HTML we fetched.
