What SmutFinder still sells

On 11 November 2026 the pricing page still said pay as you go, with no recurring subscription. Starter was 200 credits for $4.99, Creator was 1,000 for $19.99, Studio was 3,250 for $49.99, and Mega was 11,250 for $149.99.

The credits page says packs stack with a daily allowance and sit in the wallet for six months. The page does not say how many credits a chapter costs, so the $4.99 pack is the only rate you can measure yourself.

The terms and the cancellation policy, both dated 4 February 2026, still mention subscriptions. Believe the checkout line, and our SmutFinder review has the longer note on training and refunds.

The home page describes stories of 5 to 40 chapters, roleplay chats with images, and a public library you can fork. Leave it when you want a monthly cap, lorebook co-writing, or a hard daily free limit.

RedQuill, the closest chapter machine

RedQuill takes a short prompt, plus characters, tone and a kink level, and writes a chapter you can keep extending. A community library lets other people remix stories that are published, which is the part to notice before you hit post.

Our RedQuill review records Basic at $13 a month, or $8.50 a month when you pay $102 for the year. Pro is $15 a month or $120 a year, and Premium is $27 a month or $214 a year.

Basic's published day is 80 Quills, which RedQuill describes as covering 16 chapters, 40 roleplay messages, 40 images and 1,000 words of audio. Unused Quills reset, and a refund request is allowed within 24 hours only if no credits have been used.

The free tier allows some stories each day, and RedQuill does not print the exact number. SpicyRanked's RedQuill review goes further into the remix rules.

NovelAI, the private co-writer

NovelAI writes with you. You steer each scene, and lorebooks hold people and places across a long story, which is a poor match if you wanted one click and a finished chapter.

Its subscription guide, read on 11 November 2026, prices Tablet at $10 a month, Scroll at $15 and Opus at $25. Tablet includes unlimited text and text-to-speech for the month, Scroll adds a larger context, and Opus remembers up to 28,672 tokens.

Plans renew 30 days after payment. A Paper trial lets you test text and image generation before that, and the privacy page says prompts are encrypted and that generated images are not kept on the server unless you save them.

NovelX, for chapters and a chat in one box

NovelX turns one prompt into chaptered fiction or a character chat, with Spicy and Explicit as the two heat levels. The company behind it is Sub Zero Labs OÜ in Tallinn.

The free start is 10 generation tokens. Premium is $18.99 a month with no token limit, and a yearly option has been shown at a discount of about 43 percent.

The terms, updated 30 March 2026, say your data is not used to train the models directly. Refunds are the messy part: the terms call them case by case, and the FAQ asks you to write within 30 days of the first month and leaves renewals out.

Our NovelX review scores that mismatch. SpicyRanked's NovelX review covers the same 10-token start.

AISmutWriter, for a free day of short scenes

AISmutWriter writes short custom scenes on its own models. You can try a scene without an account, and a signup adds 6 credits every day, which is enough to sample the prose and not enough to plan a serial.

Paid plans raise the daily allowance to 50, 220 or 1,500 credits under the names Casual, Creator and Infinity. Our AISmutWriter review records the annual prices checked on 16 October 2026 as $90, $250 and $1,500, and it rates reliability at 5 out of 10 after reports of lost stories.

Payments are described as final. Copy anything you want to keep before you close the tab.

SpicyWriter, when the model is the choice

SpicyWriter lets you write with personas such as Spicy, Pyrite, Indigo and ENI on top of models you choose, and it can build lorebooks from the story. The site still labels itself early access.

Basic accounts get 150 Lite points and 20 Balanced points a day. Pro adds unlimited Lite and Balanced use, with Advanced points capped at 150 a day and 2,000 a month, and we could not find a Pro price on the public pages.

One model route notes that Meta may use prompts and outputs for training. Check which model you have selected before you paste a scene you would not want reused.

SpicyChat AI, when you wanted a chat

SpicyChat is a character library, and the help center says the core chat stays free. It will not hand you a finished chapter from one prompt, which is why it sits outside the story ranking.

Paid plans rechecked on 26 October 2026 were Get a Taste at $5.95, True Supporter at $19.95 and I'm All In at $29.95 a month, after a rise dated 8 September 2026. Context runs from 4,096 tokens on the cheap plan to 8,192 on True Supporter and up to 32,768 on I'm All In, capped by the model.

Text-to-speech is the top plan, and it reads chat replies. Our SpicyChat AI review has the memory detail.

Kalon AI, for pictures beside the chat

Kalon is a companion with image generation, not a story generator. Its plan cards still show a free day of 50 coins at signup and 50 coins a day, monthly Premium at $19.97 with 16,000 coins, and a year at $119.64.

On 11 November 2026 the homepage banner still said chat, image and video generation were down until further notice, while the team works on a version 2. Do not pay while that notice is up, even though the cards remain on the page.

Our Kalon AI review explains the coin prices from the October cards, including 10 coins for an image. SpicyRanked's Kalon AI review covers the same pause risk if you are comparing notes.

A short verdict

Stay with SmutFinder if you like finished chapters and you would rather buy a pack than a month. Move to RedQuill for a daily allowance, NovelAI if the encryption matters, and NovelX if you bounce between a chapter and a chat.

AISmutWriter is the free short-scene test, with a save you should not trust. SpicyWriter is the model picker, SpicyChat is the free roleplay, and Kalon waits until generation is actually on.

Privacy, in plain language

SmutFinder's privacy policy says it does not use private chats or explicit content to train outside models. The terms still allow input and output to help train SmutFinder's own models, so the promise is narrower than it first sounds.

NovelAI's encryption claim is the strongest line in this set. RedQuill's policy, last updated 11 April 2024, is less specific about each story, and published RedQuill stories are remixable by default.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Smutfinder alternative feels most like it?

RedQuill. You type a short fantasy, pick tone and characters, and it writes chapters you can extend, with a remix library that behaves a lot like SmutFinder's fork.

Is there a free Smutfinder alternative?

AISmutWriter gives signed-up users 6 credits a day, and RedQuill lets free users create a limited number of stories daily. NovelX starts at 10 tokens, NovelAI has a Paper trial, and SpicyChat keeps core chat free.

Did SmutFinder become subscription only?

Not on the pricing page we opened on 11 November 2026. The four packs were still one-time charges from $4.99 to $149.99, and the February 2026 terms still mention auto-renewing plans, so read the checkout.

Which alternative fits a monthly plan?

RedQuill and NovelAI. RedQuill meters a daily Quill allowance, and NovelAI Tablet is $10 a month with unlimited text for that month.

Which one keeps stories most private?

NovelAI. Its docs say stories and prompts are encrypted, and that images are not stored on the server unless you save them.

What is the cheapest regular option?

SmutFinder's own $4.99 pack is the cheapest way to try a finished story, because nothing renews. For unlimited text on a plan, NovelAI Tablet at $10 a month is the clear published price, and RedQuill Basic is $8.50 a month only if you pay $102 up front.
