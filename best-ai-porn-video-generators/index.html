Companion clip or standalone clip

The first group makes a short video of a character you already chat with. The second group takes a prompt or a still and returns a clip.

Free video is rare, so a free chat plan is not counted as a free video plan.

1. Kalon AI: video of the character you chat with

Kalon turns a prompt or an existing image into a short clip of the same companion, paid from the coin balance that also buys stills. On 13 November 2026 the home page said all AI features, including video, were down until further notice.

Premium was still listed at $19.97 a month or $119.64 a year, shown as $9.97 a month. Our Kalon AI review notes that a standard video has been priced at 1,000 coins.

SpicyRanked's Kalon AI review covers the same coin math. Check that generation is actually running before you buy a year.

2. Candy AI: turn a chat image into a clip

Candy AI starts as a companion and can turn a suitable chat image into a video, with the media spending tokens. On 13 November 2026 the subscriptions page showed the 12-month plan at $3.99 a month, marked 70% off, against a $13.99 line.

Our Candy.ai review explains the token cap that all media shares. An outside Candy AI review is a second look at that plan.

SpicyRanked's Candy AI review tracks the same subscription page. A few clips can spend the month's media allowance, so the cheap year is a chat price first.

3. OurDream AI: coins per few seconds

OurDream AI puts video in the same studio as the character builder, and the help center prices the plan in DreamCoins. On 13 November 2026 that help center listed $19.99 a month or $119.88 a year, both with 1,000 DreamCoins a month.

Our OurDream AI review says free accounts can chat and cannot generate video. A long clip will burn the monthly coins quickly, so price a single video before you price the subscription.

4. Secrets AI: a private companion with video

Secrets AI presents itself as a private one-to-one companion, with chat, images, voice and video in the same session and no public feed. We did not confirm a current price or free plan on 13 November 2026.

It suits someone who wants the clip to stay inside a private chat. Read the privacy policy and the billing terms on the live site before you pay.

5. SoulGen: animate a still you already like

SoulGen's video path is image to video: you approve a still, then spend credits to make it move. The pricing page did not show a dollar figure in the HTML on 13 November 2026.

That order is useful, because you can reject the face before you pay for motion. Companion chat is the thin side of this product.

6. PornJourney AI: generation controls, not a partner

PornJourney AI presents itself as a generator you refine with pose and scene controls. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

Use it when you want to iterate on a look, and skip it when you wanted a character who also replies. Confirm whether video is included in the plan you are shown at checkout.

7. Seduced AI: extensions on a base generator

Seduced AI uses a base generator plus paid extensions aimed at particular styles, including animation. We did not confirm a current video price on 13 November 2026.

An extension can fit a narrow style, and several extensions can cost more than a general plan. Celebrity deepfakes are the kind of prompt that should close the attempt, not the kind to reword.

8. Promptchan: prompt control, video on the top plan

Promptchan gives direct prompt controls, and its upgrade page treats full-length video as part of the top tier. On 13 November 2026 Plus was $14.99, Premium $23.99 and Pro $33.99, each every 28 days.

Count the 28-day renewal before you compare it with a calendar-month plan. The interface is denser than a companion app, which is the point if you write prompts on purpose.

9. VirtualCrush: video inside a scenario

VirtualCrush builds the clip into an interactive scenario, so the video follows choices rather than a typed prompt. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

It suits someone who does not want to write prompts. It gives you less precise control of the final frame.

10. Nectar AI: a clip triggered by the chat

Nectar AI pairs a companion chat with images and short video that follow the conversation. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

The clip arrives inside the chat, which is convenient, and the clips are short. Read the live plan before you assume video is included with text.

11. Pornpen AI: an image tool adding video

Pornpen AI started as an image generator with iteration tools, and video is the newer layer. We did not confirm a current video price on 13 November 2026.

It makes sense if you already work in its image flow. Treat the video features as newer than the stills, and confirm they are switched on for your account.

12. Lusychat: the conversation drives the clip

Lusychat is a chat-led tool where the conversation triggers images and short clips. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

Setup is light, and the track record is shorter than the older generators. A short plan is the sensible first purchase if you try it.

13. MyBabes AI: a simple companion with short clips

MyBabes AI presents itself as a beginner companion with image and short video included in the package. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

The onboarding is the selling point, and the clips are short. Compare that simplicity with OurDream if you want a published coin cost per few seconds.

14. AI Hentai Generator: animated styles

AI Hentai Generator focuses on hentai and animated video rather than photoreal clips. We did not confirm a current price on 13 November 2026.

Pick it when that style is the whole request. It is a poor fit for a photoreal companion, and the chat layer is thin.

15. Free Spicy: prepaid credits, no subscription

Free Spicy is a standalone clip tool with no companion chat, and you pay with credits that do not expire. On 13 November 2026 its pricing guide said 1 credit equals $0.01, with no subscription.

The same page priced a 5-second Wan 3.0 clip at 480p as 125 credits, or $1.25, and a 10-second Seedance 2.5 clip at 720p as 1,650 credits, or $16.50. Crypto was the payment method listed in that guide, which is a reason to read the checkout before you buy a large pack.

What a free plan actually includes

Kalon's free plan has covered chat and images, and video has been the paid feature, which is moot while the service is down. OurDream's free accounts chat and do not generate video, and Candy's media, including video, spends the paid allowance.

Free Spicy's pricing guide confirmed the per-second credit price, which is a way to try a clip without a renewal. Secrets, SoulGen, PornJourney, Seduced, VirtualCrush, Nectar, Pornpen, Lusychat, MyBabes and the hentai tool did not show a free-video rule we could quote.

What no-limit usually means

Every serious platform still bans minors and sexual content of real people made without consent. The volume limit is the coin, token or credit, and a long clip is where that limit shows up.
