How we ordered them

A site ranked higher when its public pages say male escorts or male companions are the point, and when you can browse by city without fighting a feed of unrelated ads. Agencies and straight-male companion services stay on the list because the search still finds them, lower when the audience is different.

We did not treat a verification badge as proof of future conduct. A badge might mean a phone, a selfie, or an ID, and those are not the same check.

Male directories

1. MintBoys

MintBoys describes itself as a place to meet gay escorts and male masseurs, and it says escorts are paid for their time. City navigation and search are the interface, which matches the search term better than a general dating app.

A directory does not fact-check every line in every profile. Coverage is stronger in large cities than in small ones.

2. Tryst

Tryst is an independent escort directory with a male section, not a male-only site. It belongs here because those male landing pages are real, and you still have to filter by gender and city.

Judge the advertiser, not the logo at the top of the directory. Inventory changes with the city.

3. Rent.Men

Rent.Men is a dedicated male escort and gay massage directory with city search, profiles and reviews. In September 2026 its public homepage was already a grid of male profiles rather than a vague landing page, and that shape was still the product to judge.

International coverage is the advantage, and thin cities are the limit. Reviews are a signal, not a guarantee.

4. HUNQZ

HUNQZ opens on a location grid of nearby profiles for a gay, bi and trans audience, with a strong European footprint. The live grid is the product, which is more useful than a marketing page and still not the same as an independent ID check.

Rules and what an ad may offer change by country. Read the local listing instead of assuming one global policy.

5. Sleepyboy

Sleepyboy is a UK directory for gay, bisexual and transgender escorts, with location and age filters on the homepage. That focus is why it outranks a general classified for a British search.

It is the wrong tool if you need a global roster. Public listings can expose contact details, so treat what you see as advertising copy.

6. FriendBoy

FriendBoy is an international directory for rent-men, gay massage and independent male companions. The positioning is clear, and the brand is smaller than MintBoys or Rent.Men.

Smaller does not mean safer. Screen the person the same way you would on a larger board.

7. Jock2Go

Jock2Go calls itself a gay escorts finder, with filters for location, position, age, body type and ethnicity. The header is unusually plain about what the site is.

The niche is the point, and a filter is not a verification programme. Check each profile's own claims.

Companions booked by women

8. MaleEscortDirect

MaleEscortDirect focuses on straight male companions for women, and it describes a mix of directory and agency depending on local rules. It says advertisers are paid for time and companionship.

That is a real male-escort search, and it is a different audience from the gay directories above. Treat the marketing lines as the platform's claims.

9. Cowboys4Angels

Cowboys4Angels is a managed agency for women, with a curated roster and concierge language. Its public site emphasises non-sexual companionship, so it is not a substitute for Rent.Men or MintBoys.

The agency model means you are not browsing an open classified. Pricing and what is included will not match a directory ad.

10. The Male Escort Agency

The name says agency, and the public FAQ describes a directory where you contact companions yourself. Categories include straight, gay and bisexual male escorts, which is why it stays on the list.

Confirm each companion's terms before you send money. The brand and the booking path are not the same thing.

11. Gentlemen4Hire

Gentlemen4Hire is a UK company aimed at women, calling itself a straight male escort company while its service rules emphasise non-sexual social companionship. Read that twice if you arrived from a sexual-services search.

Agency support and a UK focus are the reasons it is here. It is not a gay directory.

Broader boards and local ads

12. EuropEscortGuide

EuropEscortGuide is here for European city and country coverage, not because it is male-only. Use it when you are comparing markets, then open the male filter.

An ad network is not proof that a listing is legitimate. The geography is the feature.

13. EscortTime

EscortTime is a city-first board for independent providers. Male ads are part of a mixed directory, so the value is local coverage rather than specialisation.

Do not treat the site's comments about competitors as independent evidence. Contact flows are direct, which also means the screening is yours.

14. Slixa

Slixa is an independent advertising platform with polished profiles and international city pages. Male availability depends on the city, so it is a secondary directory on this list.

It is not male-only. Open a city page before you assume the roster you want is there.

15. Eros

Eros is a long-running adult advertising directory organised by location. Male listings show up in some cities, and the site is an ad board rather than a managed agency.

Being accepted as an advertiser is not an independent background check. Browse by city, then read the actual ad.

16. Escorta

Escorta's public site is an Italian-language directory with city counts and a men category in the navigation. That filter is why it qualifies, and the homepage is still dominated by other listings.

Use the men category on purpose. It is a European board, not a male-only brand.

17. ListCrawler / ManUp

ListCrawler is a high-volume classified aggregator, and its menu includes a Man Up category for male ads. The rest of the menu is why it feels noisy.

Local US volume is the reason to look, and visible verification is thinner than on the specialist directories. Expect a broad adult-ad mix.

18. Bonepage

Bonepage shows up through regional male-escort category pages. Treat it as classifieds with a male section, not as an agency that screened a roster.

Listing claims need their own check. The format is local search, nothing more.

19. Manchester Lads

Manchester Lads is a regional directory for gay and bisexual male escorts and masseurs in north-west England, with navigation for Manchester, Lancashire, Liverpool and Leeds. The local focus is the entire point.

It will not help you in another country. The inventory is smaller than a global directory because it is supposed to be.

20. AdultWork

AdultWork is a large UK adult marketplace, and male escort listings are one category inside it. It sits last because finding that category takes longer than opening a male-only site.

The UK presence is why it remains relevant for local comparison. Breadth is not the same thing as a specialist directory.

Frequently asked questions

Which sites are actually male escort directories?

MintBoys, Rent.Men, HUNQZ, Sleepyboy, FriendBoy and Jock2Go match that description more closely than a mixed classified. The others are useful only after you filter.

Why is there no hourly price?

Directories do not set one rate for every city and every service. Advertisers and agencies publish their own numbers, and a single dollar figure would be fiction.

Is a verification badge enough?

It may only prove control of a phone, an email or a photo. Read what that site's badge claims to check.

Why is The Male Escort Agency called a directory?

The public FAQ describes browsing companions and contacting them directly. The word agency in the name is not the booking path.

Are dating sites included?

MenNation, in the research this list follows, presented as a gay dating site, so it is not ranked here. Dating apps are linked above as a different job.

Verdict

For a male directory, start with MintBoys or Rent.Men, then HUNQZ or Sleepyboy if Europe or the UK is the map. Women looking for a straight companion should read MaleEscortDirect, Cowboys4Angels or Gentlemen4Hire as a separate audience, especially where the site says the time is social rather than sexual.

Use Tryst, Eros, Slixa or AdultWork only with the male filter on, and treat ListCrawler and Bonepage as noisy classifieds. Keep records of any deposit, and do not confuse a directory logo with a checked person.
