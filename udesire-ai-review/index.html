What Is uDesire AI?

You pick or build a character, chat, add voice on a paid plan, and generate images. Paid plans add short AI videos.

The brand is written UDesireAI on the site. We judged the website.

We did not check an app-store listing, and we did not create an account.

The footer names UDevoAI Limited at Office 12421, 182-184 High Street North, East Ham, London. Business-data sites list a UK company of that name, number 16398400, incorporated on 22 April 2025.

We did not confirm the number at Companies House, so it stays an unverified listing. The terms use the law of England and Wales.

Founders and model providers go unnamed.

The home page offers Girlfriend, Boyfriend and Anime modes, plus roleplay, a dating-simulator page and private group chats. The image screen has menus for clothing, pose and scene, and it can render one to four images in a run.

Generation needs a login, so we did not see outputs. The site says the AI remembers your preferences, which we could not check.

People comparing cheap companion apps usually start from the best AI girlfriend apps. uDesire's pitch on that kind of list is the annual rate.

What uDesire AI Costs

Premium is $12.99 a month. The three-month plan is $9.99 a month, $29.97 in total.

The annual plan is $6.99 a month, $83.88 paid up front. Ultra Premium is $24.99 a month, $18.99 a month over three months ($56.97), or $13.99 a month billed yearly at $167.88.

The prices are in US dollars.

The Premium page strikes through $19.99 and $35.99 and marks the lower prices as limited time. The terms do not say what a renewal costs.

Read the amount at checkout, and read it again before the year rolls.

The homepage FAQ says premium plans start at $9.99 a month and mentions a free trial. The $9.99 figure matches the three-month plan.

What the pages actually show underneath is a free plan with no clock on it.

That free plan gives 10 messages a day, 1 image generation, standard companions and 1 custom companion. There are no voice credits.

The comparison table does not say whether the single image is per day or once per account.

Premium, on the feature list, adds unlimited messages, all premium companions, five custom companions, 1,500 daily voice credits and 5-second videos. Ultra adds unlimited custom companions, advanced models, 3,000 daily voice credits and 10-second videos.

Coins are a second currency that stretches generation past a monthly base. They are non-refundable, and the balance resets when the plan ends.

A price this low only makes sense beside the rest of the category. Our note on free versus premium, and what you are actually paying for is the general version of that comparison.

How Many Images You Actually Get

The Premium feature card says 100 images a month. The comparison table and the FAQ say 30 a day.

Data embedded for search engines says 150 a month. Ultra shows 250 a month in one place and unlimited daily in another.

We do not know which figure is billed.

Ask support in writing before you buy a year, and keep the answer. A testimonial that praises the lack of restrictions is marketing copy, and it is not a quota.

Cancellation and the 24-Hour Refund

Cancel in account settings. Access runs to the end of the period you paid.

Refunds are limited to requests made within 24 hours, by email, and the site may refuse if premium features were used heavily in that window. The terms say a fraudulent chargeback leads to a permanent ban, so write to support before you write to the card issuer.

Switching to a longer billing period charges the new rate at once. Moving to a shorter one waits until the next renewal.

Coins purchased along the way do not come back, and the coin balance resets when the subscription ends.

Privacy Claims That Disagree

The app collects account details, chats, generated media, character data, usage, payment and coin records, and technical data such as IP address and device type. Card numbers are handled by a processor the site does not name.

The homepage FAQ says conversations are encrypted in transit, stored securely, and never shared. The privacy policy says AI, cloud and payment companies may process the data under agreements, and it names none of them.

The terms say you keep ownership of your input and grant a licence to use it to operate and improve the service. The Safety Center says staff open chats for technical support or legal duties.

Those four sentences cannot all be the whole story. The policy is the one that mentions the providers.

Keep real names, addresses and card details out of the chat, which is also what the terms advise.

Retention lasts while the account is active. Deleted accounts are removed in line with the law, with some data kept for a limited period.

UK and EEA users are pointed at GDPR rights, including portability and a complaint to a regulator. The privacy policy is dated 10 June 2025.

The terms and the refund policy are dated 10 June 2026.
