What Chaturbate charges

You can watch a public show with no account. You need a free account to tip, and the token menu itself waits until you are logged in, where VAT can change the pack.

The help center values 200 tokens at $20.99. Wire transfers credit at $0.08 a token with a $250 minimum, Supporter membership is $19.95 a month, and broadcasters convert tokens at $0.05, so 1,000 tokens pay $50 before payout fees.

Tips are final, and Auto Refill can buy the last pack again. Our Chaturbate review has the recording default and the Sweden rule.

Stripchat

Stripchat is the closest public grid, and VR rooms are the feature Chaturbate does not match. Public watching is free, and tips, privates and spy spend tokens.

The help text tells you to open the green plus button for prices, and larger packs can wait on a purchase history. A wallet recorded on 12 September 2026 showed a smallest card pack of 45 tokens for $4.99, and our Stripchat review puts everyday packs around $0.08 to $0.11 a token.

Models are paid about $0.05 a token. The head-to-head with the yardstick is our Chaturbate vs Stripchat comparison.

SpicyRanked's Stripchat review covers the same token.

LiveJasmin

LiveJasmin is built around paid private chat, with free rooms as the shop window. On 3 October 2026 the public filter grouped private rates from 0.01 to 9.99 credits a minute and up, and each model sets the rate.

Credit-pack prices appear after signup. Our LiveJasmin review notes that SmartBuy can purchase a 27.99 credit package when the balance hits 7.

SpicyRanked's LiveJasmin review is the outside note.

CamSoda

CamSoda publishes more of its price list than most sites here, and it is the one people open for interactive toys and the voyeur house. Public rooms are free to watch, and tips, privates, spy and clips need tokens.

The payments guide lists 50 tokens for $6.99, then larger packs up to 3,100 tokens for $249.99, with bonuses that have run from about 5 percent to 27 percent. Private shows are commonly 6 to 120 tokens a minute, set by the performer, and our CamSoda review covers Easy Re-ups, which can buy tokens when the balance runs low.

BongaCams

BongaCams has the lowest published door price in this set. Guests can watch a public room, and the September 2026 prices page listed 46 tokens for $3.99, while our BongaCams review later recorded a USD example of 47 tokens for $3.99.

Read the live pack, because those two counts cannot both be current. Default show rates on the public page are 15 tokens a minute for spy, 30 for group, 60 for private and 90 for a full private, and performers can override them.

Auto Top-up can repeat a card purchase. SpicyRanked's BongaCams review walks through the same defaults.

Flirt4Free

Flirt4Free is a set of paid formats: private, Party Chat, voyeur, fan clubs and feature shows, including interactive toys. Live rooms ask for a verified email, so the guest path is shorter on Chaturbate.

A sign-up offer of 120 credits was on the form when those pages were checked, and offers like that change. Credit prices stay behind the login, and our Flirt4Free review records VIP at $39.95 a month on top of that hidden credit price.

MyFreeCams

MyFreeCams lets every visitor watch without an account, and its wiki says you are not nagged to register. A free account, with no card, is what you need before you chat.

Show prices are fixed across the site: 60 tokens a minute for a private, 80 for a TruePrivate, 20 for spy and 10 per viewer for a group show. Token packs start at 200 for $19.99, which is a high door compared with BongaCams, and the largest packs fall toward about 8 cents a token.

Cam4

Cam4 is the amateur and international grid, with rooms that feel less produced than the big two. Guest access and the token menu were not confirmed on a public page in the September check, so assume the price appears after login.

Model payout timing is on request, with a $50 minimum on most methods in the notes we could read. It belongs here if the smaller rooms are the point, and the missing public price list is the reason it sits mid-pack.

ImLive

ImLive is the group-show discount site in this set. Private chat on the published range runs from 0.98 to 9.80 credits a minute, and a group room splits that kind of rate across viewers.

Guest access was not confirmed. Enter only after you can see the per-minute number, because a credit is not a dollar until the pack price says so.

XLoveCam

XLoveCam is the multilingual browse: the interface and the model list are built for people who want the site in their own language. The brand says guests can look around, and token prices appear after login.

A VR option is listed, which is the feature to confirm in the room rather than in a marketing line. It is a reasonable swap when language, not the token math, is why Chaturbate felt wrong.

Streamate

Streamate shows the per-minute private rate before you enter the room. Gold maps to one unit of your currency, so 1 Gold is $1 if you are paying in dollars, and the performer sets the minute.

Public browsing is free. The clarity is the product, and the minute rate is still the performer's number, which you should read twice.

Cams.com

Cams.com sits on the FriendFinder network, which is the reason to open it if you already have an account there. Public price lists were not available in the September check, and guest viewing was not confirmed.

Treat it as a network convenience, not as a cheaper Chaturbate. If the price is still hidden after you log in, leave.

Cherry.tv

Cherry.tv is on the list because people search it beside Chaturbate, and the September check could not confirm that it loads in every region. Guest access, prices and VR were all unread for that reason.

Open it only to see whether the grid appears for you. A site you cannot load is not a backup plan.

SakuraLive

SakuraLive is the Japanese-performer cam site in this set. Login is required, and a public token price was not on the pages that were read.

Choose it for that catalog, then read the rate inside the account before you tip. It is a narrow swap, which is why it sits near the end.

RoyalCams

RoyalCams looks and prices like BongaCams. The September prices page listed the same style of pack, from 46 tokens for $3.99, and the same default private rate of 60 tokens a minute.

The terms read almost word for word like BongaCams, which points at a shared platform wearing another name. About 2,680 performers were online at that check, so the crowd is the thing to compare, not a fantasy of a different token.

Jerkmate

Jerkmate ranks performers against preferences you set, which is the guided version of a public grid. The FAQ states that 1 Gold equals $1, and private or exclusive minutes are priced by the performer.

The sign-up flow asks for a card before you have seen a full public price list. Our Jerkmate review is the page to read before that card field, and the performers overlap Streamate.

Two that are not public grids

These two solve a different job, so they are not scored against the 15. One books a private call, and the other runs studio shows on a schedule.

SkyPrivate

SkyPrivate is a pay-per-minute booking layer. You pick a performer and pay for a one-to-one call on Teams, Discord, Telegram or its own SP Chat app, and it says it is not affiliated with those apps.

The published model share starts at 70 percent, and a referral bonus can take that toward 90 percent for providers who joined since 25 August 2025. Providers are told to operate through a legal entity, calls use the usernames of those other apps, and a new-member free call has been offered, so use an account that does not show your real name.

Babestation

Babestation comes from British late-night television, on air since 2002 by the site's account, and it streams free studio cams around the clock plus a wider "World Cams" list. A schedule tells you when featured performers are on, which is a different rhythm from Chaturbate's always-open rooms.

VIP memberships in Silver, Gold and Diamond renew until you cancel, and the public pages do not print those prices. Cellcast UK Ltd runs the site.

A short verdict

Open Stripchat if you want the same free grid plus VR. Open BongaCams or RoyalCams if the printed $3.99 pack is the reason you are leaving, and open CamSoda if you want the long price list and the toys.

Open MyFreeCams if you want to watch with no account and you accept a $19.99 first pack. Open LiveJasmin, Streamate or Jerkmate if the private minute is the product, and read the rate before the card is saved.

Our note on reading cam site pricing before it reads your wallet is the three ways the money leaves: the token, the tip and the private. SpicyRanked's Chaturbate review is the outside baseline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest Chaturbate alternative?

Stripchat. Public rooms are free, the token pays the model about five cents, and VR rooms are the extra Chaturbate does not have.

Which site has the cheapest tokens?

BongaCams and RoyalCams published the lowest door price we could read, a pack near 46 tokens for $3.99 in September 2026, with a later Dark-Ero note of 47 tokens at the same dollar on BongaCams. CamSoda starts at 50 tokens for $6.99, and MyFreeCams starts at 200 tokens for $19.99.

Can I watch without an account?

Chaturbate, Stripchat, BongaCams, MyFreeCams and Streamate all let you watch a public or free room first. Flirt4Free wants a verified email, SakuraLive wants a login, and Jerkmate asks for a card at signup.

Which sites show the price before you pay?

CamSoda, BongaCams, RoyalCams and MyFreeCams print pack or show prices on public pages. Streamate shows the per-minute rate before you enter, and Stripchat, Chaturbate and several others wait until you are logged in.

Who pays models the most?

SkyPrivate publishes the highest share we found, 70 percent by default and up to 90 percent through a referral bonus, and it requires a legal entity. CamSoda describes about 60 percent of tips and privates, and Stripchat, Chaturbate and MyFreeCams pay a flat $0.05 a token.

Are the free rooms actually free?

The public picture is free on the big grids. The charge is the tip, the private, the spy session, and any auto-refill you leave switched on.
