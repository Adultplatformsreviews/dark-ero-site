What Is Jerkmate?

Jerkmate is a cam finder with female, male, couple and trans performers. The About page says it was created in 2019 and claims more than 35,000 models.

That headcount is the site's own marketing number, and it was not something we could check, so it is not a fact about how many rooms are worth opening tonight.

The safety FAQ splits the product in two. Jerkmate is where you find people.

JerkmateLive is where live shows, private sessions and Gold Shows take place. The terms say the charges and fees follow the terms on that second site, which means the billing rules are not on the page where you created the account.

Gold, the Card and the Missing Rates

Public rooms and public chat are free once you have an account. Private shows give you the model's focus while other members can still watch without chatting.

An exclusive show is just you and the model. Both are charged per minute in Gold, at a rate the public pages do not list.

A Gold Show is a group show. The model sets a Gold goal, a minimum buy-in and a time limit, and the show starts when the goal is met or when the model starts it.

Gold also pays tips, Spin the Wheel and Live Vibe, which is real-time control of a toy. None of those features replaces a price list.

The card at signup is explained as an age check and a way to ready the account. The FAQ says you are not charged unless you buy Gold.

That is a claim to test against your statement, not a reason to save a card you did not plan to use. Premium is described in one FAQ as 30 days of access that can renew, and in another as a site with no subscription and no automatic payment.

Until those two pages agree, open My Account after any purchase and look for a renewal.

Safety, the Token Warning and Privacy

The terms ban anyone under 18, or under the age of majority where that age is higher, and they ban roleplay involving minors. The site says it will close accounts that break those rules.

The home page also links out to Pineapple Support for performers. That is a real resource, and it does not make the viewer's bill any clearer.

Jerkmate warns that it has nothing to do with a crypto token sold under the same name. Treat any coin using the brand as unofficial.

Account deletion asks for a login name, a full name and the last four digits of the card, either in My Account or by email. The privacy policy says account and transaction data can be kept for up to 7 years after the account closes, under Cyprus and EU tax rules.

Marketing data stays until you withdraw consent.

Jerkmate Verdict

Jerkmate is a real cam product on the Streamate network, with a quiz that shortens the search and a Gold unit that is honestly a dollar. The company is named.
