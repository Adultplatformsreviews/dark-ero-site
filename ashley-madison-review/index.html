What Is Ashley Madison?

Ashley Madison is a long-running discreet dating platform. Signing up is free. You can browse, add favorites and send a few free winks and private-key requests each day. Reading or sending messages requires a Full Member, which means a positive credit balance.

The privacy tools are the product people actually come for. Photos can be blurred. Galleries can stay private. A discreet billing name is part of the pitch, although one help article says that name is not published and another names it. When the help centre disagrees with itself, do not promise your bank statement a particular word.

The company names two operating companies and their addresses, has a data protection officer, and dates its terms, privacy policy and help guides. Support is a virtual assistant, email and a phone line. That is a real operator. It is also the operator of a site that lost a historic amount of user data.

Credits You Cannot Price From the Outside

Ashley Madison does not publish prices before you sign up. Credit packages and subscriptions appear on the Buy Credits page inside the account, and payment options vary by country. We are not going to repeat a package price from an old screenshot.

A Collect Message works like a collect call. The person you contact pays 5 credits to read it, plus more to reply. Gifts in the apps add priority inbox placement and a read receipt. There is also a mandatory fee listed in the help centre, separate from the credits themselves. Auto Credit Top Up reloads your balance when it drops to 12 credits or fewer, using your last package. You can switch that off in account settings on the website.

Purchases are generally final and non-refundable unless local law says otherwise. Unused subscription time is not refunded. Interac transfers and money orders cannot be refunded. Some places give a short cooling-off period. The terms list 3 business days for members in states such as California, New York and Illinois, with a prorated refund.

Credits are lost if you delete the account or do not log in for 12 months. A balance is not a savings account. If you are going to disappear for a year, assume the credits disappear with you.

The 2015 Breach Still Counts

In July 2015 attackers broke in and later published data on more than 36 million users. The US Federal Trade Commission said that set included people who had paid $19 for a Full Delete service that was supposed to remove them. In December 2016 the operators settled with the FTC and state regulators for $1.6 million and agreed to a security program with outside assessments.

That settlement is a decade old. It is still the right context for a site that sells discretion. A security program agreed after a disaster is not the same thing as a disaster that never happened. The privacy policy now says Ashley Madison does not sell your personal data. App signups ask for location, kept as rough coordinates, and the site ignores browser do-not-track signals.

Optional selfie and ID badges exist. A badge is a check against a document or a face, not a promise the person is single, nearby or telling the truth. Fake profiles remain a known problem. Any request for gift cards, crypto or a ticket is the exit.

Meet in public if you meet at all. Keep the first payments, if there are any, inside the site's own credit checkout. Ashley Madison cannot unwind a wire you sent because a chat felt urgent.

Ashley Madison FAQ

Is Ashley Madison free?

Signup is free. You can browse, favorite profiles and send a few winks and private-key requests. Reading or sending messages requires credits. A Full Member is someone with a positive balance.

How much do Ashley Madison credits cost?

The site does not publish package prices before you sign up. They appear on the Buy Credits page and vary by country. A Collect Message costs the recipient 5 credits to read, plus more to reply. We do not quote a package total we cannot see.

What is Auto Credit Top Up?

It reloads your last package when the balance drops to 12 credits or fewer. You can turn it off in account settings on the website. Leave it on only if that reload is a number you already accepted.

Will Ashley Madison refund me?

Purchases are generally final unless local law says otherwise. Unused subscription time is not refunded. The terms list a 3-business-day cooling-off period, with a prorated refund, in states such as California, New York and Illinois.

What happened in the Ashley Madison breach?

In 2015 attackers published data on more than 36 million users, including people who had paid $19 for a Full Delete. In 2016 the operators settled with the FTC and states for $1.6 million and agreed to outside security assessments.

Does a verification badge mean the person is real?

A selfie or ID badge means a check was completed. It does not mean the profile is honest, single or safe to pay. Fake profiles remain a known problem. Leave if the chat asks for gift cards, crypto or a wire.
