What Is ABLX?

ABLX pitches itself as an uncensored adult AI that bundles a video studio, an image tool, longer AI movies and character roleplay behind one balance. The current homepage title calls it an uncensored AI video generator with templates, audio and movies.

The domain was registered in mid 2026, and the Terms carry a July 2026 date. In other words, this product has only a few months of public history.

How ABLX Works

The flow is simple on paper. You pick a ready-made scene template, type a description, or animate a character, and the app renders a clip or image into your gallery.

Each render deducts credits from your balance. Short templates run 5 or 7 seconds, while the movie format stretches to roughly half a minute.

ABLX Video Templates

The homepage shows a row of explicit templates priced at about 30 to 42 credits each. This is the fastest way in, since you do not need to write a prompt at all.

ABLX Roleplay Characters

The character builder lets you define looks, voice, personality, backstory and an opening scene. ABLX says characters can send images and clips inside the chat.

We could not find a clear credit price for unlocking a roleplay character. Treat that cost as unknown until you see it in your own account.

ABLX Pricing and Credits

ABLX sells five credit packs, and the credits page says they never expire. Bigger packs lower the price per credit from about 3 cents to about 2 cents.

  • Starter: $2.99 for 100 credits.
  • Limited offer: $9.99 for about 415 credits, shown with a countdown timer.
  • Mid pack: $19.99 for 750 credits.
  • Large pack: $39.99 for 1,800 credits.
  • Largest pack: $79.99 for 4,000 credits.

In practice, a single image lands around 10 to 15 cents, and a 5 second clip somewhere near 60 to 90 cents. The longest movies can cost several dollars each.

There is a catch. Older FAQ and About text still mentions a free tier plus Pro and Ultra subscriptions, while the credits page says there is no subscription at all.

We found no prices for those plans, so we treat credits as the real model. Our Dark Ero guide on free versus premium adult tools explains why mixed pricing signals deserve caution.

Is ABLX Safe?

We cannot call ABLX safe yet. Its written rules ban anything involving minors, but the biggest risk in this category is left wide open.

The Real Photo Problem

Parts of the site encourage you to upload a photo of a real woman and bring her to life. Other parts promise consent-first design, no real photo uploads and no deepfakes.

We found no public description of a consent step, face check, celebrity filter or human review. That gap matters, because animating a real person without consent can be abusive and illegal in many places.

Age Checks

The only gate we saw is a button asking you to confirm you are over 18. That is the weakest form of age assurance available.

ABLX Privacy and Transparency

ABLX says it does not sell personal data, and a first visit did not appear to load third-party ad trackers. Those are genuine positives.

On the other hand, the site names no company, no address and no governing law. Support is email only, and the Terms contain no refund policy.
