What Is DreamGF.ai?

DreamGF.ai is a web companion where you design a character's look and personality, then chat, request photos and use voice. DreamAI SRL, registered in Bucharest under J40/12952/2023, operates it. The company's about page names executives, led by a chief executive. Naming a company, a number and people is more than many rivals do.

Photos are the core. You can ask for images in the chat, spend tokens on extra pictures, and use a separate generator for scenes that are not tied to one profile. The menu also points at a boyfriend version and at other DreamAI properties. We are reviewing the girlfriend site, not those spinoffs.

Registration offers email, Google or Patreon, and the roadmap has mentioned Apple and Discord. An 18+ notice sits in front of the account. That notice is a click, not a check, except where the company says UK features have required age verification since 25 July 2025.

Dark-Ero files generators like this on the AI tools category. The page is the right shelf. This particular product is not the right pick on it.

Three Price Lists and a $25.99 Renewal

On 2 October 2026 the checkout charged $12.99 for the first month and then $25.99 a month if you did not cancel. A 3-month option was $29.97, renewing at $77.97. An annual option was $69.99, renewing at $311.88. Each of those renewals works out to $25.99 a month. The discount covers the first payment. The membership policy also says vouchers apply only to the first period.

The pricing page listed the same first payments and swapped the labels, calling the $12.99 option a 3-month membership and the $29.97 option monthly. The pricing page and the membership policy promised 100 tokens a month. Checkout promised 150. A homepage FAQ still listed Bronze at $9.99, Silver at $19.99, Gold at $49.99 and Diamond at $99.99. An older review's Premium and Ultimate names were not on the checkout. Trust the number on the payment screen, and screenshot it.

The checkout also showed a limited-time countdown. It read about three and a half hours each time the page was loaded that day, so we would not treat the deadline as real. Token packs, sold to Premium members, ran from $2.99 to $179.99. Creating a companion costs 20 tokens, an image costs 10, a voice message costs 3, and a voice call costs 7 tokens a minute.

The membership policy lets the company charge former paying members $1.99 a month as a maintenance fee to keep the account active. The exit it describes is deleting the account. You can cancel a plan and keep access until the period ends. If you accept an offer of instant tokens plus a monthly grant, the policy says you cannot cancel for 30 days. Refunds are not offered unless the law requires them, and they are ruled out once you have created a girlfriend, generated a photo, sent a message or used credits.

Checkout offers card or crypto and says no adult transaction appears on the statement. Segpay subscribers manage billing through Segpay. Rocketgate subscribers are pointed at a support address. We could not find PayPal on the public pages that day.

The Builder, Voice and the Unfinished Video Page

The builder starts with looks, then personality, and you can write a short intro the companion is supposed to remember. In an earlier hands-on test, changes showed up in the images with reasonable consistency, faces looked realistic, and hands and accessories sometimes failed. That test is months old. We did not rebuild a character for this update, so treat the image praise as dated.

Voice is no longer missing. Messages are 3 tokens and calls are 7 tokens a minute, with calls dated to August 2024 on the company's roadmap. A video page linked from the footer showed placeholder text and untitled tiles on 2 October 2026. We would not pay for video on the strength of that page.

Chat in the earlier test was fine for light flirting and thin on anything deeper. The roadmap lists a newer chat model from January 2024. We have not retested the conversation since that older session, so the chat note stays an old observation, not a fresh score.

The free trial needs no card, according to the site. A daily claim starts at 10 messages and rises by one a day toward 50, and a missed day resets the streak. That is a real way to look at the builder. It is not a reason to ignore the category labels below.

Why the Age Labels Rule It Out

The written rules ban content that resembles minors. The category directory does not behave as if that sentence is the priority. On 2 October 2026 an age section of the directory linked a live category labelled Teen (18+), and a clothing option labelled School opened a category titled around schoolgirls. We did not open the images. The labels alone are the problem.

An 18+ sticker on a category name does not fix a youth-coded pitch on an explicit image site. It conflicts with the terms' promise that content will not resemble minors. Outside the UK, entry is a banner and a checkbox. We would not call that a safeguard.

The blocked-content policy also bans impersonation of real people, hate, violence and self-harm, and there is a complaints form for lookalikes. Moderation is described as automated flags, human review and manual approval before a character goes public. We could not test how well any of that works. A policy that the site's own menu contradicts is not a policy you can trust.

GirlfriendGPT has a related failure, a homepage that features school-aged framing while the rules ban it. Our GirlfriendGPT review is the companion piece, and it is another product we do not recommend.

Privacy, the Licence and Billing

The privacy policy follows GDPR and names DreamAI SRL as the controller. It is more careful than the homepage, which talks about strictly private chats and advanced encryption. Staff can review chats if you report a problem, according to the policy. Details about your sex life are processed on the basis of consent if you share them. Card data sits with the payment companies. Hosting is described as providers such as DigitalOcean, kept as long as needed, with no fixed period.

We found no clear statement, either way, about training models on your chats. The terms do give DreamAI a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use and modify content you upload or generate. EU, UK and Swiss users get access, deletion and portability rights, with replies promised within 30 days. Optional advertising cookies sit beside the necessary ones.

Read AI adult tools and your data, before you upload before you type a real name into a builder. A lookalike of a real person is already on the site's own blocked list, which is another reason not to upload a face.

The practical steps for leaving are in the guide to how to delete AI companion data. On this product, deletion is also the way the membership policy says you escape the $1.99 maintenance charge.

The terms are governed by Romanian law. The privacy policy says it is governed by the laws of the USA. That clash is another sign the paperwork is uneven. Support email, a Discord and a help center for subscribers exist. In the older test, email took a day or two. Some public pages look neglected, including a complaints footer still reading 2024.

DreamGF Pros and Cons

What Works

  • A detailed appearance builder, which was the strongest part of an earlier test.
  • Photos on request, plus voice messages and voice calls with printed token prices.
  • A free trial that does not ask for a card.
  • A named EU company, a registration number and named executives.
  • Written moderation rules, on paper.

What Does Not

  • Live age-coded categories that conflict with the site's own rule on minors.
  • Three different price lists, and a renewal at $25.99 a month.
  • A possible $1.99 monthly charge after you stop paying.
  • No refunds once you have used the service.
  • A click-through age gate outside the UK.
  • No official app, and unrelated store apps using the name.

DreamGF Scorecard

The builder is still the best thing about the product, and the company is identifiable, which keeps this from being a nameless scam warning. Voice has a printed token price. The free trial does not demand a card.

None of that survives the directory. Youth-coded category labels on an explicit generator conflict with the site's own ban on content that resembles minors, and the age gate outside the UK is a click. Pricing is a second failure: three pages, three stories, a renewal at the undiscounted monthly rate, no refund once you touch the tools, and a maintenance fee aimed at people who already cancelled.

Put together, that is 4.5 out of 10. We do not recommend DreamGF.ai. If the youth-coded categories come down and the price pages start agreeing, the builder would be worth another look. They have not.

DreamGF Verdict

DreamGF.ai can make a custom companion image, and an earlier test liked the builder more than the chat. That is not enough. The site's own menu has marketed teen and schoolgirl labels, the renewal is $25.99 a month after a cheap first charge, and former members can be billed $1.99 to keep an account they thought they had left.

Our score is 4.5 out of 10. Do not pay. Use a companion whose categories match its rules, and whose checkout matches its pricing page.
