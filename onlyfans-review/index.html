What Is OnlyFans?

OnlyFans lets creators run a paid page. Fans subscribe for a set period to unlock the page, and creators can also sell single posts, paid messages and live streams and accept tips.

The operator is Fenix International Limited, company number 10354575, with an office on Cheapside in London. It was incorporated in September 2016.

Ownership changed in 2026. Companies House shows Leonid Radvinsky ceased to be the controlling owner on 20 March 2026, and three new directors joined in May.

Group accounts for the year to November 2025 were filed in September 2026.

What OnlyFans Costs Fans

Joining is free. Each creator sets prices within limits OnlyFans publishes:

  • Monthly subscription: free, or $4.99 to $50.
  • Bundles: up to $250 for two or more months.
  • Paid messages: $3 to $100.
  • Tips: $3 to $100, rising to $200 after four months.
  • New fan spending cap: up to $500 a day, raised later for accounts in good standing.

The subscription is often only the entry fee. A $9.99 page with two $15 paid messages becomes $39.99 a month before tax.

Prices are in US dollars, so your bank may add a conversion fee.

Refunds and Auto-Renew

Subscriptions, tips, streams and paid content are non-refundable unless the Terms were breached. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you switch off auto-renew, and discounted offers renew at full price, so turn renewal off right away if you only wanted the deal.

What OnlyFans Pays Creators

OnlyFans keeps 20% of every fan payment and creators receive 80%, covering subscriptions, paid messages and tips. Refunds and chargebacks are clawed back from the creator's share.

Taxes are your job. US creators who earn and withdraw over $600 a year get an IRS Form 1099, and UK or EU VAT-registered creators follow the matching VAT policy.

The referral program pays 5% of a referred creator's earnings for 12 months, up to $50,000 per creator.

Payout Timing

Earnings stay pending for 7 days, or 21 days in a few countries with high reversal rates until four months after first earnings. Most methods have a $20 minimum, and you can withdraw manually or on an automatic monthly, weekly or daily schedule.

Funds usually land in 3 to 5 business days.

Creator Verification and Content Protection

Before posting, creators submit at least nine items, including full name, address, date of birth, government photo ID, a selfie, a selfie with the ID, bank details and tax information. Repeat selfie checks follow on a rolling 45-day cycle.

AI content is allowed only if it clearly features the verified creator and is labelled, for example with #ai. There is no public API, and plugging AI chatbots into the platform is not allowed.

Creators get free watermarking, DRM protection on new videos and a team that files DMCA takedowns. OnlyFans admits some sites ignore those notices, so leaks remain a real risk.

Can OnlyFans Help You Find Fans?

Barely. Fans can only search by exact username, and the search icon appears after they have subscribed to at least one account.

OnlyFans does not accept requests or payment for promotion.

That makes OnlyFans a checkout, not a shop window. Creators who succeed usually bring followers from X, Reddit, TikTok or Instagram.

Is OnlyFans Safe?

For payments, yes. Cards run through approved processors, every user must be 18+, and creators pass ID checks before earning.

The bigger risks come from people.

Never pay a creator through another app, gift cards or crypto; OnlyFans bans off-platform payments and cannot recover that money. On busy pages, the person replying to your messages may be a paid assistant, which fans accept in the Fan and Creator contract.

Chargebacks are tracked, and fans who file them in bad faith can lose access.

Privacy and Billing

Statements show OnlyFans or OF, with no discreet descriptor. OnlyFans advises fans not to share their full name, address or contact details with creators.

Some regions now require third-party age checks before viewing explicit content.

Regulation

In March 2025 Ofcom fined Fenix 1.05 million pounds for giving inaccurate information about its age checks. An independent safety monitor was in place from August 2021 to April 2025.

Tips for Fans Before You Subscribe

Most OnlyFans complaints come from fans who paid more than they expected. A few habits avoid that.

  • Turn off auto-renew immediately on any discounted or trial subscription, then switch it back on only if you want to stay.
  • Read the page description. Many creators say up front whether most content arrives through paid messages.
  • Set your own monthly budget. Paid messages and tips are where spending grows.
  • Keep personal details private. Never send your full name, address or other contact information.
  • Stay on platform. Any request to pay by gift card, crypto or another app is a red flag.

Tips for New Creators

Build an audience before you launch, because OnlyFans will not do it for you. Pick one or two social platforms, post consistently and point every profile to a single link.

Keep a clean record of everyone who appears in your content and their consent, enable two-factor login, and withdraw earnings regularly. If you use a management agency, read the contract carefully; you remain responsible for what is posted on your account.

Feet creators who prefer anonymity should plan framing and backgrounds carefully, since repeat selfie checks only go to OnlyFans and not to fans, but leaked content can travel far beyond the platform.

OnlyFans Verdict

OnlyFans is legitimate, regulated and fair on its creator split, with payout rules that are easy to follow. Its brand alone makes it worth having for creators with an existing audience.

It falls short on refunds for fans and discovery for creators. Fans should vet a creator before paying and disable auto-renew; creators should plan their traffic before they plan their price.
