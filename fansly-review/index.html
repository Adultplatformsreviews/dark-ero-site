What Is Fansly?

Fansly is a fan subscription website for adult and non-adult creators. Fans follow for free, subscribe to paid tiers, buy single posts and messages and send tips.

It is owned by Select Media LLC, a Maryland company based on West Ostend Street in Baltimore, with some payments handled by a partner in Lithuania. Every visitor confirms they are 18 or over before entering.

Fans can sign up by email or with X, Google or Twitch. Creators apply separately and are approved at Fansly's discretion. It is often compared directly with OnlyFans, and the overlap is large.

How Fansly Works

Each creator runs one profile that mixes free posts for followers, subscriber-only posts and pay-per-view content. You do not need separate free and paid accounts.

Followers and Tiers

Followers pay nothing but see follower posts, receive promotion alerts and can be included in mass messages. That makes them a warm pool you can convert into subscribers.

Creators can build several tiers with different perks and optional caps. Prices range from $5.00 to $499.99 a month, with 3 or 6 month discounts, sales, private offers and free trials.

Private tags help Fansly surface your work to interested users, so there is some internal discovery, which is more than OnlyFans offers.

Creator Verification

Creators must be at least 18 and an adult under local law. The process includes a third-party ID check, photos of the ID, a selfie holding the ID and a handwritten sign, and a short video. Anyone else featured needs age, identity and consent verified before posting. Tax forms come before your first withdrawal.

Fansly Fees and Payouts

Joining is free for fans and creators. Fansly keeps 20% and pays creators 80% of subscriptions, media, messages and tips, and it says there are no hidden payout, processing or currency fees.

Earnings tied to chargebacks or rule-breaking content can still be deducted.

  • Paxum: $20 minimum, usually within 24 hours.
  • Cosmopayment: $20 minimum, usually within 24 hours.
  • Local bank deposit (parts of South America and Asia, USD ACH, CAD): $20, 1 to 3 business days.
  • SEPA (EU and UK): $50, or from $20 with automatic sending after 14 days.
  • SWIFT wire: $100, batched every 3 days.
  • Crypto: $100, batched every 3 days.

Each sale waits 7 days before it can be withdrawn, and most requests are approved within about 48 hours. Deposits typically show as SELECT MEDIA LLC. Set up a backup method in case a payout partner has downtime.

Is Fansly Safe and Legit?

Yes. Fansly is run by a named US company with dated terms and detailed payout pages. The main risks are the usual ones for fan sites: purchases are final and dispute rules are strict.

Refunds and Chargebacks

Purchases and wallet funds are generally non-refundable, with occasional exceptions at Fansly's discretion. Cancelling stops renewal but does not refund the current period. Billing errors must be reported in writing within 30 days.

A fan chargeback can lead to account suspension, so contact support first. Fansly says it shields creators from most chargebacks, but unusual volumes can be clawed back, and blocking an active subscriber can trigger a refund from creator earnings.

Privacy and Data

Fansly says it does not sell personal data and does not store card numbers. However, its Privacy Policy dates from February 2022, which is old for a site that handles ID documents, and it allows biometric data such as face geometry to be collected from verification material.

We could not confirm the fan billing descriptor on Fansly's own pages. If discretion matters, check your statement after a small first purchase.

Fine Print to Know

The Terms require individual arbitration with a class action waiver unless you opt out in writing within 30 days of first use. Accounts with no login for 12 months lose $5 a month from any balance. There is no Fansly app on the App Store or Google Play, so mobile use is through the browser.

Getting Started on Fansly the Smart Way

Fansly approves creators manually, so a complete, honest application speeds things up. Use good lighting for the ID video, match the name on your payout account and have your tax form details ready before you apply.

  • Plan your tiers first. A low entry tier, a mid tier with extra sets and a premium tier for customs is a simple, proven layout.
  • Grow followers, not just subscribers. Free followers can be messaged and alerted to sales, which makes them your cheapest marketing list.
  • Add a backup payout. Paxum, Cosmopayment or crypto protects you if your main partner pauses.
  • Log in at least once a year. That avoids the $5 monthly inactive-balance deduction.
  • Decide on arbitration early. If you want to keep the right to join a class action, the opt-out window is only 30 days.

Feet creators should keep their avatar and banner tasteful, because graphic nudity is not allowed in those spaces. Watermark previews and keep identifying details such as tattoos, jewellery or home décor out of frame if anonymity matters to you.
