What Janitor is doing here

Janitor's subscription FAQ still describes the free tier as unlimited chats on standard models, with a context limit of around 9,000 tokens. A separate token guide puts that free window at roughly 8,000 to 9,000.

Older messages drop out once the window fills. The bot is not choosing to forget, because it cannot see the text.

That window is the comparison. A nicer site is still a worse Janitor swap if the free chat forgets the scene twice as fast.

Janitor+ is Janitor's own paid upgrade for more context and faster replies. This page is for when you want a different product, not a receipt from the same one.

1 SpicyChat AI

SpicyChat is the closest format. You browse a community library, open a bot, and adult chat is part of the product.

On 4 November 2026 the subscribe page listed Get a Taste at $5.95 a month, True Supporter at $19.95 and I'm All In at $29.95. SpicyChat's pricing FAQ says those monthly prices took effect on 8 September 2026, and annual Get a Taste was $60.

Free accounts and the $5.95 plan both stop at 4,096 tokens of context, with replies up to 180 tokens. That is about half of Janitor's free window.

The $5.95 plan removes ads, skips the waiting line and opens a memory manager. It does not buy a longer memory.

True Supporter moves the window to 8,192 tokens, lengthens replies to 300 tokens, and adds conversation images. I'm All In goes up to 32,768 tokens on models that support it, and adds text-to-speech.

If you stayed on Janitor because the free chat could see about 9,000 tokens, $5.95 does not replace that. It replaces the ads and the queue.

2 CrushOn AI

CrushOn is another character library. Free models do not spend credits, and stronger models do.

Voice messages are in the product. CrushOn's own voice help says they spend message credits, and the cost depends on the voice.

Homepage copy we read on 4 November 2026 still starts paid plans at Standard, $5.99 a month for 2,000 credits. The free plan is unlimited chat on the free models, plus 100 credits a month for Pro models.

Paid plans are what open the larger memory models. Adult scenes sit behind an unfiltered setting and an age check, not behind a blanket refusal.

The credit counter is the part to watch. Once the 100 Pro credits are gone, chat continues on the free model, so read the checkout before you treat $5.99 as unlimited everything.

3 Kalon AI

Kalon is a catalog companion: you pick or build a character, then chat and generate pictures of that character. Paid plans are where video and audio have lived, and it is less of a public bot dump than Janitor.

On 4 November 2026 the homepage banner said chat, image and video generation are down until further notice. The team is working on a version 2.

Free and Yearly cards were still on the page. We could not re-read the prices from that page in this pass.

Early October write-ups of those cards recorded 50 coins at signup plus 50 a day. Monthly was $19.97 with 16,000 coins, and yearly was $119.64.

Treat the dollars as October figures. Do not pay while the outage notice is up.

4 Kindroid

Kindroid is not a library. You build one companion, called a Kin, and the product is memory, calls and selfies rather than the next public bot.

The free Lite tier ended on 1 October 2026. Kindroid's help center says new accounts get a one-time preview of about 50 flagship messages, then the account goes read-only.

Our Kindroid review has the longer version: Standard at $13.99 a month on the web as of late October 2026, higher prices in the apps, and Ultra and MAX add-ons that stack. SpicyRanked's Kindroid review walks through the same change.

An independent Kindroid AI review breaks out the cost. Dark Ero's note on free versus premium is the plain version of that trade.

The preview is a sample, not a plan. Kindroid is the one with native apps, on the web, iPhone and Android, while the other names here are browser products as far as their public sites showed on this pass.

5 DreamGen

DreamGen is a story and roleplay writer with an image generator, not a bot directory. If your Janitor chats were really long scenes, this is the closer tool, and if you wanted to click the next character, it is not.

On 4 November 2026 the pricing page listed Starter at $7.83 a month, Advanced at $19.35 and Pro at $48.30. Each had a lower first-month price beside it: $6.26, $15.48 and $33.81.

Advanced shows a 15,000-token context window, 2,250 monthly credits and 75 extra daily credits. Starter's window, on DreamGen's story page, is 5,000 tokens.

Credits pay for images and for models that are not included as unlimited on your plan.

DreamGen's privacy policy, as quoted in October 2026, says private stories are not used to train models unless you opt in. The switch starts off.

Confirm the toggle in the account before you paste a long scene. Story-first readers can scan this independent list of best AI sex story generators.

6 Chub AI

Chub is a character-card library with a chat front end. The cards are the point, and hosted models are the paid part.

The Mars page on 4 November 2026 still says the subscriber tier is $20 a month for one person. That covers unlimited use of Chub's largest models inside Chub, or as an OpenAI-style API in another frontend.

New users get a one-time trial of a few dozen messages. Without the subscription, the usual free route is your own API key or a local model, which is a poor swap if you came to Janitor because the model was already there.

The docs still call the hosted line Mars. The page we opened did not show a cheaper tier.

7 Candy AI

Candy is a photo companion. You pick or build a character, and the product pushes images, which is a weak match for someone who lives in a Janitor library.

Candy's help page, which dates its dollar figures 26 October 2026, says free access is up to five chat messages in total. That includes one custom character and one generated image.

Premium was $9.99 for the first month, then $13.99. Quarterly was $26.97, and yearly was $47.88.

Paid text is described as unlimited. Pictures and video spend tokens.

SpicyRanked's Candy AI review goes further into the token math. An independent Candy AI review does the same from the cost side.

Five messages is enough to see the screen. It is not enough to judge memory.

CHAI and PolyBuzz

CHAI and PolyBuzz show up in the same searches. They do not belong on an adult-roleplay list.

CHAI's Safety Center says its models are designed to refuse sexually explicit content. The same line covers self-harm content and anything that sexualises minors.

The app contract is a bit looser about fictional adult text. Explicit images are banned outright.

A product built to refuse the scene is not a Janitor replacement. The minor rule is the correct one, and the explicit-adult refusal is why it fails this page.

PolyBuzz's terms tell users not to depict pornography or sexual acts. Its FAQ says public NSFW is prohibited.

Characters flagged for pornography can be hidden from everyone but the creator. A library that buries sexual characters is a different product.

Which one fits

Stay near SpicyChat if you want other people's bots and you can live with a 4,096-token free window. Use CrushOn if you want free models plus voice and you will watch the credit counter.

Skip Kalon until the homepage outage notice is gone. The Kindroid review is the page if you want one companion, calls and a phone app, and you have already accepted there is no free plan.

Use DreamGen for fiction, and Chub if you like character cards and will bring a model or pay $20. Use Candy only if the pictures are the point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest Janitor AI alternative?

SpicyChat AI is still a public bot library with adult chat allowed. Free context is 4,096 tokens, against about 9,000 on Janitor's free model, and the first paid plan is $5.95 a month.

Is there a free Janitor AI alternative?

SpicyChat and CrushOn still let you chat without paying. Kindroid's free Lite plan ended on 1 October 2026, and Candy's published free allowance is up to five messages.

Why is Kalon listed if chat is down?

The product shape, a catalog companion with images, still belongs on a Janitor comparison. On 4 November 2026 the homepage said generation was down, so do not pay until that notice is gone.

Does Kindroid replace a Janitor bot library?

No. You build one Kin, and it is the wrong swap if you want to open someone else's character and leave.

Why are CHAI and PolyBuzz not on the list?

CHAI's models are designed to refuse sexually explicit content. PolyBuzz's terms prohibit depicting pornography or sexual acts.

Should I buy Janitor+ instead of switching?

Only if the library is what you want and the missing piece is memory or queue speed. Switch when you want voice and credits, one long-term companion, a story tool, or pictures.
